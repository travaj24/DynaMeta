# DynaMeta v0.16.0 -- release note (2026-11-15)

Branch `wip/v016_stage`, on top of v0.15.1 (origin/main b8a73c0, release notes
`2026-11-06-v0.15.0-release.md` and `2026-11-08-v0.15.1-release.md`). Everything below is a change **relative to
v0.15.1**. The release implements the gated thin-film device-path audit and its plan
(`2026-11-14-gated-thin-film-device-path-audit.md`, `2026-11-14-gated-thin-film-device-path-plan.md`: package table
in plan section 1.2, decisions in plan section 9) and the owner-requested additions that followed it. It was built
as 23 packages (P1-P23); each wrote a per-topic note (list in section 12; all under `docs/audit/`, names
`2026-11-14-v0.16-<topic>.md`).

What is in it:

- optics bridges: the RCWA bridge picks Li's rule where it converges and rasters by area on lumenairy's node
  lattice; the PMM bridge accepts laterally structured and laterally uniform tensor gridded layers and exposes
  its accuracy knobs; every bridge checks a gridded field's z extent against its layer (section 3.1);
- carriers: the layered-optics path receives the carrier solve's native z grid, the DEVSIM 2-D mesh is graded to
  the screening length, an equilibrium film's edge electrode is its Fermi-level reference, fixed / bulk charges,
  gate work functions and film interface traps, a Kane band in the equilibrium electrostatics, an in-Newton
  density gradient for equilibrium films, and gate leakage with a lateral Fermi-level field (section 3.2);
- Schrodinger-Poisson: a corrected single-gate carrier (Fermi level, gate map, warm start), and a new dual-gate
  solver with optional finite oxide barriers, exchange-correlation and interface traps (section 3.3);
- quantum and nonlocal optics: the intersubband eps_zz without the double-counted Drude term, a self-consistent
  (RPA / adiabatic LDA) quantum-film response wired into the pipeline, hydrodynamic runs with graded density,
  a 2-D periodic TM FEM with an optional nonlocal conductor layer, thin-film damping and a density-dependent
  background permittivity (section 3.4);
- measurement averaging: cone, fabrication scatter, spectrometer and reference normalization over any optical
  solver, plus a broadening fit (section 3.5);
- materials: ready-made epsilon-near-zero (ENZ) material presets -- doped oxides, metallic nitrides, doped
  mid-IR III-V and phonon-polariton crystals -- with the lattice / oscillator permittivity models and the oxide
  scattering laws behind them (section 3.7).

**Read sections 0, 1 and 2 before upgrading.** Several defaults change; every changed default has a keyword that
restores the v0.15.1 behaviour, except where v0.15.1 was wrong (stated in the table). Every number below is
quoted from the per-topic note or the test docstring named next to it, as they stand in this tree; the test
fixtures of every v0.16 test, validation and note are a neutral generic gated film and metal-strip cell
(`tests/_gated_film_fixture.py`, `tests/_wiring_fixture.py`, `tests/_strip_cell_fixture.py`). "Measured" in the
notes means run on the dev box; the notes label what is inferred.

## Terms used here

- **TM (grating sense)**: electric field in the plane of incidence and across the grating lines; it crosses
  metal side walls.
- **Laurent / Li rule**: the two ways RCWA builds the Fourier matrix of the permittivity. Laurent's (direct) rule
  is right for a field component continuous across a wall; Li's inverse rule converges for the normal component
  (TM on metal walls).
- **Hard layer** (RCWA `formulation='auto'`): a patterned isotropic layer holding Re(eps) < 0 anywhere or a
  contrast max|eps|/min|eps| > 10.
- **Area raster**: pixel j of an S-pixel axis is the cell [x_j - P/2S, x_j + P/2S] around lumenairy's node
  x_j = j P/S and holds the area average of the painted permittivity; the point raster samples cell centres.
- **Inverse-rule companion**: the harmonic-mean pair (exx, eyy) that lumenairy's `add_layer(eps_cell_normal=)`
  takes next to an 'li' raster.
- **PMM**: lumenairy's polynomial modal method (spectral elements per lateral region; `degree` per element,
  `elements_per_region`).
- **Native carrier grid**: the carrier solve's own z mesh lines, returned instead of a uniform 32-point resample.
- **Screening length L_s**: L_s^2 = eps_s eps0 / (q^2 dn/dE_F) at the background density; the Debye length when
  non-degenerate, the Thomas-Fermi length when degenerate.
- **Equilibrium physics**: the Potential-only DEVSIM model (one Fermi level per region, Fermi-Dirac closure).
- **Fermi-level reference**: an edge electrode that sets an equilibrium region's Fermi level (E_F = -qV)
  instead of pinning the potential of its node column.
- **SP**: Schrodinger-Poisson. **DG**: density gradient. **L_q**: the DG quantum length.
- **Face-field gate map**: Gauss's law at the oxide face, eps_ox (Vg - psi_s)/t_ox = D_face, with the solver's
  conservative half-cell face flux.
- **f-sum ratio**: sum over sub-band pairs of (n_i - n_j) f_ij divided by the total sheet density; 1 for a
  complete sub-band set.
- **RPA / LDA**: random-phase approximation (Hartree response) / local-density exchange-correlation.
- **Equivalent layer**: the first-order uniaxial layer (eps_xx,eq, eps_zz,eq) that carries a quantum film's
  sheet responses into a local optical solver.
- **D_it**: interface-trap density per area and energy.
- **Gate action**: mean (V_g - phi_F)/V_g over a film whose Fermi potential phi_F varies laterally (1 = nominal).
- **u** (hydrodynamic validity): |k_L| v_F / omega; the high-frequency hydrodynamic model is meant for u < 0.5.

## 0. Before you upgrade: what most users will notice

0. **The RCWA bridge's default moves on TM metal / high-contrast 1-D cells** (`formulation='auto'` picks Li's
   rule) and every raster is area-weighted (`raster_sampling='area'`). On the generic strip cell the default's
   error against the 2-D TM FEM falls from 4.96e-1 to 7.04e-2 at n_orders 50 (section 1). TE, uniform and
   low-contrast stacks, and every 2-D stack with analytic shapes, are bit-identical to v0.15.1.
1. **`run_pipeline` with a `LayeredDevsimBuilder` and a layered optical solver uses the native carrier z grid**,
   and **the default DEVSIM 2-D mesh grades every unipolar semiconductor face to its screening length**. Both
   move results and the mesh change costs time: the shared validation cell went from 14.1 s to 110 s (7.8x;
   film 19 -> 208 z nodes) in `validation/gated_dd_builder.py` (measured on this tree; method in `carrier-grid-mesh.md`).
2. **An edge electrode on an equilibrium film is now its Fermi-level reference** (no pinned column). Results of
   every equilibrium device with an edge electrode move near that edge; two edge electrodes at different
   voltages on one film raise (`carrier-wiring.md`).
3. **`SchrodingerPoissonCarrier` changes**: depletion biases were wrong in v0.15.1 (psi_s = -13.19 V at -2 V on
   the generic film; a sign error, fixed unconditionally); the default gate map is now Gauss's law at the oxide
   face and the Fermi level is the sub-band model's own finite-temperature level (`sp-core.md`).
4. **`IntersubbandEffect` no longer adds a Drude z term of the sub-band electrons** (-1.0 to -1.3 in Re eps_zz on
   the generic slabs) and refuses an incomplete sub-band set (f-sum ratio < 0.5) (`intersubband-permittivity.md`,
   `review-fixes.md`).
5. **New default-path warnings** can reach code that runs with `filterwarnings = error`: the DOS-mass advisory
   for a density-dependent `dos_mass_kg_of_n_m3` in both DEVSIM builders, the thin-film guard of
   `dg_correct_density_1d`, the intersubband f-sum advisory, and others (section 2).
6. **Two adjacent unlabelled `HydroLayer`s raise** in `nonlocal_tmm`; `stack_rt(allow_walls=True)` restores the
   v0.15.1 physics bit for bit (`hydro-layer-runs.md`).
7. **The optical result cache schema is 8**: stores written by v0.15.1 (schema 7) are discarded on load (one
   INFO log record from the `dynameta.cache` logger says so).
8. **The 2-D TM FEM (new in v0.16) grades high-contrast dielectric corners by default** and decides substrate
   opacity from the slowest-decaying diffraction order (section 6b).
9. **The differentiable RCWA stack (`rcwa_stack_RT` / `rcwa_stack_jones`) defaults to `formulation='auto'` on
   lumenairy releases where Li's rule traces under JAX** (5.51.0), and stays on 'laurent' below (section 1).
10. **`SchrodingerPoissonCarrier` and `Stacked3DSpec` have no material-valued defaults any more**: the film's
   background density, static permittivities and mass (and, with an oxide, its permittivity) must be passed;
   code that relied on the old defaults now raises TypeError / ValueError at construction (section 1).
11. Everything else is opt-in and leaves existing results unchanged (each package proved this with a same-tree
   A/B against the v0.15.1 construction, call-log digests or both; bitwise gates were confirmed on the dev
   workstation, see section 8).

### 3.7 Materials: ENZ material presets

Factories that return a complete `Material` for an ENZ layer, every parameter a literature value with its
source and spread (guide: `docs/enz_presets.md`). Densities are in m^-3; a factory refuses a background density
outside the range of the films its parameters come from (`strict=False` extrapolates with a warning).

- **Doped oxides** (`materials.tco_presets`): `azo`, `gzo`, `in_cdo`, `ito`, `fto`. One Kane band per preset
  feeds the optical mass and the equilibrium electrostatics; the damping comes from a near-infrared optical
  mobility; the DC mobility from an intra-grain law, optionally in series with a grain-boundary barrier. In:CdO
  carries a density-dependent background (band filling of its direct gap). Published ENZ points are reproduced
  within the literature spread: GZO 1583 / 1676 / 1690 nm against 1560 / 1700 / 1710 nm, In:CdO 3.91 / 2.89 /
  1.91 um against 3.87 / 2.72 / 1.92 um, ITO optical mass 0.337 / 0.452 m_e against 0.33 / 0.44 m_e.
  `dual_gate_film_kwargs` hands a preset to `DualGateSchrodingerPoisson`; `wurtzite_polarization_charges` gives
  the bound charges of a c-axis ZnO film for the interface-charge inputs (not applied by default).
- **Other ENZ materials** (`materials.enz_presets`): `tin`, `zrn` (Drude-Lorentz film fits, visible ENZ,
  not gate-tunable), `inas`, `inassb` (two-band Kane band, mid-IR, with the InAs lattice and an interband-onset
  guard), `sic`, `aln`, `sio2` (lattice ENZ near 8-11 um; SiC and AlN uniaxial).
- **Models** (`materials.lattice_optical`, `materials.oxide_scattering`, `materials.uniaxial_optical`): the
  factorized TO/LO lattice permittivity with a passivity check and the Lyddane-Sachs-Teller static value, a

  part, plasmon-LO-phonon coupling through `with_free_carriers`; degenerate nonparabolic ionized-impurity
  scattering, Masetti and Seto mobility laws, optical vs DC mobility of polycrystalline films; a uniaxial
  tensor built from two scalar models. `lorentz_susceptibility` is the one Lorentz kernel:
  `optics.resonance.lorentzian_eps` is unchanged bit for bit, `optics.fdtd_seam.fit_drude_lorentz` to

## 4. New public API

Signatures are the library source's. Every name below is opt-in; nothing changes until it is used.

**`KaneBand` and `carriers.kane_band`.** `materials.transport_model.KaneBand(m0_kg, alpha_per_eV)` (frozen; also
`dynameta.materials.KaneBand` and `dynameta.KaneBand`) is one object for a Kane band E (1 + alpha E) =

equivalent parabolic counting mass), `optical_mass()` (= `KaneOpticalMass.from_band(band)`). Put it on
`TransportModel(..., band=band)` (equilibrium physics only; `dos_mass_kg_of_n_m3` must then be omitted) and the
2-D builder, `Stacked3DSpec(bands={region: band})` / `from_design`, and
`physics_equilibrium.setup_semiconductor_region(..., band=band)` solve the finite-temperature Kane density.

  1/Im kz of the full complex kz. With n_sub = 1.45 + 1e-6j the buffer is within 6.8e-8 of the lossless one, where

## 6b. Fixes from the independent pre-release review

Each fix has a regression test shown to fail on the code before it (or on a deliberate mutant where the code
before already carried the fix); the per-topic notes carry the numbers.

- **`fem_2d_tm` substrate opacity** (`fem-2d-tm.md`): the substrate was called opaque from order 0 alone, so in
  grating-coupled total internal reflection a diffracted order propagating into the substrate was dropped (T
  None, its power reported as absorption, buffer and PML sized on the wrong order; 60 nm grating: abs(dr) 9.9e-5
  and 4.4e-3 of false absorption, below the energy warning). Opacity is now decided from the slowest-decaying
  order; against PMM the same cell agrees to 9.5e-9 in r with T = 0.0044062. New gates against PMM for
  grating-coupled TIR (lossless and weakly lossy substrate) and a pure-TIR case that stays opaque.
- **`fem_2d_tm` dielectric corners**: high-contrast dielectric corners are graded by default (section 1); the
  docstring's "order 4 is converged" now states the measured accuracies.
- **Gain media through the bridges** (`pmm-bridge.md`): feature probe `pmm_gain_media`, PMM refusal where it fails,
  gain half-spaces refused everywhere (section 2); a gain-strip bridge test against the 2-D TM FEM (PMM degree 16:
  5.5e-7 from the FEM on 5.51.0).
- **Differentiable RCWA default** (section 1) and the `_common.py` record of what 5.51.0 changes (small-element
  guard thresholds and warning band; verified span 5.22-5.51).
- **`MeasurementSetup` reference amplitude** (section 2; `measurement-averaging.md`).
- **One p-reflection sign** (`quantum-film-wiring.md`): `film_stack_rt` and `QuantumFilmResponse.stack_rt` (new
  in v0.16) return r_p with the sign of `fem_2d_tm`, the bridges, `tmm_reference` and `solve_film_cell` (r_H =
  -E_x ratio; t stays the tangential-E ratio). `nonlocal_tmm.stack_rt`, which exists in v0.15.1, keeps its
  tangential-E sign (the negative), stated at the top of its docstring. A test pins the relation across routes.
- **`IntersubbandEffect` normalisation length** (`intersubband-permittivity.md`): with `thickness_m=None` the
  'subbands' mode normalises by the wall-to-wall thickness (z[-1] - z[0] plus h at each end node where the states
  do not vanish), not by z[-1] - z[0]; eps_zz now converges at second order in the grid (successive-difference
  ratios 3.93 / 3.98 / 4.00, the old length 1.64 / 1.83 / 1.92). The legacy mode is unchanged bit for bit.
- **`DualGateSchrodingerPoisson`** (`sp-barrier-xc-traps.md`): xc refused for g_s != 2 or g_v != 1;
  `eps_static` validated under its own name; the oxide-side xc convention documented: in the penetration region
  the model carries height_eV - V_xc(n_bg)/q (+114 meV at 3e26 m^-3), correct when height_eV is the band offset
  without the film's electron gas (tabulated or Anderson-rule offsets); a barrier measured from the degenerate
  film's own band edge must be passed as H_measured + V_xc(n_bg)/q. Tested in both directions.
- **Gate-leakage docstring** (`gate-leakage-lateral-drop.md`): the vertical spread is J t/(2 q mu n), a fraction
  t^2/L^2 of the lateral drop with one contact (4 t^2/L^2 with two): 3.6e-7 over 10 um, 2.1 % in a 40 nm cell, not
  "below 1e-4 in every gate".
- **Pupil quadrature accuracy** (`measurement-averaging.md`): at the default `n_radial=4`, NA 0.5, 1.9e-11 for a
  uniform pupil but 1.2e-6 (power) / 2.4e-6 (amplitude) for a Gaussian of s0 = 0.35; n_radial >= 8 is recommended
  for a Gaussian with s0 <= NA (<= 1e-14 there).
- **`BursteinMossEdge` absorption edge in the KK transform**: the Maclaurin sum made an O(h) error at the optical
  gap whose size and sign depended on where the gap fell inside a grid cell. The edge is now integrated
  analytically (Gauss-Jacobi with the Tauc weight on a window above the gap, the Maclaurin sum beyond); dn moves by
  at most 1.4e-4 of the dn scale (p = 0.5) and 4e-3 relative (p = 0), and now matches the quad-to-infinity oracle to
  ~1e-5 at second order in the grid step. No keyword restores the old quadrature.
- **`validation/` is a regular package** (an `__init__.py`): as a namespace package it lost to any regular package
  named `validation` on the path (e.g. lumenairy's checkout), and about ten test files then imported the wrong
  modules. Release checks must assert `dynameta.__file__`, `lumenairy.__file__` and `validation.__file__` resolve
  to the intended trees: stale editable installs of both libraries can win over `PYTHONPATH`.
- **Square-root branch of a lossless medium** (`resonance._outgoing_sqrt`): the out-of-plane wavevector `_kz`
  took the principal root of `eps k0^2 - k_par^2` without the `+ 0j` of the numpy expression it replaced, so an
  argument on the negative real axis whose imaginary part was an exact -0.0 took the GROWING root -i sqrt(a). A
  lossless medium carries that -0.0 easily: `np.conj` of a real permittivity, `complex(x, -0.0)`, a negative
  permittivity times a positive number (a lossless metal eps = -20 - 0j on every Python version, and the square
  of the passive index (-0.0, +sqrt 20) that `tmm_reference._passive_sqrt` returns for it), and from Python 3.14
  on mixed complex-real arithmetic, which keeps the sign of a zero. Measured before the fix, a lossy film on a
  lossless metal half-space gave R = 0.990 instead of 0.854 in `layered_smatrix_complex` (s, normal incidence;
  `nonlocal_tmm.stack_rt` and `film_stack_rt` share the kernel and were off the same way);
  `quantum_film_cell.stack_admittance` returned the opposite-sign admittance; `fem_2d_tm` on
  such a substrate stopped with an OverflowError; `FilmCell` with a superstrate index 1 - 0j took the growing root
  for every evanescent top-port order on Python 3.14 only. One rule now: an exactly-zero imaginary part of the
  argument is read as +0.0 (the root is bit-identical to before for every other argument, gain included), and it
  also covers the substrate kz of `solve_fem` and the SHG background, `rudnick_stern_flat_shg`'s metal index and
  transmitted cosine (its totally-reflected case differed between Python 3.13 and 3.14), and the mirror index
  of `enz_reflector_stack` (the lumenairy bridges refused it as a gain half-space). Roots that already flip onto
  Im >= 0 were not affected. Cost: about 50 ns per `_kz` call, 1-4 % on `layered_smatrix_complex` /
  `_stack_denominator`. Tests: `test_outgoing_sqrt.py` (fast leg) and three solver-leg tests, each building the
  -0.0 explicitly on the square-root argument.

## 11. Known limitations

- **Equivalent quantum-film layer in structured cells**: errors of 0.5-5e-3 in r on the generic strip cell
  (lateral harmonics, not the specular first-order error); use `optics.quantum_film_cell` when that matters. The
  pipeline always hands the optics the equivalent layer; the exact route is lamellar cells with laterally uniform
  layers below the pattern only. Gating is laterally uniform.
- **RPA**: no in-plane dispersion beyond an estimate, xc only as adiabatic LDA (it changes absorption by up to 0.0009
  on the generic planar stack), one damping for every transition, hard walls unless barriers are given.
- **Hydrodynamic model**: the generic film at 1200 nm has u ~ 1.06-1.07, outside the model's validity; the solvers
  are correct for the model, the model needs a Lindhard-level response there. Hydrodynamic strips and adjacent
  hydrodynamic layers are refused in the 2-D FEM; a laterally varying density has consistency checks only.
- **Thin-film damping**: the out-of-plane rate is an empirical knob (no A >= 0 matches the quantum response's line
  shape); the background model's real part only, below the gap.
- **DG**: calibrated at 300 K on parabolic, hard-wall films; no gate for small-signal, noise or transient analyses
  on a DG device; refused with bipolar regions in the same device.
- **Gate leakage**: direct / Fowler-Nordheim tunnelling only (trap-assisted and Poole-Frenkel conduction are not
  modelled; use `LeakageTable`); refused in 3-D and with DG; no noise model.
- **RCWA 2-D Li rule**: not used by 'auto' (measured drift on two weak-metal 2-D cells; no strong-metal 2-D cell
  measured against a 3-D reference).
- **Measurement**: the conical path with the library solvers neglects s <-> p conversion (no solver returns the
  cross-polarized specular amplitude at conical incidence); the in-plane approximation is O(NA^2).
- **DEVSIM meshes**: legacy and user meshes keep the ~0.1 nm upper-face cell; a contact node on an interface is
  given to the contact only (inset by the gate-leakage builder, not yet by the drift-diffusion full-edge strips).
- **Platforms**: the new CI layout, Linux / Python 3.10-3.13 and the runner itself were not measured in the
  full-run package.
- **ENZ presets**: starting points, not film data. Deposition route, thickness and anneal move the optical
  mobility and eps_inf of a doped oxide by more than the dopant does (AZO 10-45 cm^2/(V s), eps_inf 2.9-3.9);
  fit your own film and pass the overrides. The damping comes from near-infrared data (set it from data for
  mid-IR / THz work); phonon parameters are room-temperature values; no amorphous IZO preset (no open data);
  the In:CdO background fall follows one paper's abstract (its text quotes a larger fall); AlN's static
  permittivity along c is uncertain by ~4 %.
