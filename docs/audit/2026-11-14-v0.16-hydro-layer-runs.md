# v0.16 P9: hydrodynamic layer runs and graded-density nonlocal films (finding Q6)

Package P9 of the 2026-11-14 gated thin-film plan, extended to graded density. Branch
`wip/v016_p09_hydro` (from ebdfe5c). Files: `dynameta/optics/nonlocal_tmm.py`,
`tests/test_nonlocal_tmm.py`, this note.

## What changed

- `HydroLayer(..., conductor_id=None)`: new LAST field (positional construction unchanged).
  Adjacent HydroLayers with one non-None id form a RUN: one connected electron gas, J_z = 0 only on
  the run's two outer faces. Different non-None ids are two conductors in contact: a wall between
  them (giving two materials the same id is how a user opts in to treating them as one connected
  gas). An unlabelled HydroLayer directly adjacent to another HydroLayer raises ValueError unless
  `allow_walls=True` is passed to `stack_rt` / `pole_function` (the explicit opt-in to the v0.15.1
  physics, bit-identical to it). A run mixing local-limit (beta = D = 0) and nonlocal members, or a
  nonlocal member with wp = 0, raises ValueError.
- A p-pol nonlocal run enters the 2x2 cascade as one block built by `_hydro_run_matrix_p` (stable
  banded global solve). A run of one layer uses `_hydro_char_matrix_p`, unchanged. s-pol and local
  runs use the per-layer local matrices exactly as before.
- Kernel (importable, for the 2-D FEM follow-on): `HYDRO_INTERFACE_FIELDS`, `hydro_conductivity`,
  `hydro_psi_per_charge`, `hydro_state_generator`, `hydro_modes`, `fermi_velocity`,
  `beta_from_density`.
- `hydro_layers_from_profile(z_m, n_m3, *, m_eff_kg, eps_inf, gamma, n_layers=None,
  convention="high_freq", D=0.0, conductor_id="graded")`: n(z) on a grid -> run (exact cell means
  of the piecewise-linear profile, so every cell's sheet density is conserved; local beta(n)).
- `hydro_validity(omega, layers, *, convention, u_limit, m_eff_kg, T_K, degeneracy_min)` ->
  `HydroValidity(u, degeneracy, ok, message)`; `stack_rt(check_validity=True)` emits
  `UnreliableResultWarning` on a failed report. Default False: no existing call warns.
- `pole_function` refuses a p-pol nonlocal run of >= 2 sub-layers (NotImplementedError).

## Derivation (the kernel the 2-D FEM follow-on should use)

Pressure model: a barotropic electron gas, pressure p(n), chemical potential mu(n), dp = n dmu.
Per-particle Euler equation (charge -e, effective mass m):

    m (dv/dt + gamma v) = -e E - grad mu(n).

Equilibrium n0(z) is taken to be in force balance under the SAME pressure, -e E0 = grad mu(n0) (this
is what makes a graded profile stationary). Linearize, n = n0 + n1, v = v1, E = E0 + E1: the zeroth
order cancels exactly and

    m (dv1/dt + gamma v1) = -e E1 - grad( mu'(n0) n1 ).

With J = -e n0 v1, rho1 = -e n1, wp**2 = n0 e**2/(eps0 m):

    (d/dt + gamma) J = eps0 wp**2 (E1 + grad psi),     psi := mu'(n0) n1 / e   [V],
    psi = -beta**2 rho1 / (eps0 wp**2),                 beta**2 := n0 mu'(n0) / m.

So J = sigma (E + grad psi) with sigma = eps0 wp**2/(gamma - i omega) (exp(-i omega t)), and
rho1 = div J/(i omega). For a uniform layer this is exactly the module's existing equation
beta**2 grad(div J) + omega (omega + i gamma) J = i omega eps0 wp**2 E. beta is LOCAL in the density:
for a degenerate parabolic band n0 mu'(n0) = (2/3) E_F, beta**2 = v_F(n0)**2/3 (Thomas-Fermi), and the
'high_freq' (3/5) v_F**2 is the same form with the pressure rescaled by 9/5. GNOR: beta**2 ->
beta_eff**2 = beta**2 + D (gamma - i omega) inside psi (the diffusion current, with an Einstein-
relation D, is driven by the same electrochemical gradient: J_diff = (sigma/e) grad(delta mu)).
Per unit volume the pressure term is e n0 grad(beta**2 n1 / n0) = eps0 wp**2 grad(psi), NOT
e grad(beta**2 n1); the two differ by the -e n1 E0 force of the equilibrium field, which is present
in a graded gas and absent in a uniform one.

Interface conditions inside one conductor. Divide the normal component of the momentum equation by
wp**2:

    d(psi)/dz = (gamma - i omega) J_z / (eps0 wp**2) - E_z.

Across an interface (or a thin smooth transition), J_z and E_z are bounded and wp**2 stays away from
zero, so the integral of d(psi)/dz over a vanishing width vanishes: psi is continuous. Charge
conservation, d(J_z)/dz = i omega rho1 - i k_par J_x, with a bounded rho1 (a sheet charge inside the
gas would put a delta-prime in grad psi that nothing balances) makes J_z continuous. With Maxwell:

    (E_x, H_y, J_z, psi) continuous at every internal interface;  J_z = 0 at the conductor boundary.

In the static limit this is "equal Fermi levels across the junction" (electrochemical potential
continuous). For one effective mass across the run, psi continuity is continuity of
beta**2 div(J)/wp**2 (the audit's original proposal); it is NOT continuity of the pressure term
beta**2 n1 = (m/e) n0 psi unless n0 is continuous. Energy: with real beta**2,
`(1/2) Re(conj(J) . E) = (1/2) Re(abs(J)**2 / sigma) - (1/2) Re div(psi conj(J))` (the `-i omega psi conj(rho)` term is
imaginary), so the conserved flux is S_z - (1/2) Re(psi* J_z): continuous at an interface exactly when
psi and J_z are, zero at a hard wall.

Planar p-pol state and generator, u = (E_x, H~ = H_y/(omega eps0), J~ = J_z/(omega eps0), psi),
Delta = eps_T - eps_inf (sigma = -i omega eps0 Delta), k0 = omega/c, fields ~ exp(i k_par x):

    dE_x/dz = i (k0**2 - k_par**2/eps_inf) H~ + (k_par/eps_inf) J~
    dH~/dz  = i eps_T E_x - k_par Delta psi
    dJ~/dz  = -k_par Delta E_x - i (k_par**2 Delta + wp**2/beta_eff**2) psi
    dpsi/dz = (k_par/eps_inf) H~ + i (1/Delta + 1/eps_inf) J~

(`hydro_state_generator`), eigenvalues +-i k_z,T, +-i k_z,L with closed-form eigenvectors
(`hydro_modes`): transverse H~ = 1, E_x = +-k_z,T/eps_T, J~ = i k_par Delta/eps_T, psi = 0;
longitudinal E = (k_par, +-k_z,L), H = 0, J~ = +-i eps_inf k_z,L, psi = i eps_T/Delta. For a graded
conductor the generator evaluated at each z IS the continuous ODE.

For the 2-D FEM: take psi as an H1 unknown over the whole conductor and J in H(div) (or eliminate J
via J = sigma (E + grad psi)); the internal conditions are then natural (conforming spaces), and only
J.n = 0 on the conductor boundary is imposed. wp(x, z) and beta(x, z) enter pointwise; use
`hydro_conductivity` and `hydro_psi_per_charge` for the coefficients.

## Solver

Unknowns: the four wave amplitudes of each sub-layer, '+' waves referenced at the top face, '-' waves
at the bottom face, k_z chosen with Im >= 0, so every exponential in the system is exp(i k_z d) with
modulus <= 1. Equations (4N): J_z = 0 at the top, E_x and H~ prescribed at the top (two right-hand
sides), 4-vector continuity at the N-1 interfaces, J_z = 0 at the bottom. Banded (6 below / 4 above),
row and column equilibrated, `scipy.linalg.solve_banded` (partial pivoting); the bottom (E_x, H~) of
the two solutions are the 2x2 matrix columns. No 4x4 transfer matrix is formed (it would carry
exp(+abs(k_z,L) d); abs(k_z,L) d = 334 for the 40 nm Na-like case below). Cost: 2 ms for N = 40, 16 ms
for N = 300, 0.2 s for N = 4000 per frequency.

## Plan statements found wrong

1. Section 2, "Q6 interface condition": continuity of beta**2 n1 (the pressure term) is not the jump
   condition between sub-layers of different equilibrium density. It follows from integrating the
   per-volume equation written with grad(beta**2 n1), which is the uniform-density form; in a graded
   gas the linearization about an equilibrium in force balance gives n0 grad(beta**2 n1/n0), and the
   continuous quantity is psi = beta**2 n1 m/(e n0) (equivalently beta**2 rho1/wp**2 for one mass) --
   the audit's original proposal. Evidence: derivation; energy (spurious gain of 3.3-6.0e-6 under the
   pressure condition, <= 2e-15 under psi); oracle (pressure condition plateaus at 1.3e-3, psi converges
   at second order to 3.4e-7).
2. P9 brief, "propagate the 4-vector through each sub-layer with its 4x4 transfer matrix": implemented
   as a banded global solve with locally referenced amplitudes instead, because the 4x4 transfer
   matrix carries exp(+abs(k_z,L) d). The state and its continuity are as the brief says.
3. "Two adjacent HydroLayers with conductor_id=None raise" is extended to an unlabelled HydroLayer
   adjacent to a labelled one (the same ambiguity).

## Left undone / limits

- `pole_function` for p-pol nonlocal runs: refused. The run's matrix has its own standing-wave poles;
  the natural clearing factor (the determinant of the global system) depends on the face each wave is
  referenced to and is not analytic in omega. Locate such modes with `stack_rt` on the real axis.
- The near-infrared degenerate-oxide fixture is outside the hydrodynamic model's validity
  (u ~ 1). The solver returns the model's answer; `hydro_validity` reports it. A quantitative
  graded-film response in that regime needs a Lindhard-level (or quantum) response.
- `hydro_layers_from_profile` assumes a parabolic band and one mass; for a nonparabolic band build the
  layers by hand with beta**2 = n0 mu'(n0)/m_opt and wp from the optical mass (the interface
  conditions do not depend on how beta was obtained).
- No new validation script (none listed for P9); `python -m validation.nonlocal_hydro_material` passes.
