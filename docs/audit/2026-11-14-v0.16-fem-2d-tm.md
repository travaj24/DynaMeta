## Public API

```python
from dynameta.optics.fem_2d_tm import TMLayer, TMBlock, GriddedEps2D, TMMeshSpec, solve_fem_2d_tm

layers = [                                     # BOTTOM FIRST (substrate side first, like Stack)
    TMLayer("mirror", 80e-9, -120 + 20j),
    TMLayer("spacer", 8e-9, 4.0),
    TMLayer("film", 6e-9, GriddedEps2D(values=eps_z, z_m=z)),          # z-graded, z from layer bottom
    TMLayer("grating", 40e-9, 1.0, (TMBlock(125e-9, 275e-9, -60 + 4j, "strip"),)),
]
res = solve_fem_2d_tm(400e-9, layers, lambda_m=1200e-9, theta_deg=0.0, n_super=1.0, n_sub=1.45,
                      polarization="x", order=4)            # -> OpticalResult
res.r, res.R, res.T, res.per_region_absorption, res.A_independent
```

- `TMLayer(name, thickness_m, eps, blocks=(), maxh_z_m=None)`; `TMBlock(x_lo_m, x_hi_m, eps, name=None)`
  (a block crossing x = 0 or x = P wraps); an eps is a complex scalar, `(exx, ezz)`, a 2x2 x-z tensor
  (off-diagonal allowed) or a `GriddedEps2D` (values `(Nz,)`, `(Nz, Nx)` or `(..., 2, 2)`; z relative
  to the layer bottom and checked against the thickness; x periodic).
- `TMMeshSpec`: `maxh_m`, `metal_h_per_skin`, `h_scale` (uniform refinement), `corner_levels` (6),
  `corner_ratio` (0.25), `corner_mode` ('contrast' (default) / 'metal' / 'all' / 'none'),
  `corner_contrast` (2.0), `min_layer_elements`, buffers, `pml_thickness_m`, `pml_elements`,
  `pml_attenuation`, `max_grid_lines`. `solve_fem_2d_tm` also takes
  `maxh_m=` and `corner_refinement=` (levels, `(levels, ratio)` or 0) as overrides.
- Split pieces: `build_tm_cell(...) -> TMCell` (mesh + region table + reference planes),
  `solve_tm_cell(cell, polarization, order, extensions) -> TMSolution` (OpticalResult + per-order
  amplitudes/efficiencies, `energy_residual`, `pml_reflection`, ndof, timing).
- Design path: `design_to_tm_layers(design, eps_by_region, lambda_m)`, `solve_design_2d_tm(design,
  eps_by_region, lambda_m, ...)`, and `make_fem_2d_tm_optical_solver(...)` (the run_pipeline seam,
  with `solve_sweep` and `cache_fingerprint`) for 1-D lamellar designs.
- `tm_convergence_study(solve, settings)`: r per setting with its change from the previous and from
  the finest row.

Conventions (same as the RCWA/PMM bridges and `optics.tmm_reference`): exp(-i omega t); r at the
top interface, t at the bottom one over the incident field at the top; 'x' = lab E_x ratio (normal
incidence only), 'p' = the p-hat convention (r = r_H = -E_x ratio, t = t_H n_super/n_sub); R/T are
order-summed over propagating orders (the 3-D FEM's are specular only). `fit_relres` on the
OpticalResult carries the PML-reflection estimate, `R_flux/T_flux` equal R/T (all-order Fourier
projections, no fit).

## Formulation and why H_y

`div(Lambda grad H_y) + k0^2 H_y = 0`, `Lambda = eps^T / det(eps)` on `(d/dx, d/dz)`, on a
Bloch-periodic H1 space, scattered field relative to the analytic two-half-space Fresnel background,
explicit stretched-coordinate PML (quadratic profile) in z, structured tensor-product quadrilateral
mesh with every interface and block edge a mesh line, geometric grading at metal corners and at
high-contrast dielectric corners.

Chosen over the in-plane E (H(curl)) form because: (1) one scalar H1 unknown, about half the DOFs at
equal order; (2) H_y is continuous and bounded at metal corners -- the corner singularity sits in
grad H, which the geometric grading resolves with exponential-type convergence in the order (table
below), whereas the E form carries the singular field itself; (3) continuity of tangential E is the
natural interface condition, so 1 nm layers need no special treatment; (4) the Bloch phase and the
PML act on a scalar.

How the nonlocal follow-on fits (the owner's constraint): the solver assembles an optional list of
EXTENSION fields on a product space with H_y first (`space(ctx)`, `add_terms(ctx, a, f, h, hv, x, xv)`,
optional `absorption(...)`). A hydrodynamic polarisation current J_p in H(div) of the conductor
subdomain enters with `E = eps_b^-1 (J grad H - J_p)/(-i omega eps0)`: the Maxwell row gains
`+int (J eps_b^-1 J_p) . grad v`, the current row is
`int beta^2 div J_p div K + [wp^2/eps_b - omega(omega + i gamma)] J_p . K - (wp^2/eps_b)(J grad H) . K
= (wp^2/eps_b)(J grad H_bg) . K`, with `J_p . n = 0` essential on the conductor boundary and graded
`wp^2(x, z)`, `beta^2(x, z)` built by the same gridded-coefficient code as the permittivity. The
natural interface condition of the `beta^2` div-div term is continuity of `beta^2 n1`, the condition
section 2 of the plan derives for Q6. The library's stabilized scalar-potential form
(`hydro_fem.scattering_2d`, psi = div J in H1 on the metal) also transfers: in the H_y form its
couplings become `int (beta^2/Omega) eps_T^-1 (J grad v) . grad psi` and `int eps_T^-1 (J grad H) .
grad w`, i.e. the same structure with E replaced by `eps_T^-1 J grad H`. The hook is gated now with
beta = 0: a local Drude current carried as an explicit VectorL2 unknown reproduces the local
permittivity solve on the same mesh to |dr| = 1.8e-12 and closes energy through its own absorption
hook (residual 5e-10).

Hydrodynamic input is refused (`HydroParams`, `HydroLayer`, `HydroSlab`, anything with `beta` and
`wp`, finite-k Mermin) with a NotImplementedError naming the gap.

## Deviations from the plan, and plan statements found wrong

1. **Operator.** The brief writes the weak form "with inverse tensor eps^-1 = diag(1/eps_xx,
   1/eps_zz)". Read as the coefficient of (d/dx, d/dz) that is the transpose of the right operator:
   for H_y the x-derivative carries 1/eps_zz and the z-derivative 1/eps_xx (Lambda = eps^T/det eps).
   Implemented correctly and gated (the uniaxial oblique test fails by > 1e-3 with the components
   swapped).
2. **No importable PML/mesh helper exists in `optics/solver.py`** -- its PML is inline code inside
   `solve_fem` (`mesh.SetPML` with constant alpha=1j, or the explicit UPML lambdas). The 2-D solver
   carries its own explicit stretched-coordinate PML with a quadratic profile sized from the requested
   attenuation (`_pml_profile`). Reused instead: `solver._apply_fem_threads`,
   `solver._free_dof_relres`, `tmm_reference.end_media_indices`,
   `core.layered.collapse_regions_to_layers`, `geometry.guards.require_supported_geometry`,
   `core.eps_field.require_solver_time_convention`, `core.polarization.pol_vocabulary_error`.
3. **Mesh.** A structured tensor-product quadrilateral mesh (built point by point with netgen
   `Mesh`), not netgen's unstructured OCC mesher: thin layers cost one element row, no material
   boundary cuts an element, and the geometric corner grading is exact. Corner refinement is
   geometric grading (not `mesh.Refine` around points).
4. **Gridded fields.** A laterally uniform z-graded field is used on its native, possibly
   non-uniform z samples through an exact piecewise-linear coefficient (NGSolve `BSpline` in z), with
   mesh lines at the samples -- no resampling. Only a laterally structured field goes through
   `VoxelCoefficient` on a uniform resample (as the brief says), with mesh lines on the voxel grid up
   to `max_grid_lines` (an AdvisoryWarning beyond).
5. **`eps_xz=None` keyword not provided.** Per-layer / per-block eps specs (scalar, (exx, ezz), full
   2x2 incl. xz, gridded tensor) cover tensor input and keep every coefficient tied to a region whose
   interfaces are mesh lines.
6. **Dense superstrate at oblique incidence is supported** (kx = n_super k0 sin theta), unlike the
   3-D FEM which requires vacuum there; a lossy superstrate is refused, as in the 3-D FEM.
7. **Strip tolerance tightened** from the brief's 1e-3 to 2e-4 (measured 4.3e-5 at the default on the
   generic cell).
8. **Hydro docstring in `optics/hydro_fem.py`** (the brief asks for the Q7.2 limitation there too):
   not a P10-owned file. REQUEST to whoever integrates (P15): add one sentence to the
   `hydro_fem.py` module docstring: "A nonlocal response with graded density inside a periodic
   structured cell is not implemented; the local 2-D periodic TM solver is optics.fem_2d_tm, which
   refuses hydrodynamic input and documents the extension path."
9. **N2 single kernel (rule 5).** `GriddedEps2D` checks its z span against the layer thickness in
   this module (it does not slice, so P1's `slice_layer_eps_field` does not apply on this path).
   When P1 lands, the integrator may want the Design adapter (`_epsfield_to_spec`) to call P1's
   extent check so the error message is shared; no behaviour would change.
10. **`ci.yml`** gains one test-file line (the brief does not list it, but rule 7 and the CI
    coverage gate require it; P15 unions the list).

## Left undone / uncertain

- TE (E along the lines) is refused, not implemented (a scalar E_y version of the same machinery
  would be short); conical incidence is refused (not a 2-D problem).
- The extension hook is exercised only with a local current (beta = 0); the hydrodynamic current,
  its stabilization in a graded density and the interface conditions are the deferred Q7.2 work.
- The PML is not angle-adaptive beyond sizing alpha on the slowest propagating order; validated to
  35 deg (planar, 1e-9) and 20 deg (strip). Grazing orders raise.
- Near a diffraction cutoff the automatic cladding buffer grows as 8 decay lengths of the slowest
  evanescent order (the imaginary-stretch PML does not attenuate evanescent orders; measured: capping
  the buffer at 2 lambda/n moved R + T of a lossless 1.28 um grating by 1.1e-2). It is capped at
  30 lambda/n with an UnreliableResultWarning; a complex-frequency-shifted PML (real stretch) would
  remove this cost and is a possible follow-on.

## Later fixes

- **Auto buffer in a LOSSY cladding** (`_auto_buffer`). An order counted as evanescent only when
  |Re kz| < 1e-12 k0, which no lossy cladding meets, so any loss dropped the decay-length rule:
  n_sub = 1.45 + 1e-6j shrank the substrate buffer of a 1.28 um rib grating (1.04 um, 10 deg, p) from
  21.14 um to 359 nm and moved r by 1.2e-2. Now an order is evanescent when it lies beyond the LOSSLESS
  cut-off, Re(kz^2) = Re(eps) k0^2 - kx^2 <= 0 (the exact complement of the PML's "propagating" test,
  Re kz > |Im kz|), and its decay length is 1/Im(kz) of the full complex kz; 8-decay-length rule and
  30 lambda/n cap unchanged. Lossless claddings: bit-identical buffers and r. 1e-6 loss: within 6.9e-8
  of the lossless buffer. n_sub = 1.45 + 1e-3j: the new 19.96 um buffer is 4.2e-8 from a 4x longer one,
  the old 359 nm buffer 7.3e-3. 1.45 + 0.1j: 3.4 um (8 decay lengths, 1/Im kz = 0.42 um), 1.1e-11.
  Tests: `test_auto_buffer_*` in tests/test_fem_2d_tm.py.
- **T of a lossy transparent substrate** (behaviour change: T for lossy substrates now includes all
  orders). T summed order 0 only whenever Im(eps_sub) >= 1e-12, so R + T + A missed the diffracted
  substrate orders (residual 0.46 and an UnreliableResultWarning on the grating above at
  n_sub = 1.45 + 1e-3j). T is now the power entering the substrate at the interface over ALL orders,
  by Poynting's theorem in the substrate buffer: the buffer's absorption (the same Im(conj(Lambda
  grad H) . grad H) integral as per_region_absorption) plus the all-order z-flux leaving the buffer's
  bottom face into the PML (face amplitudes from the probe lines, propagated downward, so no order is
  amplified). Residual 2.7e-8 (lossless 2.4e-8); continuous: |T - T_lossless| = 1.2e-11 at
  Im(n) = 1e-12, linear in the loss; the lossless path is unchanged bit for bit. T_orders still lists
  the orders above the lossless cut-off (their sum is below T in a lossy substrate).
  Test: `test_lossy_substrate_transmission_counts_every_order`.

  grating-coupled total internal reflection). The substrate was called opaque when ORDER 0 decayed

  propagating diffracted order lost its transmission: T None, the transmitted power reported as
  absorption (A = 1 - R), the bottom buffer / PML sized for the order-0 decay with unit strength.
