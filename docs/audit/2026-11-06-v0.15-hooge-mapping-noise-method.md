## 1. Per-region / per-carrier Hooge alpha (4.2.4)

**What was wrong.** `noise_spectrum(hooge_alpha=...)` took one float for every region and both carriers.
alpha_H is a material (and sample) parameter: Hooge 1994 (IEEE TED 41, 1926) gives ~1e-4 for a perfect
lattice and 1e-6 .. 1e-3 measured; the historical 2e-3 is, per that paper, an average over "rather poor
samples". A device with two materials could not be modelled, and there was nowhere to store a material's
value.

**What changed.**
* `noise_analysis.noise_spectrum(hooge_alpha=...)` also accepts a mapping `{region: alpha | (alpha_n,
  alpha_p)}`. A region absent from the mapping, or a `None` entry of a pair, carries no Hooge source (it
  contributes exactly 0). A non-finite or non-positive alpha, a pair of the wrong length, an unknown region
  and a region without a continuity equation raise `ValueError` naming the entry. Regions left out of
  `regions=` are ignored. The resolver is `_resolve_hooge_alpha`; `_RegionData` now receives the region's
  `(alpha_n, alpha_p)` (`_region_hooge_alphas`). For a float that is `(float(a), float(a))`, and the source
  is still `math.sqrt(alpha) * J / sqrt(n_eff)`, so the float path is the v0.14 arithmetic.
* `TransportModel.hooge_alpha_n` / `hooge_alpha_p` (appended, default `None`, no per-material defaults).
  They must be finite and > 0. Electrons need `drift_diffusion` or `bipolar_dd`, holes need `bipolar_dd`;
  otherwise the value would be silently ignored, so it raises. The only reader is
  `LayeredDevsimBuilder.noise_spectrum(hooge="material")` (section 2).
* Docs: the noise_analysis module doc (item 2) now gives the alpha values and their sources;
  `noise_spectrum`'s docstring says when to use a float and when a mapping; `TransportModel`'s Args.
* `tests/test_band_temperature.py::test_defaults_off` asserted that `band_temperature` is the LAST
  TransportModel field. The two new fields are appended after it, so the assertion now checks the order
  it was protecting (band_temperature right after carrier_statistics, then the two Hooge fields).

**Finding against the plan's gate.** The plan's oracle "S f = I^2 Sum_r alpha_r / N_r" leaves out the
resistance-share weights. Re-derived in the test: S = Int alpha (J . grad y)^2 / (n f) dV with y = x/L gives
`S_r f = I^2 (L_r/L)^2 alpha_r / N_r`, the series sum S_I/I^2 = Sum (R_r/R)^2 S_{R_r}/R_r^2. On the gate's
fixture the plan's form is off by a factor 2.41 (MEASURED, asserted). The gates use the weighted form.

**A second finding: the split is first order in the bias, the total second order.** With one alpha in both
regions the total meets the gate-F3 budget max v^2/12 (MEASURED -2.32e-10, budget 2.42e-10). The per-region
split was off by +5.4e-8 / -2.4e-8, though. The cause is in DEVSIM's own adjoint field: next to the grounded
contact it sits 6.3e-9 above x/L, the DD contact layer at 1 mV. That moves noise between regions at first
order. The total sees it only at second order, because Sum Delta y is fixed by the two contacts. The test
derives budget (b) from eta = max |(dy/dx) L - 1|. It reads DEVSIM's native adjoint, with the contact rows
filled exactly as the module doc states. That is the field noise_analysis consumes, not its source
assembly, which is the code under test. Then S_T = S_T^closed (1 + eta_T)^2. On the intrinsic bipolar bar
the neutral perturbations are not screened, so the bias bends y over the whole bar at O(V/V_T). MEASURED:
the per-carrier ratios were off by +1.8e-2 / -5.0e-2 at 1 mV. That bar is therefore biased at 0.1 uV.

## 2. `LayeredDevsimBuilder.noise_spectrum` / `terminal_noise` (4.2.5)

**What was wrong.** The hand recipe for gate noise had four traps:
1. `solve`, then `set_ssac_gate`, then `circuit_alter`, then a raw `ds.solve` re-settle. That bypasses
   the extended Newton, the density-scaled default abs_tol and the well-posedness check.
2. The circuit is process-global and cannot be deleted. A second `set_ssac_gate` died inside DEVSIM AFTER
   the gate's bias Dirichlet had been deleted, leaving a device without a gate equation.
3. The results are per unit depth.
4. Nothing forced the default mechanism list to follow the noise module's.

**What changed (`dynameta/carriers/devsim_layered.py`).**
* `noise_spectrum(frequencies, *, gate, vg=None, source_name="V1", node_name="vac", mechanisms=None,
  hooge=None, external_generation_shot=True, regions=None, maps=False, per_cell=True, rel_tol=1e-5,
  abs_tol=None, max_iter=60, v_step=0.25) -> LayeredNoiseSpectrum`. `terminal_noise(...)` takes the same
  arguments and adds Y from `ssac_admittance` and S_V = S_I/|Y|^2 at the same state.
* **Circuit-drive once.** `set_ssac_gate` is called the first time; the gate and its node are recorded.
  * Later calls reuse it. The same gate under another source name raises `ValueError`.
  * `set_ssac_gate` itself now refuses a name already in the process-global circuit (or a second call for
    the same electrode) with `ValueError` BEFORE it removes the bias Dirichlet. A circuit DEVSIM refuses
    raises `RuntimeError` naming the fresh-process remedy (fix (b) of P2 6.2).
* **Library re-settle.** The gate's source is ramped from its last converged bias to vg in `v_step` steps
  (bipolar: min(v_step, 0.05)). Each step is ONE `dc_solve.solve_dc` with the branch `solve()` uses (DD:
  `mesh_2d.dc_method` + semiconductor_regions; bipolar: newton).
* **State restore.** Every equation variable of every region and the circuit `dcop` / `dcop_prev` values
  are saved before the call and restored after it (in a `finally`). The gate's own circuit unknowns start
  every call at 0.0, the value DEVSIM gives a new circuit node. So a result depends only on (state, vg),
  and `solve()` continues from where it was. On an extended-precision builder the restore is to the
  double-rounded values; this is documented, not gated.
* **Per cell.** By default S, every breakdown and every map are multiplied by `UnitCell.period_y_m`.
  * This is exact for this builder's y-invariant device with spatially white sources (S_cell = W S_2D).
  * It is not the noise of a cell whose geometry varies along y (a square patch modelled by its x-z
    section), nor of a source correlated over lengths ~W. The docstring says so.
  * `per_cell=False` returns the unscaled per-depth result.
  * `LayeredNoiseSpectrum` (a `NoiseSpectrum` subclass defined in the builder module; noise_analysis is
    untouched for 4.2.5) records units, depth_m, gate, vg_V and voltages.
* **Mechanisms.** `mechanisms=None` means `noise_analysis.DEFAULT_MECHANISMS`, read at CALL time. A later
  default-on source (the trap keys of plan row 5) propagates without touching the builder.
* **Hooge.** `hooge="material"` maps each carrier region to its material's (`hooge_alpha_n`, `hooge_alpha_p`,
  the latter only on bipolar regions). Regions without either are left out, and all-empty means no Hooge.
  A float or a mapping is passed through.
* **Scope (decision 35).** An electrode with carrier contact equations, or on a carrier region, raises
  `OhmicContactNoiseError(NotImplementedError)`. An unbuilt or unsolved builder raises `RuntimeError`. A
  bipolar builder at vg != 0 warns `UnreliableResultWarning`, the builder's gated-bipolar caveat.
* The bipolar solve path now records `_last_converged_bias`, which the method's vg default reads. The
  bipolar solve itself never reads it, so this is no behaviour change.

**Gates** (`tests/test_devsim_noise_method.py`, 11 tests). One module fixture runs 7 child processes

## Release-note lines

* `noise_spectrum(hooge_alpha=...)` accepts a per-region mapping `{region: alpha | (alpha_n, alpha_p)}`.
  Regions not listed get no 1/f source. `TransportModel.hooge_alpha_n` / `hooge_alpha_p` store a
  material's values; they have no defaults. Use ~1e-4 (Hooge 1994, perfect lattice) or your measured
  value, not the old 2e-3. A plain float behaves exactly as before.
* New `LayeredDevsimBuilder.noise_spectrum` / `terminal_noise`: the noise of a dielectric gate in one call.
  It drives the gate circuit once, re-settles through the library solver (so extended precision, the
  default tolerance and the well-posedness check apply), and returns results per cell by default. It
  inherits the noise module's default mechanisms, so thermionic-interface noise is included. It leaves the
  builder's operating point unchanged. Ohmic contacts are refused (`OhmicContactNoiseError`) for now.
* `set_ssac_gate` refuses a source or node name the process-global circuit already holds BEFORE it
  removes the gate's bias contact. Before, DEVSIM failed after the removal and left a broken device.
