## Q1. Warm start honours the boundary potentials

`solve_self_consistent(U_init_J=...)` built the initial potential as `-U/q` on the whole grid; the Newton updates
interior nodes only, so the warm start's END values were the boundary conditions and `phi_left_V` /
`phi_right_V` were ignored. New helper `_warm_start_phi`: `phi = -U/q + linspace(phi_left - phi[0],
phi_right - phi[-1], N)`, then the two ends are written exactly (`a + (b - a)` need not round to `b`). The ramp is
identically zero when the ends already match, so that case is bit-identical to v0.15.1. A wrong-shape `U_init_J`
now raises. The Neumann-body branch is unchanged in structure (Poisson stays Dirichlet at both ends); the bulk-buffer
branch is covered by its recursion.

| case (6 nm, 241 nodes, 0.5 V -> 1.0 V warm start) | v0.15.1 | v0.16 |
|---|---|---|
| `phi2[-1]` | 0.5 | 1.0 exactly |
| max abs(n_warm - n_cold) / max n_cold | 0.34-0.39 | 1.8e-7 (Dirichlet), 2.6e-7 (Neumann body), 2.9e-7 (bulk buffer) |

## Q3. Bulk Fermi level of the sub-band model

Derivation. The fill is separable: parabolic z dispersion (mass m) and per sub-band the in-plane Kane sheet
density `n_s = pref0 [kT F_0(eta) + 2 a kT^2 F_1(eta)]`. In a wide flat-band slab, `E(k_z) = hbar^2 k_z^2/2m`,
`k_z = i pi/L`, `<|psi_i|^2> = 1/L`, so `n_sep(E_F) = (1/pi) Int_0^inf dk_z n_s`. With `u = hbar^2 k_z^2/(2 m kT)`
and exchanging the integrals, `Int u^(-1/2) F_0(eta - u) du = sqrt(pi) F_1/2(eta)` and
`Int u^(-1/2) F_1(eta - u) du = sqrt(pi) F_3/2(eta)` (normalized F_j), giving the closed form
`n_sep = N_c [F_1/2(eta_F) + 2 a kT F_3/2(eta_F)]`, `N_c = g_s g_v (m kT / 2 pi hbar^2)^(3/2)`. At T -> 0 the ratio
to the 3D Kane density at the same E_F is `(1 + 0.8 a E_F)/(1 + a E_F)^(3/2)`.

Implementation: ONE kernel, `sp_bulk_density_m3` (quadrature of the solver's own `_fermi_log`/`_fd1` over k_z to
`max(E_F, 0) + 60 kT`) and `sp_bulk_fermi_level_J` (brentq with a proven bracket); method wrappers
`SchrodingerPoisson1D.bulk_density` / `.bulk_fermi_level`. The legacy relation is `bulk_fermi_level_3d_T0_J`
(bit-identical to the v0.15.1 carrier expression). `SchrodingerPoissonCarrier(fermi_level="model")` is the default;
`fermi_level="bulk3d_T0"` restores v0.15.1 exactly (bitwise test).

Deviation from the plan: brentq `xtol` is `1e-12 kT` (about 4e-33 J), not 1e-28 J. With 1e-28 J the density error
is bounded only by ~2e-8 (dn/n ~ dE/kT in the non-degenerate limit); measured 1.8e-10 at 1e22 m^-3 against the closed
form evaluated with mpmath (the degenerate-film value is not recorded here). With `1e-12 kT` the
closed form is met to <1e-12.

| quantity (3e26 m^-3, 0.30 m_e, 300 K) | v0.15.1 (T = 0 3D) | v0.16 (model) |
|---|---|---|
| E_F, alpha 0 | 0.544707 eV | 0.543695 eV (-1.013 meV) |
| E_F, alpha 0.4 | 0.460049 eV | 0.492361 eV (+32.312 meV) |
| n_sep(E_F)/n_bg, alpha 0 / 0.4 | 1.00278 / 0.89570 | 1 to < 1e-10 (closed form by quadrature) |

## N4. Gate-voltage to surface-potential map

Derivation (body z = 0 at phi = 0; film to z = t; oxide to t + t_ox; gate at Vg; no work-function difference, no
interface charge). The oxide is charge-free, so its field is uniform, `dphi/dz = (Vg - psi_s)/t_ox`. Continuity of
the normal displacement at z = t: `eps_s eps0 phi'(t-) = eps_ox eps0 (Vg - psi_s)/t_ox`, i.e.
`Vg = psi_s + F_face/C_ox` with `F_face = eps_s eps0 phi'(t-)` (signed: > 0 when the potential rises toward the gate,
accumulation; < 0 in depletion). Integrating Poisson over the film, `F_face = F_body + q Int (n - N_d) dz`.

Discrete flux: the solver's interior stencil is the control-volume balance `F_{i+1/2} - F_{i-1/2} = -q h (Nd_i - n_i)`;
the half cell of the end node gives `F_face = eps (phi[-1] - phi[-2])/h - q (h/2)(Nd[-1] - n[-1])` (second order;
left end `eps (phi[1] - phi[0])/h + q (h/2)(Nd[0] - n[0])`), and the sum of all cells is the exact discrete Gauss law
`F_right - F_left = q trapz(n - Nd)`. ONE function, `sp_face_flux_C_m2(phi, n, Nd, h, eps_r, side=...)`, for P12.

Maps (`SchrodingerPoissonCarrier(gate_map=...)`):
- `"face_field"` (default, decision 9): residual `psi_s + F_face(psi_s)/C_ox - Vg`. Since F_face does not decrease
  with psi_s, the residual has slope >= 1, so its root lies between 0 and `-g(0)`: one column solve brackets it.
  brentq to 1e-9 V.
- `"flat_band_excess"`: the v0.15.1 method with the sign fixed, `psi + s q N_exc(s psi)/C_ox - |Vg|`, N_exc the
  signed excess over the psi_s = 0 profile; `[0, |Vg|]` brackets for both signs (r(0) = -|Vg|, and N_exc(s|Vg|) has
  the sign s). brentq to 1e-9 V; the bracket expansion and the UnreliableResultWarning stay for a column whose excess
  has the wrong sign. It equals the face-field law with the flat-band face field dropped; psi_s(0 V) = 0 by
  construction.

The plan's `xtol 1e-7 V` was tightened to 1e-9 V: the residual's slope in psi_s reaches ~10 in accumulation (most of
Vg on the oxide), so 1e-7 V in psi_s would allow ~1e-6 V in the Gauss residual, the test's own bar. The cost is one
or two extra column solves.

## Release-note lines

- `SchrodingerPoissonCarrier` E_F is the sub-band model's own finite-temperature bulk level (`fermi_level="model"`);
  `fermi_level="bulk3d_T0"` restores the old level. Moves E_F by -1.01 meV at alpha 0 and +32.3 meV at
  alpha 0.4 eV^-1 (3e26 m^-3, 0.30 m_e) and removes the matching spurious band bending.
- `SchrodingerPoissonCarrier` gate map: depletion biases were wrong (sign error; psi_s about -17 V at -2 V). Default
  is now Gauss's law at the oxide face (`gate_map="face_field"`); the sign-fixed old method is
  `gate_map="flat_band_excess"`. Both solve to 1e-9 V. psi_s moves by 0.20-0.30 V against the flat-band map on a 6 nm
  film (-4..+2 V); at 0 V it is no longer 0 with an oxide given.
- `SchrodingerPoisson1D.solve_self_consistent` warm starts honour `phi_left_V` / `phi_right_V`.
- New: `SchrodingerPoisson1D.bulk_fermi_level`, `.bulk_density`; module functions `sp_bulk_fermi_level_J`,
  `sp_bulk_density_m3`, `bulk_fermi_level_3d_T0_J`, `sp_face_flux_C_m2`; `SchrodingerPoissonCarrier.gate_face_flux`.
