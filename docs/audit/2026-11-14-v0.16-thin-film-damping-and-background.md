## Usage

```python
from dynameta.constants import M_E
from dynameta.materials import (DrudeOptical, Material, MatthiessenGamma, BoundaryScattering,
                                BandFillingBackground, TabulatedBackground, KaneOpticalMass)
from dynameta.materials.transport_model import KaneBand, TransportModel

band = KaneBand(m0_kg=0.22 * M_E, alpha_per_eV=0.4)
film = Material("FILM", optical=DrudeOptical(
        eps_inf=4.0,                                   # = eps_b at n_ref for an anchored background
        m_opt_kg=band.optical_mass(),                  # v_F of the size terms follows this band
        gamma_rad_s=MatthiessenGamma(gamma_const_rad_s=1.2e14),        # any bulk rate (float / callable)
        boundary=BoundaryScattering(specularity=0.0,                   # in-plane Fuchs-Sondheimer
                                    perp_coefficient=0.0,              # out-of-plane A (empirical; see below)
                                    perp_length="film",                # or "local"
                                    thickness_m=None),                 # None: the carrier region's extent
        background=BandFillingBackground(gap_eV=3.75, edge_amplitude=2.5, n_ref_m3=3e26,
                                         conduction=band, valence_mass_kg=None, bgr_coeff_eV_m=4.5e-10)),
    transport=TransportModel(n_bg_m3=3e26, eps_static=9.0, physics="equilibrium", band=band))
# run_pipeline / assemble_eps: the film becomes a graded (Nz,Ny,Nx,3,3) diagonal tensor EpsField.
film.optical.eps_tensor(1200e-9, n_m3=n_profile, z_axis_m=z)   # direct use; eps() refuses (uniaxial)
TabulatedBackground(n_m3=(1e25, 1e26, 1e27), eps_inf=(4.05, 3.95, 3.7))   # measured eps_inf(n), absolute
DrudeOptical(4.0, 0.30 * M_E, 1.2e14, background=lambda n, lam: my_eps_inf(n))  # callable, absolute
```

## Part 1: size- and position-dependent damping

### Models

- **In plane (eps_xx = eps_yy), Fuchs-Sondheimer, exact.** sigma = F(kappa, p) sigma_Drude with
  F = 1 - (3(1-p)/(2 kappa)) int_1^inf (t^-3 - t^-5)(1 - e^{-kappa t})/(1 - p e^{-kappa t}) dt (Fuchs, Proc.
  Camb. Phil. Soc. 34, 100 (1938); Sondheimer, Adv. Phys. 1, 1 (1952)). At frequency omega the Boltzmann
  equation is the d.c. one with 1/tau -> 1/tau - i omega, so for a field uniform across the film
  kappa = d (gamma - i omega)/v_F (complex). Evaluated as 1/4 - (1-p) sum_m p^(m-1) (E_3 - E_5)(m kappa) (E_1 from
  scipy, upward recurrence for |z| < 50, asymptotic series beyond; the p-series is stopped by a rigorous tail
  bound). Limits: p = 1 -> F = 1 exactly; |kappa| >> 1 -> F = 1 - 3(1-p)/(8 kappa), i.e. an additive
  (Matthiessen) rate 3(1-p) v_F/(8d) at any frequency; kappa -> 0 -> (3/4) kappa (ln(1/kappa) + 0.4228).
  **The d.c. size effect is NOT the optical one**: at 1200 nm |kappa| ~ 11.8 for the 6 nm generic film (thick
  in the optical sense) although d < l (thin in the d.c. sense); using the d.c. rate optically over-states the
  extra damping (6.17e13 vs 5.01e13 rad/s below).
- **Out of plane (eps_zz), Kreibig-type Matthiessen rate.** gamma_zz = gamma_bulk(n) + A v_F(n)/L, a
  PHENOMENOLOGY with an order-one coefficient A (Kreibig and Vollmer 1995; L_eff = 4V/S, Coronado and Schatz,
  J. Chem. Phys. 119, 3926 (2003); for a slab 4V/S = 2d, in which convention the in-plane FS thick-film rate
  is A = (3/4)(1-p)). `perp_length="film"`: L = 2d. `"local"`: 1/L(z) = 1/(2d) + |dn/dz|/n -- a HEURISTIC
  confinement length for carriers in an accumulation/depletion layer; it returns the film value where n is
  flat (including at an extremum of n(z)); no wall-distance term (1/distance makes the sheet damping
  log-divergent for a classical profile that is finite at the face).
- **Density dependence.** v_F = hbar k_F/m_opt(n) at the LOCAL density (`nonlocal_tmm.fermi_velocity` per kg
  divided by m_opt(n): the shared kernel takes a scalar mass; for a `KaneOpticalMass` this is exactly the Kane
  v_F, gated against dE/dk of the dispersion). The bulk rate keeps its existing seam: a density-dependent
  ionized-impurity or grain-boundary rate is ALREADY expressible (callable gamma(n), MatthiessenGamma's
  Brooks-Herring channel / constant floor), so no mobility input mu(n) was added (checked first, reused).
- **Which component.** The output is diag(eps_par, eps_par, eps_perp) with z the assembler's stack axis.
  Validity: classical motion across the film (k_F d >> 1; k_F d = 12.4 here: indicative), d << skin depth,
  isotropic tau, degenerate gas. Do not combine with `optics.quantum_film` (it contains the out-of-plane
  physics) or a hydrodynamic layer with GNOR D > 0 (itself a size-dependent damping); the quantum-film module
  uses one damping for every transition and adds none of these terms, so there is no double count unless a
  user feeds both.

Flat band (A1200 only): 10 nm A_RPA 0.00377 -> A = 1.72 (film) / 0.40 (local); 20 nm 0.00745 -> 1.35 / 0.31.
RPA at a 2e13 bulk rate, (0,0) V: FWHM 2.02e13 (EQW 2.23e13).

Findings:
1. **The RPA out-of-plane resonance is not Landau-broadened beyond the bulk rate** at 6 nm: its FWHM equals
   the bulk damping at every bias (1.20-1.30e14 at 1.2e14; 2.02e13 at 2e13), i.e. the discrete confined
   spectrum adds < ~1e13 rad/s; the collective mode carries nearly all the oscillator strength (EQW within
   0-25 % of FWHM). Peak 0.90-1.05e15 rad/s (RPA).
2. **The local graded Drude film is already broader than the RPA at A = 0** (FWHM 1.44-2.43e14): its
   eps_zz(z) spreads plasma frequencies over the density profile (incl. the dilute dead layers), an
   inhomogeneous broadening the nonlocal response removes. Hence **no A >= 0 matches any linewidth, at any
   bias, with either length**: adding damping moves the local model AWAY from the RPA line shape.
3. The off-resonance **absorption at 1200 nm** of the RPA is 1.2-2.0x the local film's at A = 0, and A <= 3
   reaches it only at (-2, -2) V (A = 2.17 film length / 0.51 local length); at the other four biases no A in
   [0, 3] does. For flat-band films A = 1.72 / 1.35 (film length) at 10 / 20 nm: the coefficient is not stable across bias
   or thickness.
4. **Recommendation.** Do not use the out-of-plane rate as a model of quantum confinement in gated few-nm films
   (it is not constant across bias or thickness, and it worsens the line shape); use `optics.quantum_film` there.
   It is shipped OFF (A = 0) as an empirical knob. The `"local"` heuristic is not recommended. The in-plane
   Fuchs-Sondheimer term rests on standard theory and is the part to use, with the classical-validity caveat
   (k_F d ~ 12).

## Part 2: background permittivity eps_b(n)

### Models

- **`BandFillingBackground` (physically based).** Direct-allowed edge eps2(E) = A sqrt((E - Eg)/Eg0)(Eg0/E)^2
  (parabolic joint DOS, constant momentum matrix element: prefactor independent of the gap; Yu and Cardona,
  Fundamentals of Semiconductors, sec. 6.2; same shape as `BursteinMossEdge` with exponent 0.5). Gap
  renormalization Eg(n) = Eg0 - C_bgr n^(1/3) moves the edge (amplitude kept). Band filling (T = 0) blocks
  E < Eg(n) + dE_BM, dE_BM = E_F(n) + hbar^2 k_F^2/(2 m_v), E_F from the SAME conduction band as the carrier
  statistics (`KaneOpticalMass.fermi_energy_J`, a `KaneBand` or a parabolic mass; m_v = None: no valence
  recoil) (Burstein 1954; Moss 1954; Hamberg et al., PRB 30, 3240 (1984); Hamberg and Granqvist, J. Appl. Phys.
  60, R123 (1986)). The real part below the gap is the exact Kramers-Kronig transform, in closed form:
  chi = A (Eg0/Eg)^(3/2) (4/pi) int_{t_F}^inf t^2/((1+t^2)((1+t^2)^2 - x^2)) dt, x = E/Eg, t_F^2 = dE_BM/Eg
  (partial fractions: pi/(2(1+b)(b+c)(1+c)) minus an arctan sum, b^2 = 1-x, c^2 = 1+x). eps_b(n) = eps_inf +
  chi(n) - chi(n_ref): anchored (eps_b(n_ref) = eps_inf exactly), only the density-induced change enters. It has
  an imaginary part only above the gap; **only the real sub-gap part is implemented** (evaluation at/above the
  renormalized gap raises), which is the exact KK partner of the stated eps2, so causal by construction.
  Validity: below the gap, degenerate gas, direct parabolic edge (the Kane band sets the edge position, not the
  shape); the rest of the interband spectrum is density-independent.
- **`TabulatedBackground`** (absolute measured eps_inf(n), linear in log10 n, no extrapolation unless
  `extrapolate="hold"`) and **a callable** fn(n, lambda) -> eps_b (absolute). Scalar models: `eps()` keeps
  working; Im < 0 or non-finite values are refused.

Comparison with published In2O3 material data, honest reading:
- **Optical-gap widening.** With the Kane band of In2O3 (m0 0.18 m_e, C 0.5 /eV: Feneberg et al., PRB 93, 045203
  (2016), as quoted by Stokey et al., arXiv:2104.14611) and a flat valence band, band filling alone widens the
  onset by 0.09 / 0.37 / 0.68 / 0.97 / 1.25 eV at 1e25 / 1e26 / 3e26 / 6e26 / 1e27 m^-3. Hamberg et al. (1984)
  report widening up to ~0.8 eV (Sn-doped In2O3 up to 9 mol% SnO2, onset 3.75 eV; I could read only the abstract,
  and the top density ~1e27 m^-3 is my approximation): band filling alone OVERSHOOTS, the renormalization is
  required. A one-parameter fit C_bgr = 4.48e-10 eV m (0.8 eV at 1e27) gives -0.01 / 0.16 / 0.38 / 0.59 / 0.80 eV.
  This is a fit to one number, not a validation of the shape.
- **eps_inf.** Undoped In2O3: 4.05 +- 0.05 (Stokey et al. 2021), 4.08 +- 0.02 (Feneberg et al. 2016, as quoted);
  degenerate Sn-doped In2O3 films: 3.4-4.0 (T. Gerfin and M. Gratzel, "Optical properties of tin-doped indium
  oxide determined by spectroscopic ellipsometry", J. Appl. Phys. 79, 1722 (1996): ellipsometry of sputtered
  films), and 4.31-5.42 with no
  monotonic trend in N in a 2023 deposition study (PMC10343510: 2.8e20 cm^-3 -> 4.31; 4.5e18-1.3e19 -> 5.30-5.42;
  attributed there to grain size). The model (fitted C_bgr) gives eps_b(1e27) - eps_b(undoped) = -0.018 / -0.046 /
  -0.074 at 1200 nm (-0.017 / -0.042 / -0.068 at 5 um) for edge amplitude A = 1 / 2.5 / 4. **Sign agrees; the
  magnitude covers only the low end (~0.05) of the measured 0.05-0.65 decrease**: either the edge amplitude is
  larger than these values (not checked against a measured In2O3 eps2), or the larger measured changes come from
  other density-correlated factors (microstructure, Sn incorporation, higher interband transitions), which this
  model does not represent. I found no data set that isolates eps_inf(n) at fixed microstructure; for such data
  use `TabulatedBackground`.
- **Zero crossing of Re(eps) at 1200 nm, the generic film** (0.30 m_e parabolic, 1.2e14, eps_inf 4.0 anchored at
  3e26): constant background n_c = 9.3447e26 m^-3; with band filling (Eg0 3.75 eV, no valence recoil): A = 1 /
  2.5 / 4 -> -0.70 / -1.73 / -2.73 % (eps_b(n_c) 3.972 / 3.931 / 3.891); with C_bgr 4.48e-10: -0.56 / -1.38 /
  -2.18 %.

## Integration

- Every optical route takes eps from `assemble_eps` (pipeline per-wavelength and sweep paths); the models live
  behind `OpticalModelEffect` / the Material, so no route-specific code. MEASURED on a 6 nm graded film with
  boundary scattering (tests, generic fixture): layered scalar TMM refuses ("carries a TENSOR eps"); Berreman
  bridge solves it and equals an independent uniaxial transfer-matrix solve of the same slabs to 1.7e-17 in R
  (component swap moves R by 9.1e-6); RCWA bridge equals Berreman to 1.6e-17; PMM bridge refused at the time of
  this package ("gridded TENSOR EpsField ... use the
  RCWA bridge"; since P19 it solves it, 3.9e-14 from Berreman on the generic fixture); FDTD structured path
  refuses tensors (existing); FEM (`assemble_eps_cf`) takes graded tensors
  (existing path, not re-run here). `wp_gamma` (FDTD Drude ADE) refuses both options.
- Background models are scalar and reach every route, including the scalar TMM.
- **Cache.** Drude materials enter `cache._materials_fingerprint` through their repr (eps() needs n). The new
  options appear in the repr when set, so every option change gives a new key (7 variants -> 7 keys, tested);
  a default model keeps its v0.15.1 repr, so existing on-disk keys are unchanged. The eps_by_region values are
  keyed anyway.

## Release-note lines

- New, opt-in: `DrudeOptical(boundary=BoundaryScattering(...))` -- thin-film size effect: exact frequency-dependent
  Fuchs-Sondheimer in-plane response (`fuchs_sondheimer_factor`, `fs_effective_rate`) and an empirical Kreibig-type
  out-of-plane rate A v_F / L (default A = 0; calibration against `optics.quantum_film` in the package note:
  not a model of quantum confinement); the material becomes a uniaxial tensor through the assembler (routes that
  take tensors use it, the others refuse).
- New, opt-in: `DrudeOptical(background=...)` -- density-dependent background permittivity:
  `BandFillingBackground` (band filling from the carrier band + gap renormalization, closed-form KK below the
  gap, anchored at n_ref), `TabulatedBackground`, or a callable.
- `KaneOpticalMass.fermi_energy_J`; `Material.field_response / eps_fields`; `assemble_eps` supplies
  `STACK_AXIS_FIELD` to materials that need it. Defaults unchanged bit for bit.
