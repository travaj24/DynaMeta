## 1. Item 4.4.4: `setup_dg_hard_wall` is a deprecated shim onto `setup_dg_insulator_wall`

**What was wrong.** The unipolar hard wall pinned u = 1e-6 sqrt(N_D), Lambda = -k V_t (a ramped device
parameter) and the CARRIER row n = u^2 at the wall node. The pinned carrier row made the wall node a carrier
sink: its SG current is driven by the pinned Lambda jump, and the converged profile was a spurious
wide-depletion branch. The test pinned that as a characterization (deficit 3-1000 n0 L_q).

**What changed.**
- `setup_dg_hard_wall(device, contact, *, lambda_pin_factor=15.0)` still validates `lambda_pin_factor`
  (ValueError before any warning), then issues `DeprecationWarning(HARD_WALL_DEPRECATION)` (removal in
  v0.16; the text states the measured change) and calls `setup_dg_insulator_wall(device, contact)`.
  `lambda_pin_factor` is ignored; no `wall_lambda_pin*` parameter is created. Name kept (decision D2-c).
- `seed_dg_from_solution(device, region, *, walls=(), u_floor_rel=1e-6)`: the wall-aware seed, by
  delegating to the ONE seed kernel `_seed_dg` (also behind `seed_bipolar_dg`). `walls=()` writes the old
  values bit for bit (gated).
- `set_dg_gamma` keeps its pin co-ramp branch for one release (a device built by an old script may carry
  `wall_lambda_pin_full`); no library code creates the parameter. Delete with the shim in v0.16.
- The old rows survive only as a TEST FIXTURE, `tests/_dg_legacy_wall.py` (the "fails on the old code"
  halves, the negative control, and the cross-version SG-flux fixture, whose 6595bde arrays were recorded
  with those rows).
- `setup_dg_insulator_wall`'s docstring now covers the unipolar wall; `setup_bipolar_dg` refuses (before
  installing anything) a carrier already closed by the 4.4.3 closure.

**Gates** (`tests/test_density_gradient.py`; fixture: uniform unipolar bar, m* = 0.35 m0, frozen flat psi,
nested exponential mesh -- nodes x_k = L (e^{beta k/N} - 1)/(e^beta - 1), last/first spacing 40, so halving
h0 doubles N and nests the meshes. DEVSIM's graded mesh lines gave orders 1.2-1.5 from their segment joints,

## 2. Item 4.4.3: strong-inversion DG through the quasi-equilibrium closure

**What was wrong.** A 1-D MOS capacitor fails beyond 0.70 V of band bending with DG and beyond 0.78-0.80 V
classically. Re-measured here on the P4 fixture (150 nm, 268 nodes, 3e23 p-Si, 0.70 V, equilibrated dense
SVD of DEVSIM's DC Jacobian): sigma_min = 2.6e-16 (sigma_max 3.9). That is the minority-electron near-null
mode.

**What changed (decisions 48/49: the drift-diffusion module).**
`physics_bipolar_dd.setup_quasi_equilibrium_carrier(device, region, carrier, *, contact,
imref_shift=None, allow_imref_split=False)` replaces the carrier's continuity row by an algebraic row:
its quasi-Fermi level equals that of the ohmic `contact`.

    R_n = phi_ref - [(psi + D_c + Lambda_n) - V_t (ln(n/N_c) + Delta(n/N_c) + ln(N_c/n_i))]

Holes are mirrored. Details:
- The FD activity ln u + Delta(u) is `physics_heterojunction.setup_log_activity`: closed form, arctan by
  half-angle reductions. Plan 5.6: reused, not re-implemented. D_c / D_v are the BGN quasi-fields when
  present; Lambda is the DG quantum potential when the carrier carries DG.
- The Jacobian is written out: dR/dc = V_t g(u)/c (the activity's exact derivative), dR/dpsi = -/+1,
  dR/dLambda = -1.
- phi_ref = `<contact>_bias` plus the contact node's own fixed imref offset. The offset is READ from the
  contact node at setup, so it includes whatever the contact pins (the CELEC/CHOLE floors, FD, BGN). On the
  Boltzmann p-type body it is exactly 0.0 (measured).
- The equation keeps its name, so contacts, Gummel freezing and the noise refusal keep working.
- `delete_quasi_equilibrium_carrier` restores the recorded continuity equation call for call.
  `quasi_equilibrium_carriers` lists the closed carriers.
- Refusals:
  - another ohmic contact pinning the carrier (unless `allow_imref_split=True`);
  - a circuit-driven reference contact;
  - a second closure;
  - `setup_bipolar_dg` on a closed carrier. It now refuses BEFORE installing anything. The first version
    left half-installed DG rows, which the 4.4.2 hook then caught as an exactly singular matrix (measured).
- Allowed: ssac (the low-frequency C-V). Refused: `noise_analysis`, by its existing edge-model check.
- Defaults: nothing changes unless it is called. The `_bipolar_calllog` digest is unchanged (gated).

**Findings that change the plan's picture (measured).**
1. *The hole block is the second near-null mode.* Closing only the electrons gives sigma_min = 5.1e-8.
   diagnose_singular reports "mode in Holes, x in [15, 87] nm": the depleted holes. With BOTH carriers
   closed, sigma_min = 1.46e-4 and cond = 1.5e4. Closing both is exact for the DC MOS-C, which is in
   equilibrium for both carriers. These are P4's 1.45e-4 / 1.4e4, so P4's "closure" number was in effect
   the both-closed system.
2. *DG with a hole quantum potential needs the hole closure too.* With only the electrons closed, the psi
   ramp with the hole DG active fails at 0.5 V. Installing the DG at 1.3 V after a classical ramp overflows
   the hole DG flux. With both closed, every step takes <= 10 iterations.
3. *Quasi-Fermi variables do not help*: sigma_min x 1.7 (P4 measured 0.65x). Pinned as a gate.
4. *The full model depends on tau.* At tau = 1e-5 s the full classical model already fails on the ramp to
   0.7 V in extended precision; it also fails at the plan's tau = 1e3 s. The weaker the SRH tie, the weaker
   the inversion layer's coupling to the body.
5. *The full DG model's own psi ramp fails*: "Matrix solve failed" on the way to 0.6 V, with or without the
   hole DG. The D2d GATE E reached 0.6 V only by installing the DG after a classical ramp. G1-2b uses that
   order (`mos_qe(dg_at_target=True)`).
6. *The plan's G1-1 meshes cannot resolve 1.3 V classical Boltzmann.* There the layer is l = V_t/E_s =
   2e-12 m thick (n_s = 2.3e30 m^-3): a numerics regime, not physics. G1-1 uses nested meshes with 3000 /
   6000 / 12000 segments (first spacing 6.9e-4 .. 1.7e-4 nm).

**The SP inversion oracle (G1-4): new and independent.** It is `sp_inversion` in the validation module.
- Electrons: subbands of `SchrodingerPoisson1D.solve_schrodinger` in a 30 nm box at the wall, filled with
  the 2-D Fermi-Dirac sheet density at the body Fermi level; classical F_1/2 beyond the box. It uses the
  DG's own isotropic model: m_n = 0.26 m0, g_v = 6.
- Holes: classical, the exact F_1/2 series.
- Poisson: its own damped Trellakis predictor-corrector Newton, not the solver's.
- Convergence: h 0.05 -> 0.025 nm moves N_inv and the centroid by 1e-4. A 45 nm box with a 250 nm body
  changes nothing.
- Self-checks: the free-gas deficit is 0.626609 (exact 0.626657). In the classical limit (quantization mass
  x 4e4, N_c kept) it matches the FD first integral to -3.1e-7 / -2.4e-6 at 0.6 / 0.9 V. In strong
  inversion the classical limit cannot be reached on a practical mesh (the de Broglie length would have to
  be resolved inside a 0.1 nm layer), so it is not claimed there.

**Anisotropic Si (decision 51: reported, not gated).** With two-fold (m_z = 0.916, m_d = 0.19) plus
four-fold (m_z = 0.19, m_d = 0.417) valleys at 1.2 V, the SP gives N_inv +41 % and the centroid -25 %
relative to the isotropic-model SP. The isotropic model the DG represents (m = 0.26 m0, g_v = 6) is
therefore a poor stand-in for a real Si inversion layer at fixed surface potential. A physical Si claim
needs the anisotropic masses, or a DG calibrated to them.

## Release-note lines

- `physics_density_gradient.setup_dg_hard_wall` is deprecated (removal in v0.16): it now installs the
  zero-flux `setup_dg_insulator_wall`. The old rows were a carrier sink and converged to a wrong
  wide-depletion profile (23-28 n0 L_q); the insulator wall reproduces the closed-form dark space
  (1.187207 n0 L_q) to 2e-5 and conserves carriers. `seed_dg_from_solution(..., walls=(contact,))` seeds
  next to the wall.
- Strong inversion (opt-in): `physics_bipolar_dd.setup_quasi_equilibrium_carrier` replaces a carrier's
  continuity row by "its quasi-Fermi level equals the body contact's". It is exact for a DC MOS capacitor
  and the low-frequency C-V; ssac is allowed, noise is refused, and multi-contact regions need
  `allow_imref_split=True`. The classical and the density-gradient MOS-C now converge through 1.3 V of
  band bending in <= 10 double-precision Newton iterations per 50 mV step (they failed at 0.78 / 0.70 V).
- Against a new Schrodinger-Poisson inversion oracle, DG at GAMMA_WALL gives the inversion charge within
  +11 % (registered target 10 %: missed, a documented model result), the centroid within 9 %, the
  threshold shift within 6 mV and the quasi-static C-V within 3 %. The classical error is 250-730 %.
