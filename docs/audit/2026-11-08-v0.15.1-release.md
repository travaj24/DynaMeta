# DynaMeta v0.15.1 -- release note (2026-11-08)

On top of v0.15.0 (`2026-11-06-v0.15.0-release.md`). One addition. No default changes, and no
existing result moves.

## optics.soa: opt-in C-band (1550 nm) SOA material

(`2026-11-08-v0.15-soa-cband.md`; user decision 2026-11-08)

- **`BulkGainParams.c_band()`** is a bulk SOA whose active region is InGaAsP lattice-matched to InP:
  Ga0.440 In0.560 As0.937 P0.063.
  - **Source:** Vurgaftman, Meyer & Ram-Mohan, J. Appl. Phys. 89, 5815 (2001), Eq. (5.4) and
    Sec. V.B.1. The alloy is built from the InP law and the Ga0.47In0.53As law with a bowing of
    0.13 eV. Its temperature dependence comes from the binary Varshni laws, as the paper recommends.
  - **Gap:** 0.768 eV at 300 K.
  - **Gain peak (measured):** 1549.7 nm at the reference drive (130 mA, 600 um, 298 K).
  - **Device gain:** 26.2 dB at alpha_i = 3000 /m, or 27.5 dB at 2500 /m.
  - **Drift:** 0.68 nm/K from the gap alone, 1.00 nm/K at fixed current.
  - The composition was solved to put the peak at 1550 nm and then rounded to 0.001. The peak still
    moves with drive current and temperature.
  - Pass other fields as overrides, e.g. `BulkGainParams.c_band(T_K=320.0)`.
- **The default `BulkGainParams()` is unchanged, bit for bit.** It is still the In0.53Ga0.47As L-band
  device, with its peak at 1612.5 nm (1182-value hash census, v0.15.0 against v0.15.1).
- **This replaces the v0.14.0 advice** for C-band work, which was to pass
  `Eg0_eV=0.816, varshni_alpha_eV_K=2.9e-4, varshni_beta_K=193.0`. That triple has no source. Its gap
  slope is 30 % low, so its drift is 0.48 nm/K gap-only. Its peak is at 1560.4 nm. It still works as a
  device-effective override.
- **New gap-law names:**
  - `ingaasp_lm_gap_law(z)` for any InP-matched GaInAsP composition (z is the As fraction);
  - the material key `"InGaAsP_LM_C"`;
  - `ingaasp_lattice_mismatch`, `LATTICE_CONSTANT_300K_A`, `LATTICE_MATCH_RTOL`, `INGAASP_LM_C_Z`,
    `INGAASP_LM_BOWING_EV` in `dynameta.materials.band_temperature`.

  `ingaasp_lm_gap_law` is also exported from `dynameta.materials` and `dynameta.optics.soa`.
- **`varshni_eg_ev` accepts lattice-matched quaternaries** (and so does everything built on it:
  `BandTemperature`, `qd_params_at_temperature`, `gain_peak_drift_nm_per_K`). One end point of the
  `(A, B, z, C)` spec may now be a ternary name. Such an end point must be lattice matched to the
  other one by Vegard's law, within 1e-3. A mismatched pair is refused with the mismatch quoted, as
  before.
- **Limits:**
  - Only the gap is specific to InGaAsP. The masses and recombination constants are the module's
    generic defaults.
  - No measured bulk-InGaAsP SOA drift was available to compare against. Measured InP-based 1550 nm
    quantum-dot and quantum-dash SOAs drift 0.77-0.78 nm/K. See the audit note.
