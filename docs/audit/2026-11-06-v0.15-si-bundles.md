## Finding: the legacy FD statistics break detailed balance on degenerate layers

`fd_enhancement=True` makes the transport FD (zero current <=> ln n + Delta(n/N_c) - psi_n/V_t = const),
but every recombination term and the ohmic contacts used the **Boltzmann product** n p = n_ie^2. The
transport's own equilibrium is n p = n_ie^2 exp(-Delta(n/N_c) - Delta(p/N_v)) -- exactly Yan & Cuevas
Eq. (1). So next to a degenerate layer the device carries a current at zero bias.

MEASURED (R27-c long diode, n+ / p 1e17, no BGN, both contacts at 0 V, hole current 20 nm into the
emitter, in units of J0p = q n_i^2 D_p/(N_D L_p)):

| N_D (cm^-3) | legacy (`statistics=None`) | exp(-Delta_fit) - 1 | `"fermi_dirac"` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1e19 (eta -0.89) | -0.1198 | -0.1219 | +1.59e-6 |
| 1e20 (eta 2.6) | -0.6987 | -0.7081 | +4.86e-7 |

The `"fermi_dirac"` residual is the O(h^2) discretisation of the edge-averaged g: x4.03 / x4.08 smaller
at h/2, x4.03 / x4.00 larger at 2h. **Correction (2026-11-06, v0.15 close-out):** superseded -- the FD mode's transport now takes the
degeneracy as a quasi-field, so this residual is round-off: 2.7e-12 / 4.4e-13 J0p
(docs/audit/2026-11-06-v0.15-fermi-dirac-mode-complete.md section 5). The legacy FORWARD J0 is already the FD one (0.8789 of the
Boltzmann J0 at 1e19, exp(-Delta) = 0.8781): the transport is FD, only the equilibrium is not.

**What I did:** fixed it opt-in, under `statistics="fermi_dirac"`; the default is unchanged (decision
28). In that mode:
- `n_ie` becomes a solution-dependent node model, n_i exp(dEg/2kT) sqrt(gamma_n gamma_p), with
  gamma = (c/N) exp(-L(c/N)) = exp(-Delta). L is the existing closed-form log-activity of
  `physics_heterojunction.setup_log_activity` (plan 5.6: reused, not re-implemented).
- Derivative models `n_ie:Electrons` / `n_ie:Holes` are written in closed form, without cancellation:
  d ln gamma/dc = -(GA + GC s)/(N (1 + GB s + GD s^2)). DEVSIM's `diff()` of a model reference uses
  `<model>:<var>`; I confirmed this with a probe.
- Every string referencing `n_ie` then vanishes where the transport is in equilibrium: SRH, Auger,
  radiative, neutral bulk traps, and the noise pair rates.
- The contacts and the seed pin the FD charge-neutral root `_neutral_root_fd`:
  n0 p0 = n_ie0^2 gamma(n0) gamma(p0), solved with `fd_activity_inverse` (Newton on the convex activity).
- **Refused (`NotImplementedError`) rather than silently inexact:** BTBT, CHARGED bulk trap levels and
  interface traps (their analytic Jacobian / equilibrium occupancy assume a fixed n_ie).
- **Documented approximation:** SRH's n1, p1 scale by sqrt(gamma_n gamma_p) (the shared n_ie
  substitution), not the gamma_n n1, gamma_p p1 of commercial TCAD. They differ only in the denominator
  and only where n1 ~ n on a degenerate side.
- **Correction (2026-11-06, v0.15 close-out):** both bullets above are superseded by
  docs/audit/2026-11-06-v0.15-fermi-dirac-mode-complete.md: BTBT (local, tuples, nonlocal), charged bulk
  levels, interface traps and kinetic traps are IMPLEMENTED under `"fermi_dirac"` with exact Jacobians (the
  refusals are gone), and every SRH channel now uses the exact FD emission densities gamma_n n1, gamma_p p1
  (so a charged level has a Fermi-function equilibrium occupancy at any capture ratio).

**Open decision for the user:** make `"fermi_dirac"` the default (it moves released FD results on
degenerate layers; the size is the table above).
**Correction (2026-11-06, v0.15 close-out):** the per-mechanism size of the move (BTBT, traps, interface traps, thermionic FDT) is in
docs/audit/2026-11-06-v0.15-fermi-dirac-mode-complete.md section 1; the decision is still open (v0.15.0
release note).

## Accuracy note: the rational FD fit (docstring corrected, model unchanged)

MEASURED against scipy quadrature of F_1/2 (relative error of Delta, the code's minus the exact):

| eta | -4 | -3 | -2 | -1 | 0 | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 7 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| % | -0.21 | +1.03 | +2.30 | +3.35 | +3.86 | +3.88 | +3.76 | +3.27 | +2.68 | +2.15 | +1.72 | +1.16 | +0.99 | +0.78 |

So the error is above 1 % from eta ~ -3 to ~ 8 (the old text said "< 1 % where degeneracy matters").
This matches the temperature agent's +3.27 / +1.72 / +0.78 % at eta = 2 / 5 / 10. In absolute terms:
4.2e-3 at Si:P 1e19 cm^-3 (eta = -0.93) and 3.6e-2 at 1e20 (eta = 2.43). Consequence for the house
bundle: n0 p0 is **0.42 % low at 1e19 and 3.5 % low at 1e20** against Eq. (1), inside the paper's own
+/-0.003 meV on A (7 %). Every gate in that range budgets the fit error explicitly and **attributes** the
miss: miss / exp(Delta_exact - Delta_fit) - 1 must vanish to round-off.

**G4 detail, the paper's quoted maximum (finding).** The paper says the FD p0 n0 peaks "at 9e19 cm^-3".
The exact Eq. (1) with its own constants peaks at **7.78e19** (scipy). The code's fit peaks at 7.57e19.
The maximum is flat: p0 n0(9e19) is 0.78 % below the peak, and the oracle at the code's peak is 0.03 %
below it. So "9e19" read off the log plot is consistent with the equation only through that plateau. A
peak at 9e19 itself would need N_c ~ 3.2e19 (inferred).

Likewise the paper's "FD gives a smaller p0 n0 above ~1e20": Eq. (1) crosses the Boltzmann set at
3.96e19 and is 30 % lower at 1e20 (scipy). Both are noted, not acted on.

   refuses its p nodes above 1e14 cm^-3. Gate G3 states `outside_basis="warn"` for its unnarrowed p base
   and asserts the warning. When the constants arrive, a region needs an n-and-p BGN model (one
   `BGNModel` per region today).
2. **`"fermi_dirac"` as the default?** It fixes a released thermodynamic defect (table above). User
   decision.
3. **chi 4.05 eV and eps 11.7** are the common tabulated values; the primary was not re-read.

5. **Not combined with `"fermi_dirac"`:** BTBT, charged traps, interface traps (refused); density
   gradient (not refused, not verified).
   **Correction (2026-11-06, v0.15 close-out):** BTBT, charged traps, interface traps and kinetic traps are now supported (see above);

## Release-note lines

- **Si bundles (new, opt-in):** `dynameta.materials.semiconductor_presets.silicon_transport(...)` builds a
  bipolar Si material from the house pairing: Yan & Cuevas 2013 real bandgap narrowing with Fermi-Dirac
  statistics, n_i = 9.65e9 cm^-3, N_c = 2.86e19, N_v = 3.1e19 cm^-3. It is n-type only until the 2014
  p-type constants are available, and mobilities are required.
- **Pairing check:** a paired BGN set now refuses a region whose n_i, N_c/N_v, statistics or temperature
  differ (`BGNPairingError`, with the gap equivalent of the n_i mismatch). Override with
  `BipolarOptions(allow_unpaired_bgn=True)`. Legacy presets are unchanged and unchecked.
- **Carrier statistics switch:** `TransportModel.carrier_statistics` = `None` (unchanged) | `"boltzmann"`
  | `"fermi_dirac"`. The new `"fermi_dirac"` makes recombination and contacts consistent with the FD
  transport. The default statistics carry a zero-bias current next to degenerate layers (-0.12 J0 at
  1e19 cm^-3 Si), which `"fermi_dirac"` removes.
