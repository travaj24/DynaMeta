# v0.15.1: why a density-gradient Newton iteration cost 7-32x what it should (UMFPACK 5.1's 2-by-2 strategy) (2026-11-10)

Branch `wip/v0151_dgperf` from `fe65c9b`. The user asked for this explicitly, after the valley-resolved-DG note
(`2026-11-10-v0.15.1-valley-resolved-dg.md`, section 6) found the EXISTING isotropic density-gradient (DG) model
1.2-10x slower per Newton iteration than the new valley-resolved one, despite fewer unknowns, and guessed "the
direct solver's pivoting / fill" without looking. "Measured" = run on this box (Windows, py3.14, DEVSIM 2.11.2,
scipy's OpenBLAS) against this worktree, unless it says WSL (the Linux CI proxy: Ubuntu 24.04, the CI's apt
OpenBLAS, DEVSIM 2.11.2). "Inferred" says so. Scratch scripts: the session scratchpad `dgperf/` (named below).

**Correction (2026-11-12, branch `wip/v0151_guard2`; section 12).** The first guard (v1, `9e1c427` .. `4cd3af7`)
redid EVERY 2-by-2 choice with the symmetric strategy. A solver benchmark on `validation/bipolar_diode_2d.py`
(18,357 nodes, no DG) showed that AUTO also picks 2-by-2 there (v1 replaced 23 / 23 symbolic steps) and that 2-by-2
does not fill in there: factor time 48 s with v1 against 42 s without (quiet reruns here: within the run-to-run
spread, 42-49 s for every variant; section 12.3), max componentwise backward error 9.6e-8 with v1 against 9.0e-11
without, identical Newton iteration counts (114) and results (1.4e-15). The claim below that the
symmetric solves are "at least as accurate" was measured only on DG matrices where 2-by-2 fills in, and is false in
general. The guard now (v2) keeps 2-by-2 unless its symbolic upper bound on nz(L+U) exceeds the symmetric
strategy's by more than 1.15x (measured: <= 1.070 wherever 2-by-2 factorized as sparsely as symmetric, >= 1.291
wherever it filled in 4-45x). The DG results of sections 1-6 are unchanged by v2 (the five fixtures that filled in are
still replaced); the three that did not are back on 2-by-2, bit for bit the pre-v0.15.1 factorization.

## Summary

- **Root cause: the linear solver's strategy choice, not the model.** DEVSIM 2.11.2 factorizes every Newton step
  with the UMFPACK **5.1.0 (May 2007)** it bundles (`devsim/umfpack/`, on Windows and on Linux), with UMFPACK's
  default control, strategy AUTO. For every DG + quasi-equilibrium-closure Jacobian measured here (8 fixtures, every
  bias tested, flat band included), 5.1's AUTO picks its **"symmetric 2-by-2" strategy**. Its numeric
  factorization then takes many more off-diagonal pivots than the plain symmetric strategy on the SAME ordering,
  and fills in: 2-D, electron + hole DG, 7980 unknowns, 32577 nonzeros: **nz(L+U) 4,992,974 against 116,018
  and 902 ms against 6 ms** for the numeric factorization, 97 % of the 926 ms per iteration (Windows; the valley
  note had 947). UMFPACK itself **disabled the 2-by-2 strategy in 5.4.0 (May 2009)**.
- **The isotropic and the valley-resolved model have the same disease.** Both get 2-by-2 on all 8 fixtures; how
  badly it fills depends on the pivot sequence (isotropic 17-43x the symmetric fill, valley 1.0-4.2x). The
  "valley model is faster" result was this luck, not a property of either model.
- **Trigger (bisection):** DG + closure. Without the DG (classical + closure) AUTO picks symmetric; the DG without
  the closure (full continuity rows) gets unsymmetric. Fermi-Dirac vs Boltzmann, the quantum SRH term, gamma and
  the bias do not matter (fill 18x already at flat band).
- **Fix:** `dynameta.carriers.devsim_env.umfpack_2by2_guard()` (installed by `import_devsim()`): when AUTO's
  symbolic step picks 2-by-2, the symbolic step is redone with UMFPACK's SYMMETRIC strategy; every other matrix is
  factorized exactly as before. Opt-out for A/B: `DYNAMETA_UMFPACK_2BY2=keep`. (v1. Since v2, section 12: redone
  only when 2-by-2's symbolic nz(L+U) bound exceeds the symmetric one's by more than 1.15x; otherwise kept.)
- **Speed:** the three slow isotropic fixtures 7.4-28x cheaper per Newton iteration on Windows (122 -> 13, 207 ->
  28, 926 -> 33 ms) and 16-32x in WSL; whole ramps 6.6-26x (Windows: 26.8 -> 2.8, 19.8 -> 3.0, 102.9 -> 3.9 s) and
  14-38x (WSL). The valley-resolved model with the hole DG 2.1-8.6x per iteration. The fixtures that did not fill
  in (1-D electron-only both models, 2-D electron-only valley) are unchanged per ramp within the run-to-run spread;
  their one-iteration probe pays one extra symbolic step per iteration (+0.6 .. +6 ms, section 6).
- **Results:** not bit-identical, at the round-off floor: identical Newton iteration counts in all 8 ramps; converged
  densities differ by <= 6.5e-15 (relative), potentials by <= 1.1e-16 V, quantum potentials by <= 2.1e-16 V --
  smaller than the same old code's Windows-vs-WSL difference (up to 9.6e-15 / 2.2e-16 V). Where 2-by-2 fills in,
  the symmetric solves are more accurate (componentwise backward error <= 1.6e-14 against up to 1.3e-12 on the old
  path). CORRECTED (section 12): where 2-by-2 does NOT fill in, it is usually the more accurate one (2-D diode
  9.0e-11 against 5.0e-8; 1-D electron DG Newton steps 9e-14 against up to 3.3e-5), which is why v2 keeps it there.
- **Gates:** a structural cost gate (max LU fill ratio <= 4 and no 2-by-2 on the DG + closure Jacobian: 2.0 new,
  80.5 old -- fails on `fe65c9b`) and three guard unit/child gates (all fail on `fe65c9b`). No timing asserts.

## Terms (plain language)

- **Newton iteration:** one linearized step of the nonlinear device solve: DEVSIM ASSEMBLES the Jacobian matrix J
  and the residual (C++ model evaluation), then SOLVES J dx = -r with a sparse direct solver.
- **Sparse direct solver / LU factorization:** writes J = L U (lower and upper triangular factors, after row and
  column permutations) and then solves two triangular systems. Cost is driven by **fill-in**: the nonzeros of L
  and U that are not in J. "Fill ratio" below = (nz(L) + nz(U)) / nz(J).
- **Symbolic / numeric factorization:** UMFPACK first chooses an ordering from the pattern (symbolic step), then
  computes L and U with the actual numbers (numeric step), choosing pivots as it goes.
- **Pivot / off-diagonal pivot:** the entry used to eliminate a column. UMFPACK's symmetric strategies prefer the
  diagonal entry; if that entry is too small relative to its column, it takes another row (an off-diagonal pivot),
  which changes the pattern of what follows and can create fill.
- **UMFPACK strategy:** AUTO lets UMFPACK pick. UNSYMMETRIC = column ordering (COLAMD) of J, free row pivoting.
  SYMMETRIC = minimum-degree ordering (AMD) of J + J^T, diagonal pivots preferred. 2-BY-2 (UMFPACK 4.1-5.3 only) =
  first a row permutation that tries to move large entries onto the diagonal, then the symmetric strategy.
- **Fixtures:** the bipolar DG MOS capacitor of `validation/bipolar_density_gradient.py mos_qe`: p-Si 3e23 m^-3,
  Fermi-Dirac, GAMMA_WALL, insulator wall, quasi-equilibrium closure, surface potential ramped 0 -> 0.6 -> 1.2 V
  (1-D: 569 nodes, first spacing 0.05 nm; 2-D: the nested x mesh with first spacing 0.1 nm x 4 lateral lines, 1140
  nodes), electron DG or electron + hole DG,
  isotropic (`m_eff_n_kg`) or valley-resolved (`electron_valleys=SI100_ELECTRON_VALLEYS`). The same 8 fixtures as
  the valley note's table.
- **Backward error:** how much J and the right-hand side would have to change for the computed solution to be
  exact; the standard accuracy measure of a linear solve ("componentwise": entry by entry, relative).

What UMFPACK's report says for every one of the 8: "strategy used: symmetric 2-by-2", "ordering used: amd on
A+A'", and under "2-by-2 pivoting to place large entries on diagonal" the "# unmatched" equals the "# of small
diagonal entries" (e.g. 2264 / 2264 for 2-D eh iso): the 2-by-2 row permutation found NOTHING to swap, so the
pattern, its symmetry (0.36-0.52) and the AMD ordering statistics are identical to the symmetric strategy's
(both report "est. nz in L+U 183520" for 2-D eh iso). The difference arises in the NUMERIC step: the 2-by-2 run
takes 1.0-1.7x as many off-diagonal pivots, and the extra ones widen the frontal matrices (2-D eh iso: largest
front 2,240,277 entries). Why 5.1 pivots differently under 2-by-2 with an identity row permutation is inside
UMFPACK 5.1 and was not investigated further (its 5.3.0 ChangeLog lists a "bug fix for 2-by-2 strategy (diagonal
map) for structurally singular matrices"; these matrices are not structurally singular, so that is NOT claimed
as the cause).

UMFPACK history (its ChangeLog, read 2026-11-10 from the SuiteSparse repository): 4.1 (2003) added the AUTO /
unsymmetric / symmetric / 2-by-2 strategies; **5.4.0 (May 20, 2009): "disabled 2-by-2 strategy. It conflicts
with how structurally singular matrices are handled."** The AUTO rule of the later 5.x (SuiteSparse 5.4.0's
`umfpack_qsymbolic.c`): symmetric if the pattern symmetry >= 0.5 and >= 90 % of the diagonal is nonzero, else
unsymmetric; UMFPACK 6 lowered the symmetry threshold to 0.3 (`UMFPACK_DEFAULT_STRATEGY_THRESH_SYM`, "was 0.5").
DEVSIM 2.11.2 ships 5.1.0 (May 31, 2007), older than both changes.

Reading it: the pathology needs the DG AND the closure. The closure row of a carrier c is phi_ref - (psi -/+
Lambda_c) +/- V_t A(c/N): its own diagonal dR/dc = V_t A'(c)/c is of order V_t/c (25 mV over a density in m^-3, i.e.
1e-27 .. 1e-6 here) next to the unit entries for psi and Lambda. UMFPACK's count of "small diagonal entries" in the
isotropic fixtures is exactly one per closed DG carrier per non-contact node (567 = 569 - 2 in 1-D e, 1134 in 1-D
eh, 1132 = 1140 - 8 in 2-D e, 2264 in 2-D eh); that these ARE the closure rows is inferred from that count and the
derivative sizes, not read off UMFPACK. The valley fixtures have more (1135, 1702, 2268, 3400: their extra rows add
some). The pattern is only partly symmetric (0.36-0.52, UMFPACK's report), and that combination sends 5.1's AUTO to
2-by-2. Without the DG, AUTO picks symmetric; without the closure (continuity rows instead of the closure rows),
AUTO picks unsymmetric (both measured; the reasons -- a more symmetric pattern, no tiny diagonals -- are inferred). The physics options (FD, quantum SRH, gamma) and the bias do not matter. Not in the
table: the hole DG with the holes NOT closed did not converge to 1.2 V (2-D: "Convergence failure!" at 40
iterations; 1-D: a DEVSIM FATAL in the edge model HJ_Holes_elog) -- the full hole DD row in strong inversion, the
documented reason the closure exists.

Isotropic against valley-resolved: the SAME strategy and the same kind of small diagonals; the valley model's
extra rows change the pivot sequence, and the 2-by-2 cascade happened to stay small in the electron-only cases
(within 0.1 % of the symmetric nz(L+U)) and to be 4-10x milder than the isotropic one with the hole DG. Nothing
structural in either model (QLambda a solution variable vs a node model) decides it, and both are cured by the
same change (section 4).

## 4. The fix: keep UMFPACK 5.1 off the 2-by-2 strategy

`dynameta/carriers/devsim_env.py`, `umfpack_2by2_guard()`, called by `import_devsim()` (DynaMeta's one devsim
import home, so every DynaMeta solve path has it). It wraps `devsim.umfpack.umfpack_loader.umf_control.symbolic`,
the one call through which every symbolic step of DEVSIM's UMFPACK shim goes (`umfshim.dsobject.factor`):

1. run DEVSIM's symbolic step exactly as before (Control unchanged, strategy AUTO);
2. if UMFPACK reports Info[UMFPACK_STRATEGY_USED] == 2 (2-by-2) and the caller had left the strategy at AUTO,
   drop that Symbolic object and redo the symbolic step with Control[UMFPACK_STRATEGY] = SYMMETRIC (restored to
   AUTO afterwards);
3. everything else (numeric factorization, solve, DEVSIM's symbolic reuse inside a Newton solve) is DEVSIM's own
   code, untouched.

Choices and why:

- **Symmetric, not unsymmetric, as the replacement.** On these matrices the 2-by-2 strategy IS the symmetric
  strategy plus a row pre-permutation that was the identity every time; replacing it by symmetric keeps the same
  ordering and the same diagonal preference, and it is what UMFPACK 6's own AUTO would choose here (symmetry >=
  0.3). It is also the more accurate of the two on the 2-D matrices (section 5). Both are equally fast here
  (section 2).
- **v2 (2026-11-12): only the 2-by-2 choices that fill in are touched** (section 12). The rest of this list
  describes v1, which replaced every 2-by-2 choice.
- **Only the 2-by-2 choice is touched.** A matrix for which 5.1's AUTO picks symmetric or unsymmetric is factorized
  bit for bit as before (gated, `test_umfpack_2by2_guard_redoes_only_the_2by2_choice`). In particular the
  classical drift-diffusion devices whose AUTO choice is symmetric / unsymmetric, ssac matrices of that kind, and
  the DG without the closure are unchanged.
- **Survives `ds.reset_devsim()`**, which re-registers umfshim's callback (the guard patches the loader class, which
  the callback uses; gated in a child process).
- **Not installed** when DEVSIM did not load its UMFPACK shim (a pip `mkl` makes MKL-PARDISO the direct solver;
  not measured here, see "Not covered") or when the loader lacks the expected names (then the reason says so).
  `DYNAMETA_UMFPACK_2BY2=keep` restores the old factorization for A/B comparisons (read once per process).
- **Cost of the check:** when AUTO picks 2-by-2 the symbolic step runs twice. DEVSIM reuses the symbolic
  factorization for the later iterations of one `ds.solve`, so in a ramp that is one extra symbolic step per bias
  step (0.6-6 ms here). The one-iteration probe below pays it EVERY iteration, which is why its symbolic column
  doubles; see the ramp columns for the realistic effect.

Why not something else (considered, not done): calling UMFPACK with a newer version (DEVSIM ships 5.1 inside its
wheel; DynaMeta cannot swap it); a custom `solver_callback` (would be wiped by `ds.reset_devsim()` and duplicates
the shim); caching AUTO's decision per sparsity pattern to skip the first symbolic step (5.1's AUTO choice depends
on the VALUES as well -- measured on small test matrices -- so a cache would make a factorization depend on what
was solved before); rewriting the closure row to have a larger diagonal (changes the equations, and the trigger
is the solver's strategy, not the model).

## 9. For the record: the DEVSIM side (NOT reported upstream; the user holds any DEVSIM report)

- **What:** DEVSIM 2.11.2's pip wheels (Windows and Linux, measured both) load "UMFPACK 5.1 as direct solver" from
  `devsim/umfpack/` ("UMFPACK V5.1.0 (May 31, 2007)" in its report) through the Python shim `umfshim.py`, which
  never sets `Control[UMFPACK_STRATEGY]`, so every factorization runs 5.1's AUTO, which can select the 2-by-2
  strategy that UMFPACK disabled in 5.4.0 (2009). On DynaMeta's DG + quasi-equilibrium Jacobians that choice costs
  17-43x the fill and 36-145x the numeric time of the symmetric strategy, and 10-430x the backward error.
- **Minimal reproduction** (needs only devsim 2.11.2, numpy, scipy, and a Jacobian saved as a scipy sparse matrix,
  e.g. `scipy.sparse.save_npz("J.npz", J)` of the 1-D electron + hole DG MOS of `test_dg_closure_jacobian_
  factorizes_without_fill_in`, taken with `ds.get_matrix_and_rhs(format="csc")`):

```python
import array
import devsim
import scipy.sparse as sp
from devsim.umfpack import umfpack_loader as L

info = devsim.get_parameter(name="info")
L.global_data_instance.initialize_umfpack(info["math_libraries"], info["blas_symbol_prefix"],
                                          info["blas_symbol_suffix"])
A = sp.load_npz("J.npz").tocsc()
for strategy in (0, 3):                  # 0 = AUTO (what devsim uses), 3 = SYMMETRIC
    uc = L.umf_control(L.global_data_instance, "real")
    uc.Control[5] = strategy             # Control[UMFPACK_STRATEGY]
    M = L.matrix(uc=uc, Ap=array.array("i", A.indptr.astype("int32")),
                 Ai=array.array("i", A.indices.astype("int32")), Ax=array.array("d", A.data))
    N = uc.numeric(matrix=M, Symbolic=uc.symbolic(matrix=M))
    print("strategy used", uc.Info[18], "nz(L+U)", uc.Info[43] + uc.Info[44], "off-diagonal pivots", uc.Info[76])
```

  Measured (`dgperf/mkJ.py` saved the Jacobian of that fixture after its closure solve at flat band: 3983 unknowns,
  13063 nonzeros; the script above then ran under `python -I` with devsim alone, no DynaMeta import): AUTO ->
  "strategy used 2.0 nz(L+U) 942618.0 off-diagonal pivots 1919.0" (72x the nonzeros of J); SYMMETRIC ->
  "strategy used 3.0 nz(L+U) 26112.0 off-diagonal pivots 1136.0" (2.0x).
- **Possible upstream remedies (not proposed anywhere):** set `Control[UMFPACK_STRATEGY]` away from 2-by-2 in
  `umfshim` (or expose the Control array), or ship a UMFPACK >= 5.4.
- **Checked and NOT a bug:** `umfpack_loader.di_symbolic` frees its Symbolic object (a first reading suggested a
  second, empty `__del__`; that one belongs to the matrix class). 300 factorizations through `umfshim.dsobject` of
  the 2-D valley Jacobian grew private memory by 0.9 MB after the first (`dgperf/leak.py`): no leak.

## 12. v2 (2026-11-12): keep 2-by-2 where it does not fill in (measured)

Branch `wip/v0151_guard2` from `4cd3af7`. Scratch scripts: the session scratchpad `guard2/` (named below).

**What prompted it.** A solver benchmark (2026-11-12, Windows) ran `validation/bipolar_diode_2d.py` (18,357 nodes,
54,027 unknowns; bipolar drift-diffusion + SRH, no DG, no closure) with and without the v1 guard. AUTO picks 2-by-2
there for every symbolic step, so v1 replaced 23 / 23 of them, yet 2-by-2 does not fill in on that device. v1 cost
factor time 48 s against 42 s unguarded (means of 3 runs; run-to-run spread 35-51 s) and raised the max
componentwise backward error of the Newton solves from 9.0e-11 to 9.6e-8; Newton counts (114) and results
(1.4e-15) were identical. The "at least as accurate" claim of section 5 had been measured only on the eight DG
matrices, where 2-by-2 fills in.

### 12.1 The rule

When AUTO's symbolic step picks 2-by-2, the guard also runs the symbolic step with the SYMMETRIC strategy (as v1
did) and compares UMFPACK's two symbolic upper bounds on the factor size, Info[UMFPACK_LNZ_ESTIMATE] +
Info[UMFPACK_UNZ_ESTIMATE] (Info[23] + Info[24] in 5.1's layout). It keeps AUTO's 2-by-2 Symbolic object (and
restores the Info array, so DEVSIM's call sees exactly its own symbolic step) unless

    bound(2-by-2) > 1.15 x bound(symmetric),

in which case the symmetric Symbolic object replaces it. DEVSIM reuses the Symbolic object for the
`is_same_symbolic` numeric steps of a Newton solve, so each decision holds for those steps too. Cost: one extra
symbolic step per fresh symbolic factorization that AUTO sent to 2-by-2, the same as v1 (the 2-D diode: 1.4 s of
CPU over 23 symbolic steps, ~3 % of its factor time). The returned record gains `auto_2by2` and `kept`
(`replaced + kept == auto_2by2`); the env switch and the not-installed cases are unchanged.

Indices: Info[23] / Info[24] were read against `umfpack_di_report_info` on the same factorization (the "upper bound"
column of "nz in L (incl diagonal)" / "nz in U (incl diagonal)": 9,601,722 / 9,875,001 for 2-D electron + hole DG
under 2-by-2, 6,910,000 / 7,085,696 under symmetric; the diode likewise), the same way Info[18] was verified.
(In 5.1 the estimate block is Info[20..30]; Info[36] is the AMD estimate "est. nz in L+U" that section 2 found
equal for the two strategies.)

**Why the symbolic bound and not the actual fill.** The preferred decision point was the symbolic step (no wasted
numeric factorization). The two strategies' AMD estimates (Info[36]) are identical, as section 2 found, but the
upper bounds are not: 5.1's 2-by-2 symbolic analysis gives a larger bound exactly on the matrices whose numeric
step then fills in. A decision on the actual fill after the numeric step (the fallback considered) would have to
throw away one blown-up numeric factorization per bias step: on the 2-D electron + hole DG fixture that is ~1 s per
step against the 6 ms of the symmetric one, i.e. it would give back most of v1's speed-up there. It was not needed,
because the bound separates the two kinds of matrix with margin (next table).
