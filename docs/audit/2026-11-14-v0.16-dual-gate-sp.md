## What it is

`DualGateSchrodingerPoisson` solves a thin degenerate film gated through oxide on BOTH faces:

```
bottom gate | bottom oxide stack | film z in [0, t] | top oxide stack | top gate
```

- Each gate is a `GateStack(layers=((t_m, eps_r), ...), work_function_offset_V=0.0,
  interface_charge_C_m2=0.0)`; a bare `(t_m, eps_r)` pair or a list of pairs is accepted, and `None` is an
  ungated face (zero displacement beyond it). Gate voltages come from `bias.voltages[bias_keys[i]]`
  (default keys `"bottom"`, `"top"`; a missing key raises).
- The film is contacted laterally: its Fermi level is the grounded reference, E_F is the model's own
  bulk level (`sp_bulk_fermi_level_J`, P6), and the net film charge is whatever the gates and interface
  charges induce (no neutrality imposed). Parabolic or in-plane Kane bands (`alpha_np_per_eV`), the same
  model as P6.
- Inner solve: `SchrodingerPoisson1D.solve_self_consistent` unchanged (slab mode, hard walls on both
  faces, Dirichlet ends at the face potentials, warm-started through P6's Q1 ramp). Outer solve: Newton on
  the two face potentials with a forward-difference Jacobian, Broyden updates, a 0.5 V step cap and
  backtracking; stop at max|R| < 1e-9 C/m^2 with every inner solve converged.
- Sweeps: every solved bias is cached (LRU, 128) with its face potentials, profile and Jacobian; a new
  bias starts from the nearest cached one with the tangent predictor dx = J^-1 diag(C_b, C_t) dV and
  walks there by adaptive continuation (step halving, at most 10) if the direct Newton fails. If that
  fails, `DualGateConvergenceError` (a RuntimeError) names the bias reached and why. A repeated bias
  returns the cached solution (copies).
- Output: `solve(bias) -> CarrierField(ndim=3)` exactly like `SchrodingerPoissonCarrier` (native uniform
  z grid, laterally uniform, density and potential), with extras: face potentials, gate charges,
  interface charges, film charge, sheet excess, both Gauss residuals, Newton iterations, inner solves,
  continuation steps, `from_cache`. `solve_voltages(Vb, Vt) -> DualGateSolution` gives the same as a
  dataclass (`neutrality_residual_C_m2`, `sheet_excess_m2` properties).

```python
from dynameta.carriers import DualGateSchrodingerPoisson, GateStack
from dynameta.sweep import BiasPoint
s = DualGateSchrodingerPoisson(
    t_film_m=6e-9, n_bg_m3=3e26, m_eff_kg=0.30 * M_E, eps_static=9.0, nz=481, alpha_np_per_eV=0.0,
    bottom=GateStack(((4e-9, 3.9), (6e-9, 22.0)), work_function_offset_V=0.3, interface_charge_C_m2=-5e-3),
    top=GateStack((8e-9, 20.0)))
cf = s.solve(BiasPoint({"bottom": -1.0, "top": 2.0}, "b1"))        # CarrierField for the bridge
sol = s.solve_voltages(-1.0, 2.0)                                     # cached: same point, full detail
```

## Boundary conditions (derivation)

In a charge-free oxide layer the displacement is uniform, and with no charge between layers it is the
same in every layer, so the drop across a stack is G sum t_i/(eps0 eps_i) = G/C (G = eps0 eps_i dphi/dz,
C the series capacitance per area). The gate metal sits at phi_g = V - V_wf (V_wf = (W_gate - W_film)/q,
W_film = chi + E_c - E_F of the flat-band film). Gauss's law on a pillbox at each film/oxide interface
with a fixed sheet charge sigma, D_z(+) - D_z(-) = sigma, D_z = -eps0 eps dphi/dz:

    top (z = t):     F_t - C_t (phi_gt - phi_t) - sigma_t = 0
    bottom (z = 0):  C_b (phi_b - phi_gb) - F_b - sigma_b = 0

F = eps0 eps_s dphi/dz on the film side, signed along +z at both faces, taken from P6's conservative
flux `sp_face_flux_C_m2` (half control volume of the end node; the hard-wall end node has n = 0 and its
half cell holds q (h/2) N_d). The discrete Gauss law across the film is then exact, and
Q_gate_b + Q_gate_t + sigma_b + sigma_t + q trapz(N_d - n) = 0 with Q_gate = C (phi_g - phi_face). Both
residuals increase with their own face potential. The plan's residual signs (section 6) agree; the plan
had no interface charge, work-function stack or ungated face.

## Cost

Per bias, 6 nm / nz 481, parabolic: first (cold) point ~0.14 s (anchor + FD Jacobian, ~13 inner solves);
warm sweep points ~0.04 s (3-4 inner solves); a cold jump to +8 V on a 2 nm high-k oxide ~0.18 s.
Kane (alpha 0.4) ~0.25 s per point. 40 nm / nz 1601: ~1.0 s per point (more sub-bands).
`tests/test_sp_dual_gate.py` fast subset ~30 s; the `@slow` semiclassical test ~40 s.

## Deviations from the plan (section 6, P12)

- Signature: `bottom=` / `top=` take a `GateStack` (layers, work-function offset, interface charge) per
  the owner's requirements; the plan's `ox_bottom=(t, eps)` form is accepted as a plain pair. `None`
  (ungated face) added; it is the exact C_b -> 0 limit used in the single-gate check.
- Charge balance gated at 2e-9 C/m^2 absolute, not "1e-9 of |Q_film|": the balance measures the inner
  self-consistency (final Schrodinger fill vs the Poisson predictor density), bounded by the inner
  tolerance in absolute terms; a relative bar is unreachable where Q_film crosses zero (e.g. 1.4e-3 C/m^2
  at (1.5, 0) V would need 1.4e-12).
- Mirror-symmetry bar 1e-9 instead of 1e-10: measured 1.6e-10 for the symmetric case with the outer stop at
  1e-11 C/m^2 (the test's setting; at the default 1e-9 C/m^2 stop 1.2e-9, see (*) above).
- Inner solve with `relax=1.0` (retry at 0.5 on non-convergence) and `tol_V=1e-11`: measured 2x faster
  than the default 0.7, converging to 1e-12 V; needed so the face flux is quiet (eps_s/h ~ 6.4 F/m^2, so
  1e-11 V is 6e-11 C/m^2).
- Sub-band count: `n_states=None` (default) takes a rigorous count from the min-max bound
  E_j(T + U) >= E_j(T) + min U with the exact discrete hard-wall kinetic eigenvalues: every state below
  E_F + 30 kT for a potential up to max(face potentials, warm start) + 0.25 V, plus one.
- Single-gate limit compared on the top face potential and the top 5 nm, with the thickness convergence
  recorded, rather than "the top half": the far wall perturbs the sub-band ladder of the whole film
  (6.0e-3 n_bg at 10 nm, 1.2e-5 at 40 nm), so a half-film window would measure film size, not the face
  law.

## Scope, validation and other packages

- `validation/sp_dual_gate.py` gates the face laws (re-derived fluxes, charge balance),
  mirror / gate-exchange symmetry, second-order grid convergence, the single-gate limit and the signs (9 gates,
  ~20 s, pure numpy).
- Laterally uniform only (no per-column map as in `SchrodingerPoissonCarrier.surface_potential_xy`);
  no charge inside the oxide stacks (enter a known amount as a V_wf shift); single material film (no band
  offsets, constant mass along z).
- `carriers/__init__.py` now lists `DualGateSchrodingerPoisson`, `GateStack` and `sp_dual_gate` in
  `__all__` (lazy, devsim-free). No ci.yml entry needed: the test file needs no solver stack and runs on
  the plain `pytest tests/ -m "not slow"` legs.
- Bitwise determinism across machines not checked (no bitwise gate added).

## Release-note lines

- New: `carriers.DualGateSchrodingerPoisson` / `GateStack` -- self-consistent Schrodinger-Poisson for a
  film gated through oxide stacks on both faces (per-gate voltage, oxide stack, work-function offset,
  interface charge; parabolic or Kane; the film Fermi level is the reference, film charge is free). Gauss's
  law at both faces to 1e-9 C/m^2; cached, warm-started bias sweeps; `DualGateConvergenceError` on
  failure. Emits `CarrierField(ndim=3)` like `SchrodingerPoissonCarrier`.
