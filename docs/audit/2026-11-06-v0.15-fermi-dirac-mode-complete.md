Reading it: the BTBT, nonlocal and bulk-trap legacy numbers shrink 2.7-4.1x per halving of h -- they are the
transport's discretisation battery (defect 2 below; the Richardson limit of the trap row is ~0, |.| <~ 1e-3,
INFERRED), present in the FD mode too until this work. The interface-trap row does not move with h: that is
the statistics defect itself, 41 % of the surface exchange, because the surface states see degenerate surface
electrons through a fixed n_ie. The "exact equilibrium" column isolates the statistics defect of each rate
expression: node-wise O(1) wherever a carrier is degenerate (1.08, 1.10, 0.785), but for the BTBT and bulk
traps those nodes carry almost no exchange (6.8e-8, 3.8e-7 integrated), and the nonlocal model has none (it
already reads the FD activities). The non-degenerate limit (gate N): the legacy interface imbalance at
zero bias scales with the largest degeneracy offset, 7.5e-4 / 1.13e-2 of the exchange at delta_max = 1.2e-3 /
1.27e-2 (N_D = 1e23 / 1e24 m^-3).

Under `statistics="fermi_dirac"` every one of these is round-off (<= 64 eps (1 + max|L|) ~ 9e-13; MEASURED
4e-16 .. 6e-15), at h and at h/2.

**Two defects add up in the legacy column**, and the second one was also in the FD mode until this work:

1. **Statistics** (the documented one): Boltzmann-product n_ie in the rates' D / n1 / p1 and in the contacts'
   minority pin. It lives where a degenerate carrier meets a mechanism: the neutral degenerate side
   (SRH / traps / interface traps -- the minority quasi-Fermi level is pinned wrong at the contact), and the
   degenerate fringes of a depletion layer (the Hurkx D). It is h-independent.
2. **Transport discretisation (FINDING, new)**: the FD Scharfetter-Gummel flux with the edge-AVERAGED g,
   g_avg = (g0 + g1)/2, vanishes iff g_avg ln(c1/c0) = (psi1 - psi0)/V_t, while every detailed-balance term
   (and the contacts, and the thermionic interface) uses the activity L = ln u + Delta(u), whose exact edge
   difference is Int g d ln c. The two differ by the trapezoid error (d ln c)^3 g''/12 per edge: an O(h^2)
   **zero-bias battery** next to every degenerate gradient. MEASURED: 1e9 A/m^2 "zero-bias" current (0.4 mV)
   across an n+ 1e26 / n 3e25 m^-3 homojunction at h = 1 nm; 1.35e-4 V across the n+ 1e26 / n 3e25 thermionic
   heterostack (3.97e-5 V at 0.5 nm). On the p+ 1e25 / n+ 1e26 Zener this battery is **the whole** legacy BTBT
   and nonlocal imbalance (the FD mode with the averaged g gave the same 2.52e-2 / 2.44e-2 to 4 digits, and
   both shrink ~2.8-3.6x per halving of h); the statistics part there is only ~7e-8 (MEASURED: the legacy
   BTBT at an exact FD equilibrium built on the solved psi).

So making `"fermi_dirac"` the default would remove both. The size of the move on released results is
item (1) (si-bundles G2: -0.12 J0 at 1e19, -0.70 J0 at 1e20 cm^-3) plus item (2), which is mesh-dependent
(< 1e-5 relative on the R27-c diode, 2.5 % of the BTBT exchange on a 0.2 nm abrupt Zener).

   under `"fermi_dirac"` and by `physics_drift_diffusion.setup_semiconductor_region_dd(statistics="fermi_dirac")`,

   g = 1 in the SG prefactor and the degeneracy as a quasi-field in the Bernoulli argument,

   noise module's edge density, so the diffusion noise is Nyquist-exact on degenerate edges (derivation in
   the docstring). BGN composes (the quasi-field is appended to the BGN band-edge argument).
   *Tried first and rejected*: the divided-difference g_e = (L1 - L0)/ln(c1/c0) (Bessemoulin-Chatard 2012;
   same equilibrium, same FDT). From the usual charge-neutral seed of the abrupt n+ 1e26 Zener its Newton drove

   seed.

## 3. Exactness: derived, then gated

* **Algebraic**: every mechanism's net rate is a product with a factor that vanishes identically when
  L_n + L_p = ln(n_ie0^2/(N_c N_v)) (local terms) or when E_F is flat along a path (nonlocal). So at an exact
  discrete equilibrium the rates are round-off. Gate Z builds such a state on the solved psi (Newton on
  DEVSIM's own HJ_L models; construction residual 7.1e-15) and checks every term node by node.
* **Device**: with the quasi-field transport the solved zero-bias state IS such a state, so the device's
  zero-bias mechanism exchange is round-off too, at every mesh (no convergence study needed; checked at h and
  h/2). With the averaged g it is not (O(h^2), item 2 of section 1) -- which is why the original brief's
  "at the discretisation order" alternative was what the old transport could give.

## 4. Thermionic FDT residual (coordinator scope addition): root cause

Reported: unipolar FD ITO/AZO heterostacks miss S = 4kT Re Y by 1.8e-5 .. 1.5e-4, Boltzmann stacks pass to
1e-12. **Root cause (MEASURED): not the thermionic strings, the averaged-g FD transport.**

* The flux A*T^2 [exp(w0) - exp(w1)] with w = L - k is written in the FD activities on both sides: it vanishes
  exactly at a flat E_F and its linear response is A*T^2 e^w d(Delta E_F)/kT, i.e. the pair 2 A*T^2 e^w gives
  4kT G_int exactly -- at any degeneracy (within the Maxwell-Boltzmann Richardson tail it models). Nothing to
  make FD-consistent there; the legacy statistics use the same strings.
* What breaks it: (a) the averaged g's zero-bias battery (the stack is not at equilibrium at V = 0), and (b)
  the averaged g's edge conductance is not q mu n_eff. Both O(h^2).
* MEASURED FDT residual S/(4kT Re Y) - 1 (1 Hz; +-1e-7 / +-1e-6 V averaged):

| Stack (1-D) | averaged g (legacy statistics / unipolar default) | quasi-field (`"fermi_dirac"`, bipolar or unipolar) |

## 7. Findings outside the brief (reported, not changed)

1. **Local BTBT FDT in 1-D, any statistics.** The 1-D edge assembly gives each edge the MEAN of its two
   nodes' Hurkx D and delivers it to both nodes; its linear response couples the two nodes asymmetrically
   (Onsager symmetry broken at O(h)), which no node-local GR source reproduces. S = 4kT G_dc misses by
   -6.35e-5 at h = 1 nm and -1.59e-5 at 0.5 nm (MEASURED, BTBT 90 % of S), identically under Boltzmann and FD.
   The v0.15 BTBT-presets FDT gate (1e-4) sits inside it. A fix would change the 1-D assembly (e.g. D at one
   point per edge, or a two-point edge noise source); the 2-D/3-D element assembly uses each node's own D.
2. **ssac floor on degenerate devices** (as the nonlocal note found on the 5e24 Zener): on the FDT diode with a
   3e25 m^-3 n+ side, ssac Re Y(1 Hz) is 4.3e-4 off the DC conductance of 703 S/m^2 (MEASURED; ~0.3 S/m^2
   absolute) with no extra mechanism. Gate F therefore uses a Richardson DC conductance.
3. **DEVSIM model cache**: replacing an edge model that others depend on is NOT seen by the next solve's first
   assembly (MEASURED: a solve "converged" in one iteration on the stale model, then get_matrix_and_rhs showed
   a 1e11 residual). Re-setting a solution variable forces the re-evaluation.
4. **False convergence under the 'positive' update**: DEVSIM's relative-update criterion can be met while the
   limiter shrinks a minority node towards 0 (rel error falling by exactly 1e3 per iteration). The gates check
   the solved state's residual (`_fd_mode_child.residual_scale`, <= 1e-12) as well.
5. **Circuit criterion at exactly zero current**: with an exact equilibrium the circuit node's relative update
   never converges (0.1-0.4); the FDT scenarios run at +-1e-7 V (the odd term cancels in the average).
