## 2. The model, and how the phonon-assisted transcription was verified (decision 22)

Holes appear at the path start x0 (tunnelling energy eps = E_V(x0)), electrons at the end l where
E_C(l) = eps; the path runs along -grad E_V. Net hole recombination rate at the start:

* direct (Eqs. 480-484): `R = |grad E_V| (g pi/36h) (Int dx/kappa)^-1 exp(-2 Int kappa dx) [f_n(l) - f_p(0)]`,
  kappa = (2/hbar) sqrt(m_r (eps - E_V)(E_C - eps)/E_g);
* phonon (Eqs. 490-493): `R = |grad E_V| C_p exp(-2 Int_0^x0 kappa_V - 2 Int_x0^l kappa_C) [f_n - f_p]`,
  `C_p = l [g(1+2N_op)D_op^2/(2^6 pi^2 rho eps_op E_g)] sqrt(m_V m_C/(h l sqrt(2 m_r E_g))) / (I_V I_C)`,
  kappa_V, kappa_C Keldysh, x0 where they are equal.

Choices: k_m -> infinity (the cut-off factors are 1) and m0 -> infinity in alpha (Eq. 483), so m_V = m_C =
2 m_r is the consistent phonon default (`mass_ratio_V_over_C = 1`).

**Transcription check of Eq. 491.** The page image (`lit_nonlocal/img/sd-0485.png`, read for this note) shows
an outer `Int_0^l [...] dx` whose integrand holds only material constants and l. The P1 plan flagged it as
uncertain. I took the uniform-field limit of Eqs. 490-493 by hand, with that integral read as l x [...]:

* x0 = m_C l/(m_V + m_C);
* 2 Int kappa = 2^(7/2) pi sqrt(m_r) E_g^(3/2)/(3 q h F). This is Eq. 498 exactly;
* (Int dx/kappa_V)^-1 (Int dx/kappa_C)^-1 = qF(m_V + m_C)/(2 hbar^2 l);
* R = (qF)^(5/2) g(1+2N_op) D_op^2 (m_V m_C)^(3/2) / (2^(21/4) h^(5/2) m_r^(5/4) rho eps_op E_g^(7/4))
  exp(-B/F). This is Eq. 497 exactly: every power, and the 2^(21/4).

A misplaced l, dx or integral would change at least one of those powers. So **decision 22, first option:
Sentaurus Eqs. 490-491 are implemented**, not our average-field form. Gate N1 re-checks the identity
numerically. The direct form (Eqs. 495-496) was verified the same way.

**Constants.** h = H_PLANCK and hbar = h/(2 pi). The package's HBAR literal is rounded 6e-10 low, which would
break the 1e-12 identity through B/F ~ 30.

## 3. Discretisation, and a finding against the plan's design

* **Band edges.** E_V = -q psi + const and E_C = E_V + E_g along the path. In units of the gap,
  v = (psi - psi(x0))/E_g runs from 0 to 1.
* **Starts and path.**
  * Start nodes are the interior non-contact nodes.
  * |grad E_V(0)| is the 3-point (second-order) nodal derivative, and its sign is the direction (1-D).
  * The end is the exact linear root v = 1.
  * A path is discarded, and counted in `last_stats`, when it:
    * leaves the region ("boundary"; decision 24: no reflection);
    * bends back ("reentrant": v not strictly rising, or a nodal slope <= 0 along it);
    * ends in an edge touching a contact node ("contact": DEVSIM drops that bulk row);
    * is longer than max_length ("too_long", with a C^1 taper over the last 5 %).
* **Exponent integrals** (Int kappa, Int kappa_V, Int kappa_C) and the path length are closed forms on the
  P1 band, written cancellation-free.
  * No arcsin of sqrt(v) is ever taken. It is ill-conditioned at v -> 1: an ulp of v costs sqrt(eps). The
    first form measured 9.5e-10 on the uniform identity; the final form is <= 6.5e-14.
  * x - sin x is computed as a series.
* **Prefactor integrals Int dx/kappa: FINDING, and the fix.**
  * The plan's design took them on the P1 band too. They have an integrable 1/sqrt singularity at a path
    end, where the P1 slope is the edge secant, off the true slope by O(h v'').
  * The end piece 2 sqrt(h/sigma) therefore carries an O(h^{3/2}) error. Measured against the continuum
    oracle: per-path order 1.30 (direct) and 1.37 (phonon); the phonon Int dx/kappa_V error falls by exactly
    2.83 = 2^{3/2} per halving.
  * The same secant jump makes the rate a sqrt-kinked function of psi whenever a path end crosses a node.
    Measured: a DEVSIM DC dI/dV that moved by 2.5e-4 between +-1 mV and +-0.1 mV differences, against
    1.4e-6 without the term.
  * Fix: integrate the prefactor IN v, as Int r(v) K(v) dv, with r = ds/dv interpolated linearly between the
    nodal slopes. These are closed-form zeroth and first moments (`kane_moments`, `keldysh_moments`).
  * Result: per-path order 2.00 / 2.00, integrated current 1.99 / 1.99, and C^1 at node crossings (the DEVSIM
    dI/dV then agrees with ssac to 6.6e-7).
  * A uniform field has r constant, so both forms coincide and the identity is untouched.
* **Occupancies** come from the transport's own log activities, `L = ln u (+ Delta(u) under FD)`
  (`physics_heterojunction.setup_log_activity`, reused).
  * E_Fn is interpolated linearly to the end.
  * f_n(l) is evaluated at eps in the statistics frame: `delta = ln(N_c N_v/n_i^2) - E_g,tun/kT`.
  * f_n - f_p = sinh((a-b)/2)/(2 cosh cosh).
  * At a flat E_F the rate vanishes to round-off whatever E_g,tun is (gate N3). The refutation shifts delta by
    1e-3 and breaks it.
* **Assembly.** +q R V_i goes to the start node's hole row. The same amount goes to the electron rows of the
  end edge's nodes with weights (1-t), t.
  * The sign is measured identical to `add_recombination_term`: bit-equal rhs, scratch `probe_sign.py`.
  * Conservation is exact by construction (gate N5).

## 4. Jacobian (decision 25) and noise

* **1-D is exact.** The direction is a sign, which flips only where the gradient is 0, and the rate is 0
  there. The term declares `jacobian_exact = True`, so ssac and noise accept it.
* **How the derivatives are taken.**
  * The derivatives of every closed-form segment piece, with respect to its end values and its nodal slopes,
    are complex-step derivatives of the closed forms (Squire & Trapp 1998): exact to round-off, with no
    step-size error.
  * The chain rule through the path sums, the implicit end point t, the start gradient, the slopes (3-point
    weights), the taper and the occupancies is written out.
  * The kernel Jacobian matches 4th-order FD to 1e-10, which is the FD truncation (measured, scratch
    `smoke_jac.py`).
* **Sparsity.** The pattern follows the paths (`pattern_changes = True`; no `structural_pattern`).
  `custom_terms` warns under DEVSIM_NEW_SYMBOLIC=0.
* **Noise.** One `TwoPointSource` (mechanism `btbt_nl`) per path:
  * rows (hole, start, +1), (electron, a, -(1-t)), (electron, b, -t);
  * rate V_i R_pre [f_n(1-f_p) + f_p(1-f_n)].

## 6. Measurements

**Zener (abrupt 5e24/5e24 m^-3, Si Hurkx A, B in both models, E_g,tun = 1.12 eV).**

| V_r [V] | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.6 | 0.9 | 1.2 | 2.0 | 2.5 | 3.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nonlocal q Int G [A/m^2] | 0 (no path) | 3.0e-4 | 7.7e-2 | 1.81 | 16.0 | 649 | 2.98e3 | 9.91e3 |
| local / nonlocal | inf | 1134 | 49 | 12.8 | 6.1 | 2.2 | 1.45 | 0.96 |

* The nonlocal current is far lower at low bias. There the band bending barely exceeds the gap, so the paths
  are long or absent.
* The two converge at high bias, consistent with Jiao 2013 (about equal in Si homojunctions).
* The plan's "factor 2 at high field" holds only above ~2 V on this junction.

**Graded junction.** On a tanh junction (2 V over a 5 nm width, smooth quasi-Fermi profiles), the local Kane
rate (D = -1) over the continuum nonlocal oracle is 10.0 (direct) and 9.4 (phonon). This is a junction where
the local field differs from the band-bending field over a tunnelling length.

## 9. Findings outside this item (not changed)

1. **ssac on heavily doped junctions.** DEVSIM ssac Re Y has an absolute floor of ~1 S/m^2 on the 5e24 m^-3
   Zener and on a p+n+ junction with light contacts, with or without any BTBT term. Measured -0.56 and
   -0.33 S/m^2 against DC conductances of 6.6e-6 and 8.1e-6 S/m^2. The noise analysis on the same devices
   agrees with 4kT G_dc to 2.5e-4, which is the +-1 mV difference error. The N8/N9 gates therefore use the
   FDT diode.
2. **`tests/_custom_terms_child.fd_columns` needs a circuit.** It scans every node model with
   `get_equation_numbers`, which trips `NodeKeeper.cc:266 CIRCUIT_UNEXPECTED` (a fatal DEVSIM assertion) on a
   device without a circuit. A restricted copy lives in `_btbt_nonlocal_child.py`.
3. **Legacy FD g-factor transport.** With `fd_enhancement=True, statistics=None` the zero-bias state is not a
   strict equilibrium (the documented FD/Boltzmann mismatch). The nonlocal rate sees it: |R|/one-way is
   2.7e-4 and 1.6e-4 at 0.2 and 0.1 nm. This is reported, not gated.

## Release-note lines

* New opt-in **nonlocal band-to-band tunnelling** for 1-D bipolar devices: `btbt_nonlocal.setup_btbt_nonlocal`,
  the Sentaurus dynamic-path model.
  * Direct and phonon-assisted processes from the same `BTBTParams` as the local model.
  * Holes are generated at the path start and electrons at its end.
  * The rate is exactly zero where no final state is within reach.
  * The Jacobian is exact, and the noise source is two-point (`btbt_nl`).
  * It reduces to A F^P exp(-B/F) in a uniform field to 6.5e-14, and converges at second order to an
    independent continuum oracle.
* On an abrupt Si Zener it gives 1134x less current than the local Hurkx model at 0.3 V reverse and agrees
  within 1.5x above 2.5 V.
