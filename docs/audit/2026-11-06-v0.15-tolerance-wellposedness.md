## 4.4.1 Density-scaled default absolute tolerance

**What was wrong.** DEVSIM's Newton requires BOTH `rel < relative_error` AND `abs < absolute_error`
(P4 0.1: per equation abs = max |dx|, region = sum over equations, device = max over regions). In a
drift-diffusion region the absolute error is the largest density update in m^-3. Round-off floors it
at kappa u S (S = the same aggregation of max |x|), so the fixed default 1e10 m^-3 is unattainable
once S exceeds ~1e24 m^-3, and below one ulp above ~6e25.

**What changed.** `solve_dc(abs_tol=None)` resolves `default_abs_tol() = max(1e10, 1024 max_d u_d S_d)`
on the pre-solve state, over EVERY live device (u_d = 2^-53, or 2^-113 for an extended-precision
device). The ONE implementation; `solution_size` is public. An explicit float is passed unchanged;
Gummel sub-solves use the resolved value. (As first committed, `3159b21`, it sized only the solved
device and used 2^-113 for all devices once any was extended; both were wrong -- see "Integration fix"
below.)
Below S = 8.8e22 m^-3, and on every extended-precision device (up to S ~ 1e41), the same float 1e10
reaches DEVSIM: byte-identical by construction (gated bitwise on a 1e22 slab and a Poisson-only
equilibrium device).

**Gates (tests/test_dc_solve_abs_tol.py, 9 tests, 65 s).** Reference: the same device in DEVSIM's
extended Newton, tightened to relative 1e-25 (independent arithmetic). Measured iteration counts per
continuation step:

|---|---|---|---|
| ITO gate, unipolar DD, n_bg 4e26, 0 -> 1 V in 0.25 V | 1, **100 (exhausted)** | 1, 4, 4, 4, 4 | 1, 5, 4, 4, 4 |
| GATE C classical accumulation, n 1e24 FD, +0.15 V in 5 (peak 8.2e25) | 1, 6, 6, 11, 7, 9 | 1, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6 | 1, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6 |
| R27-a n+ slab 1e25, holes x3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| GATE C in 2 steps of 75 mV (14x in-step growth of S) | -- | 1, 7, 7 | 1, 7, 7 |

  the wall potential breaks the per-node bound.
- K = 1024 derivation: with abs_tol = 0 the round-off floor after the relative test passes is, in ulp of
  the PRE-solve S, median 1.5 / max 11.0 (GATE C) and median 0.5 / max 1.06 (ITO); K u = 512 ulp >= 10x
  the max (gated).
- The old default's ITO failure is not luck: 1e10 is 0.11 ulp of 4e26 (gated). The GATE C extra

## 4.4.2 Floating-body (ill-posed DC) detection

**What was wrong.** Nothing checked well-posedness. A region where no contact pins a carrier has an
EXACTLY singular DC Jacobian (P4 4.1), so its answer is set by round-off. The R26 slab fixture was such
a case until 2026-11-03 (`7fabbca`): pinning only the Potential left the body's charge free, and the
result depended on the BLAS build. Re-measured here on the old construction (200 nm slab, scipy
OpenBLAS): the n slab's +20 mV body shift re-converges in place 18.4 mV off; the p slab hits a fatal
UMFPACK factorization.

**What changed.** `dynameta/carriers/well_posedness.py`:
- `check_dc_well_posed(device=None, numeric="candidates"|"full")` -> `WellPosedReport`; raises
  `IllPosedDCError` (a `ValueError`).
- Static pass from DEVSIM's own commands (raw `ds.*` fixtures included): carrier components joined by
  interface equations, Potential components; a component with no contact `node_model` for that equation
  is a candidate. Kinetic-trap / auxiliary rows join a region's candidate when it already has a free
  flux block (the [C] note: a floating body with traps is still caught).
- Numeric pass (only with a candidate): ONE `get_matrix_and_rhs`, Ruiz-equilibrated; candidate vectors
  = block indicators of the kept rows (DEVSIM's returned row permutation excludes moved / replaced
  interface and contact rows), the best combination by an SVD; a mode is exact when
  `r = |y^T J_eq| / (|y| sigma_max) <= 64 eps`. A componentwise r was tried first and REJECTED: a column
  whose only entries are a tiny coupling (a tau = 1e30 slab's dU/dn) reads r = 1 although the matrix is
  singular to 1e-37.
- Error only when static and numeric agree; a refuted candidate is reported, never raised.
- `allow_floating(device, region, carriers=..., reason=...)`, recorded in `eq_registry` (cleared with it;
  `eq_registry.clear` also bumps a generation counter that invalidates cached verdicts).
- `ensure_dc_well_posed()` = the cached hook. `dc_solve.solve_dc` calls it before every DC Newton
  (newton and gummel), so the builders are covered.
- `diagnose_singular(device)`: equilibrated sigma_min / sigma_max (dense SVD <= 5000 unknowns, else
  sparse LU inverse iteration) and the support of the singular vector. `solve_dc` now runs DEVSIM's
  Newton with `info=True` and re-raises a non-converged solve as `devsim.error("Convergence failure!
  ...")`, appending the diagnosis when the failure is a STALL (all iterations used, last relative
  error < 1e-2) -- not on a diverging step, so the SVD is not paid on routine continuation failures.
- `tests/conftest.py`: every raw `devsim.solve(type="dc")` in the test suite runs the hook (decision 55);
  `@pytest.mark.floating_ok("reason")` opts out; `DYNAMETA_WELLPOSED_STATS=<path>` dumps the counters.

## Integration fix (branch `wip/v015_numfix`, from `94e61fc`): size every live device

**What was wrong (measured).** Under `pytest -n 3` an order-dependent failure appeared in about 1 of 3
runs: `test_devsim_tunneling.py::test_w1_layered_oxide_tunneling_extras_and_default_off` ran 60 Newton
iterations at `absolute_error = 1.137e11` and raised. The cause is in the tolerance rule, not in the
hook. DEVSIM's `ds.solve` runs ONE Newton over every live device and requires `abs < absolute_error`
on EACH of them. `solve_dc` sized only the device being solved. Its iteration log shows two devices in
that Newton: the builder device `tz_w1doff`, converged at relative 1.7e-15 with abs 8.9e7, and
`tz_mos`, the module-scoped 1-D MOS fixture of the same file, left at 9 V accumulation, with abs
1.96e11 > 1.137e11 on its electrons at every iteration. Before decision 59 the layered bipolar rule
had a 1e13 floor that absorbed that leftover floor, so the bug arrived with `3784cdb`. Under xdist,
whether `tz_mos` is still alive when W1 runs depends on the scheduling.

**Deterministic reproduction.** `pytest tests/test_devsim_tunneling.py::test_f1_oxide_field_matches_
gauss_law tests/test_devsim_tunneling.py::test_w1_layered_oxide_tunneling_extras_and_default_off` gives
the same message every time on `94e61fc`. So does a light 1e22 slab solved through `solve_dc` while a
converged ITO gate (4e26) is live: the light slab's own default is exactly 1e10, 0.11 ulp of the live
background.

**Second defect, same rule.** `u` was 2^-113 for every device as soon as ANY live device was extended.
A double device keeps its double residual inside the extended Newton (its equations were created
double). So a leaked extended device shrank a degenerate double device's default to 1e10 (measured:
the ITO gate with an extended 8e25 slab live: default 1e10 on `94e61fc`, 4.5e13 now).

**Fix.** `default_abs_tol(device=None) = max(1e10, 1024 max_d u_d S_d)`, with u_d taken per device,
and `solve_dc` (Newton and Gummel) resolves it over every live device. A non-converged `solve_dc` now
also lists the device / region / equation that held the joint Newton back at the last iteration.
`diagnose_singular` now searches every live device for the mode (the old message said "0 of its mass"
because the mode was in the leftover device). Byte-identity is unchanged: a device alone gives the
same float as before, and with no device above 8.8e22 live it is still exactly 1e10.

**Gates** (tests/test_dc_solve_abs_tol.py; both FAIL on `94e61fc`, measured):
- `test_default_sizes_every_live_device`: with the ITO gate live, the light slab converges under the
  default (on `94e61fc`: 60 iterations, "Convergence failure!"), to its solo answer within 1e-10.
  `default_abs_tol()` = the max-over-devices formula. Refutation: the explicit 1e10 still fails, and
  the message names `abs_left/ito/ElectronContinuityEquation`.
- `test_mixed_precision_devices_are_sized_by_the_double_one`: with an extended slab live, the default
  is 1024 * 2^-53 * S of the double ITO gate (on `94e61fc`: 1e10), and the ITO steps converge.
- The f1 + W1 pair (deterministic on `94e61fc`) now passes. The coordinator's file set at `-n 3 -m
  "not slow"` was run three times: 178 passed each time (183 / 193 / 190 s). I ran 17 files: the 16 of
  `w1_bisect.log` plus `test_devsim_tunneling.py`, because the 18th file was not recoverable from the
  logs. Own suites at `-n 3`: abs_tol, well-posedness, Gummel, smoke, recombination/BGN, extended
  precision, heterojunction, 3-D mesh, CI coverage, phase composition, bipolar DG and wave-7 carriers
  gave 199 passed, 3 xfailed.

**Joint-Newton isolation (branch `wip/v015_numfix2`, from `c6aa0a4`).** A DC solve iterates EVERY live
DEVSIM device, not only the one named. A device left live by earlier work therefore does two things:
it affects the solve's convergence (the fix above), and it is itself moved by the solve. Measured: a
converged ITO gate left live changes its node values by round-off (< 1e-12 relative, nonzero) when an
unrelated slab is solved; gated in `tests/test_devsim_isolated.py`. That makes a BITWISE comparison of
two separate solves meaningful only in a process with no unrelated device live. Measured on W1: with
two converged leftovers (an ITO gate and a 1e22 slab) the off / on builder potentials differed by
1.1e-16 V on one run and 0 on the next. With `tz_mos` alone they were equal. One leftover was not
enough to make two light-slab solves differ (measured three times: bitwise equal), so the exposure is
real but not reproducible on demand.

What changed:
- `tests/conftest.py` gains `@pytest.mark.devsim_isolated("reason")`, registered in pyproject. A marked
  test runs, unchanged, in a fresh `python -m pytest <nodeid>`. Its fixtures are rebuilt there, and the
  xdist environment variables are stripped so the child is not a worker. The child's pass / skip /
  fail and its output are reported as the test's own result.
- I chose a marker over a hand-written scenario child (the `_custom_terms_child.py` pattern) for
  these tests: the test bodies and their bitwise asserts stay exactly as written, nothing is
  duplicated into a child module, and other agents' files change by one decorator line.
- Gates (`tests/test_devsim_isolated.py`, 3 tests, ~25 s):
  - The premise: the leftover is live and is moved by an unrelated solve.
  - A marked test runs in the child with the leftover absent, and two separate solves there are

  - Refutation: a nested pytest on `tests/_isolated_probe.py` reports "1 failed, 1 passed" with the

Bitwise comparisons in the DEVSIM test suite, assessed (grep of `array_equal` / `tobytes() ==` /

## Release-note lines

- `solve_dc(abs_tol=None)` (new default) uses `max(1e10, 1024 u S)` over every live device, the
  round-off-attainable absolute tolerance of DEVSIM's joint Newton. Results are unchanged below S = 8.8e22 m^-3 and on extended-precision devices; degenerate
  devices no longer stall or exhaust Newton (an ITO 4e26 gate step used to run out of 100 iterations).
  The layered and 3-D builders use the same rule (results bit-identical or within 1e-15).
- DC solves now refuse an exactly singular problem (a floating body: no contact pins a carrier; a
  potential with no Dirichlet) with `IllPosedDCError`, naming the region, carrier and fixes; acknowledge
  an intended floating body with `well_posedness.allow_floating`.
- A stalled `solve_dc` Newton failure now says why when the DC Jacobian is near-singular
  ("near-singular DC Jacobian (sigma_min/sigma_max = 4e-16): mode in Electrons, ...").
