# v0.16 P1: slab-thickness guard (finding N2)

Branch `wip/v016_p01_slabguard`, based on 2e1af87 (v0.15.1).

## What changed

- `dynameta/core/layered.py`: new `slice_layer_eps_field(eps_field, layer, metres_per_unit, *,
  n_slices=None, rtol=1e-6)`, the one slab-thickness guard. It calls `slice_eps_field` unchanged and
  returns the same slab objects, then raises `ValueError` when
  - the gridded field has fewer than two z samples (a laterally structured field with one z sample
    used to slice to ZERO slabs, dropping the layer; a laterally uniform one raised slice_profile's
    generic length error),
  - the slab list is empty, or
  - `abs(sum(t) - layer.thickness_m) > rtol * layer.thickness_m`.

  The message names the layer, both thicknesses, their ratio and the likely cause (ratio within a
  decade of 1e-9: axes in metres where the bridge reads nm; within a decade of 1e9: the reverse;
  otherwise: the z span does not cover the layer). A uniform EpsField is still refused by
  `slice_eps_field` itself.
- Call sites switched to the guard: `optics/tmm_reference.layered_stack_from_design`,
  `optics/fdtd_seam.design_to_fdtd_layers`, `optics/lumenairy_bridge/berreman_backend.
  design_to_berreman_layers`, `optics/lumenairy_bridge/pmm2d_backend.design_to_pmm2d_stack`. The PMM and
  RCWA bridge call sites belong to P3 / P2 (same call: `slice_layer_eps_field(ef, L, 1.0 / _S_NM,
  n_slices=n_slices)`).
- `make_layered_tmm_solver` and `make_lumenairy_berreman_solver` closures now carry
  `native_carrier_grid = True` (the capability flag P4's `run_pipeline` reads). Nothing reads it on this
  branch, so no result moves here.

## Plan statements found wrong or incomplete

- The brief's "laterally structured field with one z sample" is extended to every gridded field with
  fewer than two z samples (uniform or structured, any `n_slices`), checked before slicing: with
  `n_slices` given the structured branch did not return zero slabs but divided 0/0 (RuntimeWarning) and
  then failed in `LayeredSlab` ("thickness_m must be > 0"), and the laterally uniform branch raised
  slice_profile's length error; neither message named the layer.
- `native_carrier_grid` is set on TMM and Berreman only, as the brief says. The PMM2D and FDTD seam
  solvers are left without it: FDTD cannot use sub-cell slabs, and PMM2D cost grows with the slab count
  (a native carrier z grid can carry hundreds of intervals); flagging them is an owner decision.
