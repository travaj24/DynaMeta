## 1. What changed

* **Trap g-r noise (K3).** `physics_traps._install_kinetic_level` / `_install_kinetic_bins` pass the EVENT rates
  `srh_capture_exprs(pair=True)` (times ItArea N_b c_n for a bin) as `pair=` to the two single-carrier branches;
  partner rows (+1 / -1 on the trap row) and mechanisms (`trap_n` / `trap_p`, in `DEFAULT_MECHANISMS`) were
  already recorded. The source is the 4.0.2 two-point event S = q^2 V [2 P_n |y_t - y_n|^2 + 2 P_p |y_p - y_t|^2].
* **`noise_analysis`**:
  * the circuit-contact-node refusal is restricted to equations the contact REPLACES (a contact equation of
    that name) and that have no fix-up; a trap row (kept by DEVSIM at contact nodes) is accepted.
  * the CONTACT-NODE RULE (`_single_carrier_sources`): where a contact replaces a single-carrier term's own
    row but not its partner row, the event carries no source -- the level at an ohmic contact node is
    invisible to the DC and small-signal system (its rows' couplings sit only in rows the contact replaced;
    the partner row is decoupled). MEASURED: counting it misses the gate-B FDT by +2.3e-3, exactly the two
    contact-node contributions of the per-node map. A term WITHOUT a partner keeps its contact-node events
    (the 4.0.2 convention, `tests/test_term_bookkeeping_v2.py` gate (b) unchanged).
* **The DC FORM** (`physics_traps.kinetic_dc_form`, entered by `dc_solve.solve_dc` around its Newton, hence by
  every builder): inside it the kinetic levels use the quasi-static occupancy f_SS(n, p) for their rates and
  charge and slave w to w_SS (no n, p derivative: w lags one iteration and cannot feed back). The DC root is
  the same (R_n = R_p <=> w = w_SS); transient / ssac / noise always see the physical rows (switch 0). The
  occupancy models f, 1 - f are now blends `D f_SS + (1 - D) w/(1+w)` with CLOSED-FORM derivatives (n, p, w;
  no 1 - f subtraction), and the rate terms are differentiated by DEVSIM through them. WHY (MEASURED): a gated
  bipolar GaAs `LayeredDevsimBuilder` with kinetic interface bins could not converge from its neutral seed --
  the physical row flung w of the near-valence-edge bins past 1e150, (1 - f)^2 underflowed and UMFPACK hit an
  FPE; a linear (1 + w)-scaled row instead let the interface charge swing and the carriers diverged (w
  1e190 after 60 iterations); the quasi-static twin converged. With the DC form it converges and equals the
  quasi-static state to 1e-10. (DEVSIM's diff of w/(1+w) is 1/(1+w) - w/(1+w)^2: MEASURED 2e4 eps on the
  Poisson w-column of a full level, hence the closed forms.)
* **Well-posedness** (`well_posedness`): LOCAL candidates -- every free node-only equation (the kinetic trap
  rows `TrapEquation_*`, `ItEquation*`) is checked row by row (an all-zero equilibrated row is an exact left
  null vector e_i). A kinetic device passes (the rows are refuted by many decades: each is pinned by its own
  capture and emission, or by the off-interface mask); a trap row with its rate terms removed raises
  IllPosedDCError naming the equation and the node count; a floating kinetic body raises with the trap rows in
  the conserved sum. Cost: a kinetic device now triggers one cached assembly per equation-set fingerprint.
* **Validation**: `validation/trap_kinetics.py` (nightly full tier; run_all picks it up), printing every
  gate's numbers next to its tolerance.
* **Tests**: `tests/test_trap_noise.py` (13 tests, on the solvers-devsim leg; child driver
  `tests/_trap_noise_child.py` for the builder); `tests/test_trap_kinetics.py` + the well-posedness gate, the DC
  form, the integrated charge (K1-5) and Stacked3DSpec (K2-6 3-D); the composition test's strict xfail FLIPS
  (`test_noise_fdt_with_a_charged_trap_level_high_frequency` is now a passing gate on the kinetic level, with
  the quasi-static miss asserted); `validation/devsim_noise._seed` seeds kinetic levels (no-op otherwise).

## 3. Findings (MEASURED)

1. **Contact nodes**: a kinetic level at an ohmic contact node is decoupled in DC / ssac; its events must not be
   counted (2.3e-3 FDT miss otherwise). The partner-less single-carrier convention of 4.0.2 counts them -- an
   O(h/L) inconsistency there too, flagged for the owner (not changed: gate (b) of 4.0.2 pins it).
2. **The fast-trap limit has a finite-speed correction growing as (N_t omega)^2**: a neutral kinetic level's g-r
   noise exceeds the fast-trap Lorentzian at 100 f_c by +0.223 / 2.2e-3 / < 1e-5 for N_t = 1e15 / 1e14 / 1e13 --
   the trap row's adjoint lags by omega tau_t (tau_t = N_t tau_n0/(n0 + n1 + p0 + p1)) while |y_p - y_n| rolls
   off. The plan's "O(N_t/n0)" budget misses it; K3-3 uses N_t = 1e13.
3. **The plan's fixed 1e-12 adjoint tolerance is not derivable on a junction with traps**: kappa_1 of the
   equilibrated system is 2e11; the gate uses kappa_1 eps.
4. **The physical trap row is not a robust DC Newton row** in a gated 2-D builder (section 1, the DC form).
5. **Trapping noise contact shorting** is a Debye-length effect (1.5 L_D' per end), not 2 sqrt(D tau) as the plan
   assumed for K3-4: the trapped-charge fluctuation is screened, not diffused.

## 4. K2-6 in 3-D

The builder's tensor (Kuhn) mesh reduces a laterally uniform stack to the 1-D discretization on its z-lines, so
the 3-D ssac equals a 1-D twin built on those lines with the SAME region setup to round-off (7.7e-14, table).
The gate is repointed circuit-driven by hand after `solve()` (there is no 3-D `set_ssac_gate`; the bipolar 3-D
gate carries the phi_bi frame, so the source is set to the solved gate potential). The kinetic interface bin is
installed by the builder's own `interface_traps` path and seeded by its own seed hook.

## 5. Defaults off

Nothing changes without kinetic traps: the DC-form context touches no parameter on a device without the
`KineticDCForm` parameter; the well-posedness LOCAL candidates exist only for node-only equations without flux
(none in the shipped quasi-static models); the noise changes act only on single-carrier sources with a partner
(none without kinetic traps) and on the refusal of rows no shipped source produced. The call-log digest and the
cross-version SG gate were run (report). K1-K2 kinetic results move at the ulp level (the rate derivatives are
now DEVSIM's diff through closed-form occupancy derivatives); every K1-K2 gate passes unchanged.

## Release-note lines

* carriers: trap generation-recombination noise of kinetic traps (`TrapLevel(dynamic=True)`,
  `InterfaceTraps(dynamic=True)`): the capture and emission events are noise sources (mechanisms `trap_n` /
  `trap_p`, on by default), so S_I = 4kT Re Y holds at every frequency for charged levels and kinetic D_it
  (the former D1c xfail now passes); also through `LayeredDevsimBuilder.noise_spectrum`.
* carriers: DC solves of devices with kinetic traps (`dc_solve.solve_dc`, every builder) use the quasi-static
  model with the occupancy slaved to it -- the same DC answer, a robust Newton (a gated 2-D device with kinetic
  interface bins did not converge before).
* carriers: the DC well-posedness check also verifies every node-only row (kinetic trap rows): a trap row
  without its capture / emission is reported as ill-posed.
