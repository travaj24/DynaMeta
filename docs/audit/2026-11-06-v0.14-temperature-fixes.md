## 3.2-7: 3-D bipolar DOS at T_K and the reported temperature

**What was wrong (confirmed).** `devsim_3d._setup_bipolar_semi` set `N_dos = effective_dos_m3(m)` (T_REF) and
the gate-frame block (`phi_bi`) did the same, while `BipolarOptions.T_K` set the region V_t. The 2-D
`LayeredDevsimBuilder._setup_bipolar_region` already evaluated the masses at T_K (checked: it was right), so
its rule became the shared helper `band_dos_at_T(m_n, m_p, options)` (bipolar_options; T_K None -> the
T_REF-default `effective_dos_m3` call, bit-identical). Both 3-D call sites and the 2-D one use it. The 3-D
`CarrierField.temperature_K` is now `temperature_of_V_t(region_V_t(device, field region))` (exactly T_REF for
V_t == V_T).

**Gate** `test_bipolar_body_dos_potential_and_reported_T_at_T[{3d,2d}-{250,350}]`: degenerate n-GaAs body
(eta = 10), flat-band gate.
- `N_dos`, `N_dos_p` = N(300)(T/300)^1.5 with N(300) = 2(2 pi m k 300/h^2)^1.5 re-derived in the test (exact-SI
  h). Budget 1.8e-9 = 3x the library's rounded-HBAR literal (`1.054571817e-34` vs h/2pi).
- Node Potential = V_t[eta + ln(N_c(T)/n_i)], eta by scipy quadrature of F_1/2 + brentq. Budget = 1 % of the
  exact FD offset eta - ln u: the code's Delta(u) is the rational g fit, documented +0.78 % at eta = 10 in the
  exact frame (physics_bipolar_dd "ACCURACY OF Delta"); MEASURED +0.780 % here (the solved potential equals
  the code's own V_t[ln(N_D/n_i) + Delta(u)] to 1e-10 V).
- `temperature_K == T`.

MEASURED on 189c648 (3-D): N_dos = 1.3145x / 0.7936x the closed form at 250 / 350 K; reported 300 K; body
potential 29.6 / 45.8 mV off the oracle against budgets of 1.47 / 2.06 mV (20x / 22x). Fixed: 1.15 / 1.60 mV
(the fit's 0.78 %), N_dos to 1.84e-9 (the HBAR literal), T exact. The 2-D cases pass on both trees (the 2-D
builder was already right) except for the 3-D == 2-D consistency line, which fails on 189c648.
Adversarial: the helper with T_K ignored misses the 250 K closed form by 31 % (`test_band_dos_at_T_is_the_closed_form`).

**Moved results (MEASURED, 3-D, the gate body, both trees):** see the table in "Moved results" below.

## 3.2-8: interface-trap bins at the region kT

`InterfaceTraps.sheet_levels(kT_eV=None)`: the default bin width (bin_eV None) is kT_eV, else V_T (devsim-free
callers at 300 K, unchanged); an explicit `bin_eV` wins; a non-positive kT is refused.
`install_interface_traps` passes the region's `V_t`. 300 K results are bit-identical (V_t == V_T).

**Gate** `test_interface_trap_bins_follow_the_region_kT_at_77K`: a 1-D p-Si/SiO2 stack at T_K = 77 K, uniform
split D_it = 1e16 m^-2 eV^-1 over the 1.12 eV gap. Bins <= kT(77 K); trapped charge sum N_k f_k vs scipy quad
of the N&B integral at 7 Fermi levels. Tolerance derived (in the test): Poisson summation of the midpoint
rule on a Fermi step whose derivative is continuous across the neutral-level edge (donor N(1-f) and acceptor
-N f share the derivative -D f') and flat at the band ends leaves |err| <= 2 pi D kT sum_m 1/sinh(2 pi^2 m
kT/h) -- 1.94e6 m^-2 at the installed h = 0.993 kT (6e-10 of the band charge) -- plus quad's error estimate.

MEASURED: 189c648 bins 25.45 meV = 3.84 kT(77 K); charge miss up to 4.75e12 m^-2 = 2.1e6x the tolerance,
and inside the same bound evaluated at THOSE bins (4.89e12) -- which checks the bound itself. Fixed: worst miss
1.89e6 m^-2 against 1.94e6 (the m = 1 alias term is nearly sharp; deterministic arithmetic, no solve).
Adversarial: 2 kT bins break the 1-kT bound.

Note (not changed): the P3 plan's alternative gate (the LF C-V slope gate D1c (c-i') re-run at 77 K) was not
built; the brief asked for the quadrature gate, which isolates the bin error from the mesh error.

## 3.2-9: BTBT under T_K warns

`tunneling.setup_btbt` reads the region `V_t` (set from T_K before BTBT is installed) and, if it is not V_T,
emits an AdvisoryWarning: the A, B, P are used as given (T_REF); B ~ E_g^(3/2) is not rescaled (v0.15). The
module header says to supply A, B fitted at T for a T_K device (the warning still fires: the code cannot tell
which temperature a set belongs to). Gate `test_btbt_under_T_K_warns_that_its_parameters_are_at_T_REF`: warns
at 250 K; silent at T_K None and T_K = 300.0 (V_t == V_T exactly). 189c648: DID NOT WARN.

## 3.2-10: electron affinity at T (user decision 3, modified)

User: "Refusing outright seems constraining, it may be fine to have a way to toggle this." Implemented:
- `TransportModel.chi_reference_T_K: Optional[float] = None` -- the temperature chi_eV (and band_gap_eV) were
  quoted at; None = unstated, read as T_REF. An assert-only tag: nothing is rescaled. Appended as the LAST
  field (positional construction unchanged). Same name as the plan's v0.15 `BandTemperature.chi_reference_T_K`.
- ONE kernel `physics_heterojunction.check_affinity_temperature(chi_T_K, T_K, mode=, owner=)` and the named
  error `AffinityTemperatureError(NotImplementedError)` (the builder's other "physics not modelled" refusals,
  the non-isothermal stack, are NotImplementedError too).
- Toggle `LayeredDevsimBuilder(on_chi_temperature="refuse" | "warn" | "off")` (spelled like
  `resonance.on_untrusted`), also on `Stacked3DSpec.from_design` (a 3-D heterostack runs at T_REF -- bipolar,
  the only physics with T_K, refuses extra semiconductors -- so there it fires only for a stated
  chi_reference_T_K != 300 K).
  - "refuse" (default): raises, naming the materials, the toggle and the field.
  - "warn": builds; AdvisoryWarning with the size of the assumption: Delta E_c(T) moves by gamma_chi x the
    gap-slope mismatch -- Al0.3Ga0.7As/GaAs 2.15 meV per 100 K (Vurgaftman 2001 Varshni, eq. 2.13, Tables
    I/II/XII; re-derived in `test_algaas_gaas_assumption_size_quoted_in_the_warning`), i.e. ~1.1 (gamma = 1/2)
    to 2.2 (gamma = 1) meV over 250-350 K, scaled to the actual |T - T_chi|; larger for Si/SiGe.
  - "off": silent; documented as for affinities validated at the device T.
- Order of checks (plan 5.4): chi_eV None refused first (anderson_alignment, any T), then the temperature check.
- What counts as a heterostack: semiconductor materials whose (chi_eV, band_gap_eV) are not all equal. Doping
  lives in TransportModel, so a p/n homojunction is two material NAMES of one semiconductor; it has no offset
  and is never checked (the same-material p/n case is in the gate).
- Existing T != 300 heterostack gates (T-a / T-b / T-c in `tests/test_devsim_heterojunction.py`) build through
  `validation/devsim_heterojunction.transport(T_K=...)`, which now adds the one-line tag `chi_reference_T_K =
  T_K` -- truthful: those oracles write the band diagram with the same T-independent chi. No gate weakened.

Gates: `test_check_affinity_temperature_modes` (devsim-free: consistent / unstated / stated-wrong x the three
modes, and the round trip of T through V_t), `test_heterostack_at_T_refuses_an_untagged_affinity_by_default`
(250 K N-AlGaAs / p-GaAs, refuses; MEASURED on 189c648: built silently, frames ntop 0.8701 V / pgaas 0.6270 V),
`test_heterostack_at_T_toggle_settings_and_the_tag` (@slow, ~36 s: warn / off / tagged / same-semiconductor
build; a tag stated at the WRONG T still refuses).

## Finding outside scope (reported, not changed)

`physics_bipolar_dd` says the FD built-in correction Delta(u) is "under 1 % everywhere degeneracy matters".
MEASURED against scipy F_1/2 quadrature (exact frame): +3.27 % at eta = 2, +2.68 % at 3, +1.72 % at 5, +1.16 %
at 7, +0.78 % at 10, +0.60 % at 15, +0.55 % at 20 (the last three match its anchors). So the "< 1 %" holds
from eta ~ 8 up, not in the moderately degenerate range eta 2-7, where the offset error is 1-3 % of Delta
(~1 mV at eta = 5, 250 K). The 3.2-7 gate therefore runs at eta = 10, inside the documented range.

## Release-note lines

- 3-D bipolar devices with `BipolarOptions(T_K=...)` now use the band densities of states at T_K (they used 300 K
  values, off by 1.31x at 250 K) and report the simulated temperature; results change only at T_K != 300 K.
- Interface-trap energy bins are now at most kT wide at the device temperature (they were 300 K wide, 3.9 kT at
  77 K); band-to-band tunneling under T_K now warns that its parameters are 300 K values.
- A heterostack simulated away from the temperature its electron affinities were quoted at now refuses by
  default: tag the materials with `TransportModel.chi_reference_T_K`, or choose
  `on_chi_temperature="warn"` / `"off"`.
