# DynaMeta v0.15.0 -- release note (2026-11-06)

Branch `wip/v015_closeout`, on top of v0.14.0 (`feat/devsim-2.11-v0.14`, release note
`2026-11-06-v0.14.0-release.md`). v0.15.0 ships after v0.14.0, and everything below is a change
**relative to v0.14.0**. It is the second DEVSIM release: it delivers the items v0.14 deferred
(plan `docs/plans/2026-11-04-devsim-v0.15/00_consolidated_plan.md`, status table at its top).

What is in it:

- shared infrastructure the new physics is built on: a general "two-point" noise source, a hook for
  Python-assembled equation terms, and term bookkeeping that the noise analysis reads (section 1);
- numerics: a density-scaled default Newton tolerance and detection of ill-posed (floating-body) DC
  problems (section 2);
- materials: Si parameter bundles with a "house" band-gap narrowing and a pairing check, a carrier-
  statistics switch, a band-temperature model, III-V recombination presets, extended precision chosen
  on the design, per-material Hooge coefficients (section 3);
- tunnelling: self-consistent gate-oxide tunnelling, more band-to-band tunnelling sets with
  temperature scaling and a mesh-resolution check, nonlocal band-to-band tunnelling in 1-D (section 4);
- traps and noise: thermionic-interface shot noise (on by default), kinetic traps and their noise, a
  one-call noise method on the 2-D builder (section 5);
- quantum: the old unipolar density-gradient wall retired, and strong inversion through a closure
  (section 6);
- `statistics="fermi_dirac"` made complete and made the DEFAULT, with the size of what the old default
  statistics got wrong and of what the change moves (section 7).

**Read sections 8 and 9 before upgrading.** Five defaults change. Every number below comes from the
per-topic audit note named next to it (list in section 15; all under `docs/audit/`, names
`2026-11-06-v0.15-<topic>.md`). "Measured" there means run on the dev box; the notes label what is
inferred. The four user decisions (a)-(d) are recorded where they apply.

## Terms used here

- **FDT** (fluctuation-dissipation theorem): at equilibrium the current noise is S_I = 4kT Re Y. It is
  the main oracle for every noise source in this release.
- **ssac**: DEVSIM's small-signal AC solve, giving the admittance Y(f) around a DC state.
- **Two-point (event) source**: the shot noise of a process that moves one carrier from one equation
  row to another (over a barrier, into a trap, through an oxide, along a tunnelling path):
  S = q^2 2P |Sum_r c_r y_r|^2, with P the event rate, y_r the adjoint (impedance-field) value of each
  affected row and c_r its sign.
- **Custom term**: a residual term assembled in Python and handed to DEVSIM through its custom-equation
  callback, for terms DEVSIM's model language cannot express (a node's rate depending on a distant node).
- **Detailed balance**: every process carries zero net rate at equilibrium. A violation is a current
  at zero bias.
- **gamma (degeneracy factor)**: gamma = (n/N_c) exp(-L), where L = ln u + Delta(u) is the Fermi-Dirac
  log-activity of u = n/N_c; gamma = 1 for Boltzmann statistics.
- **Kinetic trap**: a trap whose occupancy is a solution variable with its own capture/emission rate
  equation, instead of an instantaneous function of n and p ("quasi-static", the default).
- **BTBT**: band-to-band (Zener) tunnelling. **Local** = the rate at a point set by the field there;
  **nonlocal** = the rate set by the band profile along a tunnelling path.
- **SP**: Schrodinger-Poisson. **N_inv**: inversion-layer sheet density. **L_q**: the density-gradient
  quantum length.
- **J0p**: the hole saturation current density of a long diode, q n_i^2 D_p/(N_D L_p).
- **ulp**: one unit in the last place of a floating-point number.

## 0. Before you upgrade: what most users will notice

0. **The default carrier statistics are now `"fermi_dirac"`** (bipolar and unipolar drift-diffusion, every
   entry point). Results on DEGENERATE layers move (the old default carried a current at zero bias there).
   Elsewhere the moves are of the order of the degeneracy n/(sqrt(8) N_c): minority densities by 1.3 % at
   1e24 m^-3 (1e18 cm^-3) in Si, about 0.13 % at 1e23 m^-3; potentials by ~1e-6 of their range (section 7, table
   "What the default change moved"). `statistics="legacy"` (`TransportModel.carrier_statistics="legacy"`) reproduces v0.14
   byte for byte; it is not recommended.
1. **Noise of devices with a thermionic heterointerface changes** (now includes the interface shot
   noise; up to 3.27x at 1 Hz on the old xfail fixture). DC and ssac do not move (section 8).
2. **`solve_dc` refuses an exactly singular DC problem** (`IllPosedDCError`: a floating body, a
   potential with no Dirichlet condition) and its default absolute tolerance scales with the densities
   (section 8, section 9).
3. **`physics_density_gradient.setup_dg_hard_wall` is deprecated** and now installs the zero-flux
   insulator wall: a device built with it gives a different (correct) answer (section 8).
4. **1-D `btbt_generation_current` checks the mesh** and warns when its quadrature error exceeds 1 %
   (section 4.2).
5. Everything else is opt-in and leaves existing results unchanged (each workstream proved this three
   ways: a same-tree A/B, the recorded call-log digests, and the numeric cross-version gate). New
   advisories (section 9) can fire on existing scripts, e.g. ssac / noise / transient with quasi-static
   charged traps.

## 1. Shared infrastructure

(`two-point-source-custom-terms.md`, `term-bookkeeping-v2.md`)

- **Two-point noise source** (`noise_analysis.TwoPointSource`, `SourceRow`, `two_point_psd`). The
  existing GR noise (SRH, recombination terms, BTBT) now goes through it, **bit-identical** (36 of 36
  arrays `float.hex`-equal across versions). New mechanism keys `te_n`, `te_p`, `trap_n`, `trap_p`,
  `tunnel_ox`, `btbt_nl` are reserved and on by default (section 8).
- **Custom terms** (`dynameta.carriers.custom_terms`, new): one process-wide dispatcher, registered
  lazily by the first `add_custom_term`, never at import; a term is live only while DEVSIM still lists
  its device and the device carries the term's marker; local terms are refused (use
  `add_recombination_term`); explicit structural entries keep the sparsity pattern fixed. Registration
  alone moves nothing (gated in a child process). **With extended precision a custom term makes the
  Newton converge linearly** (measured 12 iterations for a weak sink, 34 for a strong link, against 4
  without): allow `maximum_iterations >= 60`. Where the term dominates its rows it can stall (section 12).
- **Term bookkeeping v2** (`physics_bipolar_dd.add_recombination_term(carrier="electron"|"hole",
  pair=, partner=, mechanism=, wrt=)`, `add_charge_term(wrt=)`). A term may change one carrier only (a
  trap's capture branch) and depend on extra node variables; the composite Jacobian follows them through
  DEVSIM's chain rule (measured: it works, but a missing `<model>:<var>` evaluates to 0 silently, so every
  declared variable is checked). `recombination_terms()` is the one inventory the noise analysis reads,
  including live custom terms (kind `"custom"`). The default `carrier="both"` is unchanged character for
  character.

## 2. Numerics

(`tolerance-wellposedness.md`)

- **Density-scaled default absolute tolerance.** `solve_dc(abs_tol=None)` (the new default) uses
  `dc_solve.default_abs_tol() = max(1e10, 1024 max_d u_d S_d)` over **every live device** d, where S_d is
  DEVSIM's aggregate solution size and u_d the unit round-off of that device (2^-53, or 2^-113 if
  extended). DEVSIM's Newton requires the absolute AND the relative criterion, so raising the absolute
  one to just above the round-off floor cannot loosen the delivered accuracy. Below S = 8.8e22 m^-3, and
  on every extended-precision device, the same 1e10 reaches DEVSIM (byte-identical). Measured: an ITO gate
  (4e26 m^-3) step that ran out of 100 Newton iterations now takes 4; every node within rel_tol |x| +
  abs_tol of a 128-bit reference (max relative error 9.4e-16), including a 1e8 m^-3 minority next to an
  8.2e25 majority. The layered and 3-D builders use the same rule (their own rules were retired;
  results bit-identical, or within 8.3e-16 on the 3-D builder).
- **Why "every live device" (a joint Newton).** A DEVSIM DC solve iterates every live device, not only
  the one named. A device left live by earlier work affects convergence (the first version sized only the
  solved device: a leftover 9 V MOS made a builder solve fail 1 run in 3 under `pytest -n 3`) and is
  itself moved at round-off (< 1e-12 relative). Compare two separate solves bitwise only with no
  unrelated device live; the test suite has `@pytest.mark.devsim_isolated` for that.
- **Floating-body detection** (`dynameta.carriers.well_posedness`, new). Before every DC Newton,
  `solve_dc` checks that the problem is not exactly singular: a static pass over DEVSIM's own equations
  proposes candidates (a carrier block or potential with no contact), and one matrix assembly confirms
  them; it raises only when both agree. Kinetic-trap rows are checked too. Cost: 0.054-0.061 % of a
  builder solve; no false positives over 699 DEVSIM tests (2,345 checked solves) and 39 validations
  (3,179 checked solves).
  `allow_floating(device, region, carriers=, reason=)` acknowledges an intended floating body.
- **Near-singular diagnosis.** A `solve_dc` Newton that stalls now says why, e.g. "near-singular DC
  Jacobian (sigma_min/sigma_max = 4.4e-16): mode in Electrons, x in [0, 8.4] nm", names the
  device/region/equation that held the joint Newton back, and for a MOS inversion stall names
  `setup_quasi_equilibrium_carrier` (section 6).

## 3. Materials

(`si-bundles.md`, `band-temperature.md`, `extended-precision-design-iii-v.md`,
`hooge-mapping-noise-method.md`)

- **Si bundles** (`materials.semiconductor_presets.silicon_transport(...)`, `silicon_bipolar_options`):
  the "house" pairing is Yan & Cuevas 2013 real BGN, dEg = 0.042 meV [ln(N/1e14 cm^-3)]^3 (Fermi-Dirac
  statistics), with n_i = 9.65e9, N_c = 2.86e19, N_v = 3.1e19 cm^-3, statistical gap 1.12973 eV, chi 4.05
  eV quoted at 300 K. The Boltzmann (apparent) set 12.96 meV ln(N/1e17) ships too. **n-type only**: p-type
  BGN nodes raise `BGNFitPendingError` until the 2014 p-type constants are read. Mobilities are required
  arguments.
- **Pairing check** (`bipolar_options.check_bgn_pairing`, one check site for the region setup and the 3-D
  gate frame). A paired BGN set refuses a region whose n_i differs by more than 1e-3, whose N_c / N_v,
  statistics or temperature (|T - 300| > 0.5 K) differ: `BGNPairingError`, quoting the n_ie^2 error and
  its gap equivalent ("+7.4 %, a gap error of +1.84 meV" for 1.0e10 against 9.65e9). Opt-out:
  `BipolarOptions(allow_unpaired_bgn=True)` (one `AdvisoryWarning` per region). Legacy presets are
  unchanged and unchecked.
- **Carrier-statistics switch** (`TransportModel.carrier_statistics`, `setup_bipolar_region(statistics=)`,
  `Stacked3DSpec.carrier_statistics`): `None` = `"fermi_dirac"` (the default since v0.15.0, section 7),
  `"boltzmann"` (the right choice for an apparent BGN set; removes the documented double count), `"legacy"`
  (the v0.14 default, byte-identical; not recommended). Unipolar drift-diffusion accepts `"fermi_dirac"` and
  `"legacy"` and refuses `"boltzmann"` (it is FD by construction).
- **Band-temperature model** (`TransportModel.band_temperature = materials.BandTemperature(gap_law,
  affinity_fraction, anchor="reference", derive_n_i=False)`): Varshni E_g(T) anchored at the material's
  quoted temperature (`chi_reference_T_K`, default 300 K), so the input gap is reproduced bit for bit
  there; chi(T) moves by a REQUIRED fraction of the gap change (1 = valence-anchored, Vurgaftman 2001;
  1/2 = the Sentaurus convention, secondary source); optional derived n_i(T). A heterostack whose
  materials carry it no longer needs `chi_reference_T_K` tags or `on_chi_temperature`. Measured on
  N-Al0.3Ga0.7As / p-GaAs at 200-400 K: V_bi within 5e-5 V of an independent band diagram, Delta E_c to
  3.3e-10 eV, TE conductance to 9.7e-5; the v0.14 route (gaps at T, chi tagged and unchanged) misses V_bi
  by 1.3-2.9 mV and the conductance by 2.6-5.0 %. Validation: `validation/devsim_heterojunction.py
  --temperature`.
- **One gap law for the package** (`materials.varshni_eg_ev`, `VARSHNI_PARAMS`, `ALLOY_GAP_PARAMS`, now
  with AlAs and `algaas_gap_law(x)`), used by the band-temperature model, BTBT temperature scaling,
  nonlocal BTBT and the SOA module. The `optics.soa` names are unchanged and its results bit-identical
  (178 values, same SHA-256).
- **III-V recombination presets** (`materials.semiconductor_presets`): GaAs B_int = 3.3e-10 cm^3/s
  (Niemeyer 2019), GaAs Auger C_n = C_p = 3.5e-30 cm^6/s (Strauss 1993's ambipolar 7 +/- 4e-30, split
  equally; secondary source), InP B_int = 1.2e-10 cm^3/s (Semyonov 2010, the n -> 0 intercept of a 2-8e18
  cm^-3 fit). All radiative presets are INTERNAL: apply the layer's photon recycling once with
  `preset.with_photon_recycling(P_r)` (B_eff = B_int (1 - P_r); measured 0.792e-10 against Niemeyer's
  0.78 +/- 0.07e-10 for a 2 um GaAs double heterostructure, 4.2x below B_int). `RadiativeModel.kind`
  ("internal" | "effective") labels any coefficient.
- **Extended precision from the design** (`Design(..., carriers=CarrierSpec(extended_precision=True))`),
  also through `run_pipeline`. An explicit builder argument wins, with one `AdvisoryWarning` when it
  contradicts what the design states. Default off is byte-identical (7 of 7 cross-commit fingerprints). A
  GaN-like 2-D diode matches the Sah-Noyce-Shockley oracle to +8.4e-4 (1.0 V) and +6.9e-4 (1.5 V); in
  double it is 12 decades low at 1.0 V and 3e4x high at 1.5 V.
- **Per-material Hooge alpha**: `noise_spectrum(hooge_alpha={region: alpha | (alpha_n, alpha_p)})`;
  regions not listed get no 1/f source. `TransportModel.hooge_alpha_n` / `hooge_alpha_p` store a
  material's values (no defaults). Use ~1e-4 (Hooge 1994, perfect lattice) or a measured value, not the
  old 2e-3 ("poor samples" average). A plain float behaves exactly as before (bitwise, three fixtures).

## 4. Tunnelling

### 4.1 Self-consistent gate-oxide tunnelling

(`oxide-tunnelling.md`)

`OxideTunnelingParams(model="tsu_esaki", coupling="self_consistent", barriers=SIO2_ON_SI,
gate_fermi_energy_eV=...)`, also through `LayeredDevsimBuilder(oxide_tunneling=)` and
`Stacked3DSpec(oxide_tunneling=)`. A two-sided Tsu-Esaki/WKB current through a dielectric stack, fed back
into the device as a custom term with an exact Jacobian:

- zero at equilibrium (|J|/J_one-way <= 1.4e-14; the v0.14 post-processor gives 25-66x (Schuegraf-Hu)
  or 1.1-2.6x (Tsu-Esaki) the one-way current there);
- supply-limited in deep depletion: within +1.3..+2.3 % of the depletion + SRH-generation estimate,
  where the post-processor on an ideal capacitor is 3e5-8e8x too high;
- conserves terminal current (0 ulp in extended precision) and enters ssac (1.1e-11 of the DC dI/dV at
  1 Hz) and noise (shot noise S = 2qJ coth(qV/2kT), mechanism `tunnel_ox`; FDT to 1.6e-9).

DC terminal currents (`extract_contact_currents`, `extras["contact_currents_A"]`) now include the gate's
tunnelling current; DEVSIM itself reports 0 for a dielectric gate in DC. The default `coupling="post"`
is the v0.14 post-processor, unchanged (Tsu-Esaki moved <= 5e-15 relative; WKB exponent <= 4 ulp).
Fowler-Nordheim and Schuegraf-Hu stay post-processors only (refused for "self_consistent").

### 4.2 Local BTBT: more sets, tuples, temperature, resolution check

(`btbt-presets.md`, `followups.md` item 1)

- New sets: `SILICON_KAO_DIRECT` / `_INDIRECT`, `GERMANIUM_KAO` (direct + indirect; Ge needs both), from
  Kao et al. 2012 re-typed from a reprint (`verification="secondary"`), and `IN053GA047AS_KANE`, computed
  by the new `tunneling.kane_direct_params` from Vurgaftman 2001 inputs. `SILICON_HURKX` keeps its bits.
- `btbt=` takes a tuple of sets; the terms sum, each with its own noise source;
  `btbt_generation_current(index=)` reports one.
- Opt-in temperature scaling: `dataclasses.replace(set, scale_B_with_gap=True)` plus a material
  `band_temperature` model gives B (E_g(T)/E_g(T_set))^1.5 with A fixed (e.g. InGaAs B x0.9641 at 350 K);
  or tag a set fitted at T with `T_K=T`. Without either, the v0.14 warning stays.
- **Mesh resolution check (default on, 1-D).** `btbt_generation_current(..., check_resolution=True)`
  estimates the quadrature error of the local current on the solved field
  (`tunneling.btbt_quadrature_error`) and warns (`UnreliableResultWarning`, naming the region and the
  worst edge) above 1 %. The returned value is unchanged. Why: the rate falls off from the field peak
  over 1/k = (V_bi + V_r)/B (0.14 nm for Si Kao direct), so one long edge on the peak carries an error
  ~k h_e/2 that refining elsewhere does not remove: a fixed 0.128 nm junction edge pinned a Si Zener at
  +11 % (estimate +12.1 %). Keep h <= 0.35/k across the peak. 2-D / 3-D are not checked (returns None).

### 4.3 Nonlocal BTBT, 1-D

(`nonlocal-btbt-1d.md`)

`btbt_nonlocal.setup_btbt_nonlocal(device, region, NonlocalBTBT(params, material=... | Eg_tun_eV=...))`:
the Sentaurus dynamic-path model, direct (P = 2) and phonon-assisted (P = 2.5, Eqs. 490-491; the
transcription was verified through its exact uniform-field limit) from the same `BTBTParams` as the local
model. Holes are generated at the path start, electrons at its end; the rate is exactly zero where no
final state is within reach; the Jacobian is exact and the noise source two-point (`btbt_nl`). It reduces
to A F^P exp(-B/F) in a uniform field to 6.5e-14 and converges at second order to an independent continuum
oracle. On an abrupt Si Zener (5e24 / 5e24 m^-3, Hurkx A, B) it gives 1134x less current than the local
model at 0.3 V reverse, 6.1x at 1.2 V, and agrees within 1.5x from 2.5 V. `setup_btbt` and
`setup_btbt_nonlocal` refuse each other's region (`BTBTDoubleCountError`). The gap comes from the
package's gap law (`material=`, temperature-aware) or an explicit `Eg_tun_eV` (the Gamma gap of an
indirect material's direct process). 1-D and, since the user's go-ahead (decision (c), 2026-11-07),
2-D -- below; 3-D is refused (`2026-11-07-v0.15-nonlocal-btbt-2d.md`).

**Nonlocal BTBT in 2-D** (`setup_btbt_nonlocal` on a 2-D bipolar region; numba required).

- Paths run along the local field from every start node and turn with the solution (dynamic direction),
  or along a fixed direction (`NonlocalBTBT(direction=...)`). They are traced exactly through the
  triangles; holes appear at the path start, electrons at its end (spread over the end triangle).
- The Jacobian is exact, including the path rotation, so ssac and noise (two-point, `btbt_nl`) are
  supported -- the refusal plan decision 25 expected was not needed.
- Laterally uniform devices give the 1-D result to round-off (6e-13 per path); rotated junctions on
  meshes not aligned to the field converge at second order (1.93-2.00). Zero current at equilibrium
  5e-15, FDT 6e-9, ssac vs DC dI/dV 1.8e-8 (0 V).
- No path reflection at interfaces (decision 24); 3-D is refused.
- Measured on a Si corner junction (5e24 / 5e24 m^-3): the LOCAL Hurkx model's planar error (12.8x at
  0.9 V, 1.44x at 2.5 V) is unchanged in 2-D; on the flanks it over-predicts by a further 8-20 %, at the
  corner 8-16 % less; on a 2 nm mesh the local current is up to 36 % off with no warning while the
  nonlocal one moves < 1 %; 19 % of the electrons from paths starting near the corner land outside that
  zone. Cost: about 2x the local model's solve time on the 12.8k-node corner device.

## 5. Traps and noise

(`thermionic-shot-noise.md`, `kinetic-traps.md`, `trap-noise.md`, `hooge-mapping-noise-method.md`)

- **Thermionic-interface shot noise, on by default** (mechanisms `te_n`, `te_p`): each direction of the
  Richardson current is an independent Poisson stream, S = 2q (J_01 + J_10) A per node pair. It closes the
  FDT (the v0.14 strict xfail missed by -69 %) at every A* from 1e-6 to 1e6 and under bias equals
  S = 2qJ coth(qV/2kT) to 2.4e-6. The area is the one DEVSIM's flux assembly uses (measured per node).
  New `NoiseSpectrum.by_interface`: S = Sum by_region + Sum by_interface = Sum by_mechanism.
- **Kinetic traps** (`TrapLevel(dynamic=True)`, `InterfaceTraps(dynamic=True, kinetic_bin_eV=)`): the
  occupancy is a solution variable (the odds w = f/(1-f): `TrapW_*`, `ItW*`) with its own SRH
  capture/emission equation. DC equals the quasi-static model (to 1.1e-16 V, densities 1.4e-15); transient
  and ssac become charge-conserving (Kirchhoff 8.7e-14, against 9.6e-3 / 9.8e-3 quasi-static); the
  Nicollian-Goetzberger conductance method (G_p/omega of D_it, the peak C_s/2 at omega tau = 1, which the
  quasi-static model misses entirely) and DLTS-like emission transients match their closed forms. DC
  solves of kinetic devices run inside a "DC form" (quasi-static rates, occupancy slaved): the same root,
  and a robust Newton (a gated 2-D device with kinetic interface bins did not converge otherwise).
  Interface bins are region-wide variables (DEVSIM has no interface unknowns), so their count is budgeted
  (`KINETIC_UNKNOWN_BUDGET` = 2e5 extra unknowns; coarsen with `kinetic_bin_eV`). The default
  `dynamic=False` is the shipped model, unchanged.
- **Trap generation-recombination noise** of kinetic levels (mechanisms `trap_n`, `trap_p`, on by
  default; they act only on kinetic levels). The charged-trap FDT, a strict xfail in v0.14 (5.6e-3 /
  6.6e-2 off at 100 kHz / 10 MHz), now holds (9.6e-7 / 7.8e-9) on a kinetic level; MOS conductance FDT to
  4.2e-13; a dilute acceptor level reproduces the ambipolar GR Lorentzian to 1.5e-5.
- **A kinetic level must carry charge:** `TrapLevel(kind="neutral", dynamic=True)` and
  `InterfaceTraps(charge="none", dynamic=True)` raise `NeutralKineticTrapError`. Such a level stored its
  occupancy without its Poisson charge (Kirchhoff off by 3.5e-3 at 1 MHz). Choose acceptor- or donor-like;
  for N_t much below the doping the choice moves the DC state by O(q N_t) only. Quasi-static neutral levels
  are unchanged.
- **One-call gate noise** (`LayeredDevsimBuilder.noise_spectrum`, `terminal_noise`): drives the dielectric
  gate's circuit once, re-settles through the library solver (so extended precision, the default
  tolerance and the well-posedness check apply), returns results **per cell** (x the lateral period;
  `per_cell=False` for per depth), inherits the noise module's default mechanisms at call time, and
  restores the builder's operating point. Bitwise equal to the hand recipe; FDT per cell to 7.8e-12.
  `hooge="material"` maps each region to its material's Hooge fields. `set_ssac_gate` now refuses a source
  or node name the process-global circuit already holds BEFORE it removes the gate's bias contact (before,
  DEVSIM failed after the removal and left a broken device).

## 6. Quantum

(`dg-wall-strong-inversion.md`)

- **Unipolar hard wall retired.** `setup_dg_hard_wall` issues a `DeprecationWarning` (removal in v0.16)
  and installs `setup_dg_insulator_wall`; `lambda_pin_factor` is ignored. The old rows pinned the carrier
  row at the wall, which made it a carrier sink and converged to a wrong wide-depletion profile; the
  insulator wall reproduces the closed-form dark space (1.187207 n0 L_q, measured +1.7e-5) and conserves
  carriers (wall current 3e-15 of scale). `seed_dg_from_solution(..., walls=(contact,))` seeds next to
  the wall (`walls=()` writes the old values bit for bit).
- **Strong inversion** (opt-in): `physics_bipolar_dd.setup_quasi_equilibrium_carrier(device, region,
  carrier, contact=...)` replaces a carrier's continuity row by "its quasi-Fermi level equals the body
  contact's". This is exact for a DC MOS capacitor and its low-frequency C-V. Close BOTH carriers for a
  MOS-C (the depleted holes are a second near-null mode). The classical and the density-gradient MOS-C
  now reach 1.3 V of band bending in <= 10 double-precision Newton iterations per 50 mV step (they failed
  at 0.78-0.80 V / 0.70 V), and equal the full model where both converge (<= 1.4e-13). ssac is allowed,
  noise is refused, a second ohmic contact on the carrier needs `allow_imref_split=True` (with an
  `imref_shift` for a channel; analytic oracle at order 2.00).
- **Against a new Schrodinger-Poisson oracle**, DG at `GAMMA_WALL` gives the centroid within 6-9 %, the
  threshold shift within 5.9 mV, the quasi-static C-V within 2.7 %, and closes far more of the classical
  error (classical 257-728 % off in N_inv); gamma = 1 closes 66-73 % of the gap. **N_inv is +10.6 to
  +11.7 % high** for N_inv >= 1e15 m^-2 against a registered 10 %: a documented model result (section 12).
  (These are the `"legacy"` statistics' numbers; at the v0.15.0 default `"fermi_dirac"`, MEASURED: N_inv
  +10.5 to +11.1 %, threshold shift within 5.6 mV, C-V within 2.6 %, classical 256-723 % -- section 7.)

## 7. `statistics="fermi_dirac"`: complete, now the default, and what the old default got wrong

(`si-bundles.md`, `fermi-dirac-mode-complete.md`)

The v0.14 default model (now `statistics="legacy"`) runs Fermi-Dirac TRANSPORT but writes every
recombination process and the ohmic contacts with the Boltzmann product n p = n_ie^2. Next to a degenerate
layer the device then carries a current at zero bias. `"fermi_dirac"` (the default since v0.15.0) removes it:

- **Every SRH channel** (bulk levels, interface traps, kinetic traps, the noise pair rate) uses the exact
  FD emission densities gamma_n n1, gamma_p p1, so a charged level has a Fermi-function equilibrium
  occupancy at any capture ratio; the contacts pin the FD charge-neutral root.
- **Local BTBT** (one set or a tuple, 1-D edge and 2-D/3-D element assembly) uses the FD Hurkx factor;
  **nonlocal BTBT** already read the FD activities.
- **Transport**: the degeneracy enters as a quasi-field in the Bernoulli argument, so the discrete
  zero-current state IS the thermodynamic equilibrium. The default transport's edge-averaged g leaves an
  O(h^2) "zero-bias battery" next to every degenerate gradient (measured: 0.4 mV, i.e. 1e9 A/m^2, across an
  n+ 1e26 / n 3e25 m^-3 homojunction at h = 1 nm). Unipolar drift-diffusion takes the same transport with
  `TransportModel(physics="drift_diffusion", carrier_statistics="fermi_dirac")`: the FDT residual of a
  degenerate ITO/AZO-like thermionic stack falls from 2.1e-6 to 9e-12.
- All with exact Jacobians; zero-bias exchange of every mechanism at round-off (4e-16 to 1.1e-14 of the
  one-way rate, at h and h/2).

4.4e-13 J0p.

**Decision (a), taken 2026-11-07: `"fermi_dirac"` is the default.** An unset statistics resolves to it for
bipolar and unipolar drift-diffusion in `setup_bipolar_region`, `setup_semiconductor_region_dd`, the 2-D and

gradient. The v0.14 default stays as `statistics="legacy"`: byte-identical (every recorded digest and the SG

zero-bias current next to degenerate layers, NOT recommended. `"boltzmann"` is unchanged.

Made to work at the default (each gated): the density gradient (unipolar: the FD quasi-field in the DG flux;
bipolar with `quantum_srh`: flat FD quasi-Fermi levels to 7.8e-16 V and detailed balance to 2.1e-14 in strong

## 11. Plan statements the implementation found wrong

Measured corrections to the v0.15 plan (details in the notes; the plan's status section lists them):

- Hooge gate S f = I^2 Sum alpha_r/N_r lacks the (L_r/L)^2 resistance-share weights (2.41x off).
- Thermionic gate S = 2q(J + 2J_s) with one J_s misses by -44..-57 % forward; the exact oracle is
  S = 2qJ coth(qV/2kT).
- Kinetic-trap unknown: the planned logit u = ln(f/(1-f)) overflows in Newton; the odds w = f/(1-f) is used.
  The K2-1 adversarial premise fails for a starved minority; trapping-noise contact shorting is a
  Debye-length effect; a fixed 1e-12 adjoint tolerance is not derivable (condition number 2e11). An
  interim "(N_t omega)^2 finite-speed excess" finding was the neutral kinetic level's missing charge.
- Oxide tunnelling: the adaptive Tsu-Esaki grid does not step J(V) by 1e-10 (measured <= 7e-15; the
  fixed grid is needed for an exact derivative); the gmsh spread is 5 % / 92 % on the 2 nm stack, not
  1.5 % / 37 %.
- Ge BTBT: the direct share DECREASES with field (it stays dominant).
- Nonlocal BTBT: "factor 2 at high field" holds only above ~2 V; prefactor integrals on the linear band
  converge at O(h^1.5) (integrated in the band variable instead: order 2).
- Tolerance: the absolute criterion IS binding in double on the MOS inversion fixture (at 0.78 V).
- Strong inversion: closing the electrons raises sigma_min by 1.9e8 (not 3e11); the holes are a second
  near-null mode; quasi-Fermi variables gave x1.7 (planned 0.65x).
- DG wall: the degenerate ITO deficit is 0.350 n0 L_q (FD g-factor), not in [0.7, 1.5]; the planned
  negative control is singular as written.
- GaN nightly gate: at the nominal 1 nm junction spacing the miss (+4.9e-3) is a DEVSIM grading remainder
  edge, not physics (gate at 0.5 nm).
- Band temperature: the planned fallback for a thin V_bi margin (an AlAs-rich alloy) would cross the
  Gamma-X crossover; the gate widens the temperature range instead.
- Custom terms: "about 12 iterations to 7e-30" in extended precision holds for a weak sink only (34 for a
  strong link; a dominating term stalls).

  discretisation.
- **FD rational fit.** The closed-form Delta(u) behind every FD model is off the exact F_1/2 by up to
  +3.9 % (eta ~ 0), above 1 % from eta ~ -3 to ~ 8 (docstring corrected): the house-bundle n0 p0 is 0.42 %
  low at 1e19 and 3.5 % low at 1e20 cm^-3 against Yan & Cuevas Eq. (1) (inside the paper's own uncertainty),
  and an exact-FD heterointerface oracle at 1e17 cm^-3 sees ~2.5e-4 eV of barrier (~0.9 % TE conductance).

**Numerics and DEVSIM behaviour.**

- **Nonlocal BTBT limits:** no 3-D; no path reflection, no finite-m0 or k_m cut-off, no BGN, no paths
  across interfaces (1-D and 2-D); extended precision with the term is not exercised; the Jiao 2013 data
  validation and a gate-edge / GIDL device are not built. The LOCAL model's resolution check
  (`btbt_quadrature_error`) is 1-D only, so its 2-D mesh sensitivity (up to 36 % on a 2 nm mesh, above)
  is not flagged.
- **Builder junction grading.** DEVSIM's line grading leaves a remainder edge next to a mesh line (0.77 nm
  at the junction of the 2-D GaN fixture): on a narrow SRH or BTBT peak it gives a first-order quadrature
  error (+4.9e-3 at 1 nm spacing there; +11 % for the 0.128 nm BTBT edge). Not changed: a builder fix would
  move every existing 2-D mesh. Use a finer spacing at the junction (0.5 nm there).
- **DEVSIM ssac floor.** ssac Re Y has an absolute floor of ~0.3-1 S/m^2 on heavily doped junctions
  (-0.56 / -0.33 S/m^2 against DC conductances of 6.6e-6 / 8.1e-6 S/m^2; 4.3e-4 relative on a 703 S/m^2 FDT
  diode), with or without any new term. Use the DC dI/dV for low-frequency conductance; the noise analysis
  agrees with 4kT G_dc there.
- **Extended precision and custom terms:** linear convergence; where the term dominates its rows (deep-
  depletion oxide tunnelling) the 128-bit Newton stalls at rel ~7e-16 for 120 iterations. The double
  deep-depletion Newton also converges linearly (~100x per iteration).
- **Joint Newton:** any DC solve iterates every live DEVSIM device and moves leftovers at round-off;
  `LayeredDevsimBuilder.teardown()` deletes every device and mesh in the process, not only its own.
- **Well-posedness:** circuit-level floating nodes (a current source left after its device was deleted;
  the frozen-carrier Poisson pre-solve of a current-driven contact) are not detected; a DC charge
  constraint for isolated wells is deferred.
- **Kinetic traps:** a 76k-node 2-D device allows <= 2 kinetic bins under the default budget; DEVSIM's
  `transient_bdf2` aborts on the 1-D bipolar DLTS device (use BDF1 / TR); McWhorter border traps are out of
  scope. A partner-less single-carrier term counts its events at ohmic contact nodes (an O(h/L)
  inconsistency; kinetic traps follow the stricter contact-node rule).
- **Thermionic noise:** on gmsh interfaces that meet along a line DEVSIM drops the thermionic flux at the
  shared nodes (a current-free seam; the noise is consistent with it).
- **Noise method:** dielectric gates only; per-cell scaling is exact only for a y-invariant device with
  white sources; no 3-D noise method (the 3-D builder has no circuit-driven gate); the restore of an
  extended-precision builder rounds to double.
- **Band temperature:** no temperature dependence of the DOS / Richardson masses, of the BGN fits, or of
  the X valley of AlAs-rich AlGaAs.
- **Si bundles:** n-type only; Klaassen mobility not available.
