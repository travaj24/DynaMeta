## 2. The occupancy variable: the odds w, not the logit u (decision 40, deviation MEASURED)

Decision 40 chose the logit u = ln(f/(1-f)). Measured (scratch `spike_var.py`): a toy trap row N df/dt =
A(1-f) - B f integrated one BDF1 step, three unknowns:

| step (one BDF1) | logit u ("default") | odds w ("positive") | f ("default") |
|---|---|---|---|
| emit, f0 = 1 - 1e-12 -> 0, dt = tau/100 | **FATAL** exp overflow | 16 its, exact | 2 its |
| emit, same, dt = 10 tau | **FATAL** | 10 its | 2 its |
| fill, f0 = 1e-12 -> 1, dt = tau/100 | **FATAL** (G overflow) | 4 its | 2 its |
| fill, f0 = 0.5 -> 1 - 1e-15, dt = 10 / 40 tau | 8 / 9 its | 10 / 12 its | 2 / 2 its |

The f-row is a sigmoid in u: Newton's linear step out of a saturated level is df/(f(1-f)) ~ 1e10, and exp(u)
overflows. DEVSIM's only damping, `log_damp`, is hard-wired to 0.0259 per step (MEASURED: a U = 40 target moves
0.19 per iteration with the region's V_t = 1). The same coupled DC solve of a 1-D diode with a kinetic level
from the neutral seed overflowed in u and converges in 13 iterations in w (the quasi-static twin: 13). The
odds keeps everything decision 40 wanted -- 0 < f < 1 under any update (w > 0, DEVSIM's "positive" update
caps a step toward 0 at 3 decades), no 1 - f cancellation (1 - f = 1/(1+w)), an exact seed (w_SS =
(tau_p0 n + tau_n0 p1')/(tau_p0 n1' + tau_n0 p)), the physical value exp((E_F - E_t)/kT) at equilibrium (gated,
K1-1 / K1-3) -- and its Newton converges. f itself (the rejected alternative) is the fastest but loses 1 - f
below 1e-16. **User-visible**: the variable is `TrapW_*` / `ItW*`; ln w is the plan's u.

## 4. Findings (MEASURED)

1. **The plan's K2-1 adversarial does not refute** (`test_conductance_minority_channel_is_starved`): with
   sigma_n = 1.5e5 sigma_p the electron channel's rate is 3e-2 of the hole channel's, and N&G's two-carrier
   time constant (both channels in detailed balance) predicts a 3 % miss at omega tau = 0.1 -- measured 1.4e-5.
   In depletion the minority electrons cannot follow the gate signal (bulk SRH off, no supply), so that channel
   adds no conduction path. The plan's premise ("must fail by c_n n_s/(c_p p_s)") is wrong for a starved
   minority; the K2-1 refutation used instead is an oracle perturbation (c_p + 1 %).
2. **The hole path to the interface dominates the low-frequency residual**: R_h = Int dx/(q mu_p p) over the
   SOLVED profile = 1.50e-8 Ohm m^2 (39x the neutral body's R_b; the analytic V_t/(E_s q mu_p p_s) estimate is
   3.6x low), and R_h C_s/tau predicts the omega -> 0 error 1.43e-5 against 1.42e-5 measured. Probed: it scales
   as 1/mu_p, not with t_si or the interface mesh.
3. **The conductance integrand converges at half the occupancy's rate in the bin width**: poles of omega
   tau/(1 + omega^2 tau^2) at Im E = (pi/2) kT give exp(-pi^2 kT/dE) = 5e-5 at kT (measured 1.2e-5 .. 4.0e-5),
   3e-9 at kT/2 (measured < 1e-9). The D1c estimate exp(-2 pi^2 kT/dE) (~1e-8 at kT) holds for the LF charge,
   not for G_p(omega). `InterfaceTraps.kinetic_bin_eV` documents it (kT/2 for 1e-8).
4. **DEVSIM 2.11.2 `transient_bdf2` aborts** ("FPE Invalid" in the UMFPACK factorization) on every step of the
   1-D bipolar DLTS device, quasi-static and kinetic alike; `transient_tr` works. The DLTS gate uses BDF1 for the
   ramp and the stiff damping steps, then TR (whose undamped stiff modes are why the damping steps exist: TR
   from the bare ramp stalled in Newton, measured).
5. **DEVSIM's 2-D mesher drops a mesh line ~0.1 nm from its neighbour** (the 0.05 nm-graded 1-D MOS could not
   be extruded: the t_si line vanished and the interface with it); 0.5 nm works.
6. DEVSIM adds node models `<var>_prev` after a solve; `kinetic_trap_variables` identifies a variable by its
   seed model (`TrapWSS_*` / `ItWSS*`), not by prefix alone.
7. **A single small gate step is not linear enough for the plan's K2-5 tolerance**: the symmetric part of
   the +-0.1 mV responses is 4.7e-3 of the antisymmetric one (first-order nonlinearity ~2.4 dV/(2 V_t)); a
   single 1 mV step would miss 2e-3 by ~20x. The gate takes (i(+dV) - i(-dV))/2, leaving (dV/V_t)^2 = 1.5e-5.
8. A circuit node at zero current cannot meet a relative test below ~1e-9 (its update is round-off over 0):
   the tests use 1e-8 there; the device rows converge to ~1e-15 regardless.

## Release-note lines

* carriers: kinetic trap levels -- `TrapLevel(dynamic=True)` and `InterfaceTraps(dynamic=True)` make the
  occupancy a solution variable with its own capture/emission rate equation (Shockley-Read-Hall kinetics); DC is
  unchanged (the quasi-static result to 1e-15), transient and small-signal analyses become charge-conserving,
  and the Nicollian-Goetzberger conductance method (G_p/omega of D_it) and DLTS-like emission transients are
  reproduced against their closed forms. Default `dynamic=False` is the shipped model, unchanged.
* carriers: an AdvisoryWarning when an ssac, noise or transient solve runs with QUASI-STATIC charged traps
  (not charge-conserving above the capture rate); `physics_traps.quasi_static_charged_traps()` lists them.
* carriers: kinetic interface bins are region-wide variables (DEVSIM has no interface unknowns): their cost is
  budgeted (`KINETIC_UNKNOWN_BUDGET` = 2e5 extra unknowns, `InterfaceTraps(kinetic_bin_eV=)` to coarsen).
